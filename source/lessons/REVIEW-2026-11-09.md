4. Test keyboard, screen-reader and narrow-screen use in actual browsers, including practical-task alternatives. Static checks alone do not establish accessibility.
5. After canonical corrections and later lesson changes, regenerate the site and rerun both curriculum and link checks. Instructor access, quiz player counts and individual-section downloads are separate workstreams and are not certified by this report.
