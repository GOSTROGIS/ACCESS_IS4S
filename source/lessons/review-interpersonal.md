# Interpersonal lesson review

Date: 2026-11-09

## Scope and depth

Reviewed 41 learner/answer sets (15 Collaboration, 14 Adaptability, 12 Creativity and Innovation) and scanned all 41 instructor files. All edits are lesson Markdown only. No core chapter, SVG, HTML or build-script edits; no commit or publication.

- **T: task-level textual review.** Read every practice prompt and matching answer/check-yourself section, with available worked-example context. Checked requested outputs, sample assumptions, counts, numerical reasoning, and concrete access barriers. Key-idea/background sections were consulted selectively where needed, not exhaustively reverified.
- **S: structural instructor scan.** Compared task-number sequences, summed every timed activity-table row against learner front matter, and searched instructor material for counts, activity format and access alternatives. Selected relevant instructor sections were read for corrections. This is not a claim of line-by-line substantive review of every instructor paragraph.
- SVG rendering/geometry and visual task inputs were not audited in this assignment. Extracted reading omitted SVG markup. External links, legal/medical/program facts and cited dates were not revalidated online; existing external claims were not silently treated as newly verified.

## Supported corrections applied

- Collaboration: stale total/series ending; three-person observer role; fact-versus-assumption prompt; explicitly invented lift-role-play assumptions; unsupported cutting diagnosis; factual-report example with invented dates, times, witness counts and completed actions; safety handover inventing that a fallen sign had been repaired; five-rule rubric; missing signed-checklist condition in claim release.
- Adaptability: five columns rather than five rows in the repair-planning prompt; offline official-profile research option.
- Creativity: all 12 Learning tasks headings now avoid presenting the whole-session total as an additional block; tables/totals were already correct. Corrected a rounded intermediate (54/94 × 100 = 57.4468…); clarified cuts are 3 mm too long; removed unsupported assertions that rework necessarily wipes out time savings; handled zero baselines; removed a six-workday-by-Friday commitment; qualified a half-day trial and a stocking-time omission; aligned poster and slide requirements.
- Added practical alternatives for Four Corners, WorkSafeBC lookup, apprenticeship-profile lookup, back-to-back brick directions, and personal-mark research/visual testing. These preserve the learning objective while avoiding device, speech/hearing, mobility or fine-motor requirements unrelated to the assessed skill.
