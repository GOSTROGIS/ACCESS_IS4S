    Part A

    Getting in: the Red Seal route to the BTech in Construction Management

    BCIT's Bachelor of Technology in Construction Management (BTech CM) is a degree completion program: it gives years 3 and 4 of a four-year degree. It has four entry options. Option D is the one built for tradespeople with a Red Seal, and it requires the Construction Operations Associate Certificate as bridging courses.

    Option D: Red Seal certification in an approved trade

    You must meet every item:

    • Red Seal: a Certificate of Qualification with an Inter-Provincial Red Seal Endorsement in a related, approved trade: cabinetmaker, carpenter, electrician, industrial mechanic (millwright), metal fabricator, plumber, refrigeration and air conditioning mechanic, or welder (level A).
    • Math, one of: Pre-Calculus 11 (60%), Foundations of Math 11 (60%), Workplace Math 11 (60%), BLDT 1011 Applied Construction Math (60%), or another acceptable BC or Yukon course.
    • Bridging courses: the Construction Operations Associate Certificate, with a minimum GPA of 67%.
    • Work experience: at least one year in the construction industry, in addition to apprenticeship hours.
    • English: English Studies 12 at 73% or equivalent (BCIT Category 1).

    The two-step admission

    1. Step 1, pre-entry assessment. The department reviews your entry option and your construction work experience. Make an appointment with the program head. When you are accepted, you get a pre-entry assessment form to include in your BCIT application.
    2. Step 2, apply. Only after Step 1 is approved, submit the online application with proof of every requirement.

    To reach the end: you need at least two years of related work experience before the final capstone project and graduation. That two years includes the work you used to get in.

    BCIT notes that students do better when they start with at least two years of industry experience, and that students must be proficient in Microsoft Word and Excel.

    The other three options, in one line each

    • Option A: a construction-related diploma or degree (GPA 67%) plus one year of construction work.
    • Option B: an unrelated diploma or degree (GPA 67%), the Construction Operations certificate (GPA 67%), plus one year of construction work.
    • Option C: an unrelated diploma or degree (GPA 67%) plus at least eight years of North American construction management experience.

    The Construction Operations Associate Certificate

    • 6 courses, 18 credits, part-time. BCIT runs it with the Vancouver Regional Construction Association (VRCA).
    • Entrance: English Studies 12 at 50% or equivalent (Category 3). Recommended: Grade 10 math. The program is designed for people with a trade background.
    • Pass mark: 50% in each course. The BTech CM route needs a 67% GPA across the certificate, so a pass alone is not enough.
    • Blended delivery. Courses run six or twelve weeks, usually one evening a week, 6:30 to 9:30 pm, at the Burnaby campus. Some are offered online.
    • Construction Operations alone does not get you into the degree. You still need every other Option D item.

    Part B

    Reading drawings

    BCIT's BLDT 1021 outline sets the goal plainly: interpret construction drawings, read a full drawing set, and connect the drawings to the specifications, the contract and the building code. Estimating, scheduling and site work all start here.

    Part C

    Estimating

    An estimate is quantities first, prices second. You measure from the drawings (the quantity take-off), add the allowances the job calls for, then extend each quantity by its unit price and its labour. A wrong quantity makes every price after it wrong, so the method and the checking matter more than speed.
