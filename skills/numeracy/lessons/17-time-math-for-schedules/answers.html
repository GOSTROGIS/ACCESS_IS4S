Time math for schedules

Tell working days from calendar days, add a job's working days to a start date while skipping weekends and BC statutory holidays, work out shift hours on a 24-hour clock, and turn crew-hours into working days.

Task 1 (level 1)

a) 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75. 7.75 h
b) 3 + 12 = 15. 15:15
c) The shift crosses midnight, so add 24 h to the end time: 06:10 + 24:00 = 30:10. Then 30:10 − 22:40 = 7 h 30 min (7.5 h).
d) 0.4 × 60 = 24 min

Task 2 (level 1-2)

a) 16:15 − 05:45 = 10 h 30 min. Take off the 30-minute lunch: 10 paid hours a day.
b) 10 − 8 = 2 hours a day at time-and-a-half. None of the day is over 12 hours, so there is no double time.
c) Regular hours: 8 × 5 = 40 h. 40 × $42.00 = $1,680.00. Overtime hours: 2 × 5 = 10 h. Overtime rate: $42.00 × 1.5 = $63.00. 10 × $63.00 = $630.00. Weekly overtime counts only the first 8 hours of each day, which is 40 hours, so there is no extra weekly overtime. Total: $1,680.00 + $630.00 = $2,310.00

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Working days: Jun 24 (1), Jun 25 (2), Jun 28 (3), Jun 29 (4), Jun 30 (5), skip Canada Day on Thursday July 1, Jul 2 (6), skip the weekend, Jul 5 (7), Jul 6 (8). The rough-in finishes on Tuesday, July 6, 2027.
b) June 24 to June 30 is 7 days, plus July 1 to 6 is 6 days. 7 + 6 = 13 calendar days.
c) The next working day after July 6: Wednesday, July 7, 2027.

Task 4 (level 2)

August 2 to August 13 has 2 weeks of Monday to Friday: 10 weekdays. Monday, August 2 is B.C. Day, a statutory holiday. 10 − 1 = 9 working days.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) 256 ÷ (4 × 8) = 256 ÷ 32 = 8 working days
b) 256 ÷ (5 × 8) = 256 ÷ 40 = 6.4. Round up: 7 working days
c) Hours each worker gives in 6 days of 10 h: 6 × 10 = 60 h. Workers needed: 256 ÷ 60 = 4.27. Round up: 5 workers. Check: 4 workers give 4 × 60 = 240 h, which is less than 256 h. 5 workers give 300 h, which is enough.

Task 6 (level 2-3)

a) September 23 + 7 days = Thursday, September 30, 2027.
b) September 30 is the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, a statutory holiday. The next working day is Friday, October 1, 2027.
c) Concrete keeps curing every day, including weekends and holidays. The cure time depends on the clock, not on when the crew works.

Task 7 (level 3)

a) 120 ÷ (2 × 10) = 120 ÷ 20 = 6 working days
b) Mon Oct 4 (1), Tue Oct 5 (2), Wed Oct 6 (3), Thu Oct 7 (4). Friday to Sunday are not working days. Monday October 11 is Thanksgiving Day. Tue Oct 12 (5), Wed Oct 13 (6). The job finishes on Wednesday, October 13, 2027.
c) Open task. A good text gives the finish date and the reason in plain words, for example: "Your job will be finished on Wednesday, October 13. Our shop works Monday to Thursday and is closed on Thanksgiving Monday, so 6 working days run into the following week."

Check yourself 1

A calendar day is any day. A working day is a day the crew works, which leaves out weekends and statutory holidays.
