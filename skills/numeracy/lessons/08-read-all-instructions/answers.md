## Task 3 (level 2)

The note says 3 frames, so every count is multiplied by 3.

a) Legs: 4 × 3 = 12 legs. 12 × 17 1/4 in = **207 in**
b) Long rails: 2 × 3 = 6. 6 × 36 5/8 in = 6 × 293/8 = 1,758/8 = **219 3/4 in**. Short rails: 6 × 14 3/8 in = 6 × 115/8 = 690/8 = **86 1/4 in**
c) 207 + 219 3/4 + 86 1/4 = **513 in**. 513 ÷ 12 = 42 remainder 9, so **42 ft 9 in**
d) For one frame: 4 × 17 1/4 + 2 × 36 5/8 + 2 × 14 3/8 = 69 + 73 1/4 + 28 3/4 = **171 in** (14 ft 3 in). The order would be 513 − 171 = **342 in short** (28 ft 6 in).
