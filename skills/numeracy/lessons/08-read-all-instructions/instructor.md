# Reading the whole task before you calculate

**Session:** Numeracy lesson 8 of 21. **Total length:** 45 minutes.

Before the session, print Task 1 on its own page, one copy for each learner. Learners should not see the rest of the learner page until after Task 1.

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Say: "People in this line of work need to remember and accurately follow procedures of many steps. Today's activities get your brain working with details." Ask: "What are some tasks where you must follow a procedure of many steps?" (painting, gluing, installing a door, starting a diesel generator, pulling an engine). Then ask: "How many ways can a procedure go wrong?" (as many as it has steps).

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- read a whole task and state the question and the form of the answer before calculating (Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy)
- pick out the numbers and conditions that matter, including a note at the end (Identify the mathematical information)
- work with fractions, mixed numbers and lengths in inches (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
- check whether an answer fits the whole task (Interpret and evaluate the information)

## Pre-assessment (3 min)

1. Has anyone had a job or a recipe go wrong because a step was missed? What happened, and what would have prevented it? (open)
2. In 5/8, which number is the denominator? (8)

## Learning tasks (27 min)

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Hand out the Task 1 page face down. Say only: "Follow the instructions on the page." Do not answer questions about it. | 1.1 Complete Task 1. | Task 1 printout | 5 min |
| 1.2 | Debrief without blame. Ask who read to the end first and what they noticed. Explain that this is not a test and there are no marks. Link it to job-site instructions that come at the bottom of a page. | 1.2 Discuss what happened and why. | Board | 5 min |
| 2.1 | Hand out the full learner page. Model the worked example on the board. Circulate as learners answer all 12 questions. Use fraction strips with learners who want them. | 2.1 Complete Task 2. Check answers with a partner. | Learner page, fraction strips | 10 min |
| 2.2 | Remind learners to read the whole cut list first. Ask: "What is the question, and what form will the answer take?" | 2.2 Complete Task 3. | Learner page, calculator with fraction key | 7 min |

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. A cut list shows 2 pieces at 24 3/8 in, with a note at the end: "make 4 of these assemblies". What total length is needed? (8 × 24 3/8 = 195 in)
2. Change 21/16 to a mixed number. (1 5/16)
3. What is one thing you will do before starting a multi-step task at work? (open: for example, read to the end, underline the numbers and notes, say the question out loud)

## Summary (3 min)

During this session we covered:

- why reading the whole task first prevents mistakes
- numerators, denominators, proper and improper fractions, mixed numbers and lowest terms
- multiplying fractions and changing improper fractions to mixed numbers
- totalling a cut list when a note changes the quantity
