Task 2 (level 1-2)

  1. The denominator in 5/8 is 8.
  2. The numerator in 3/16 is 3.
  3. Proper fraction: 3/8
  4. Improper fraction: 9/8
  5. Mixed number: 2 3/4
  6. Equal to 3/4: 6/8 (3 × 2 = 6 and 4 × 2 = 8)
  7. Lowest terms: 5/8 (4/8 = 1/2 and 6/16 = 3/8, so both can be reduced)
  8. 17 ÷ 8 = 2 remainder 1, so 2 1/8
  9. 24 ÷ 8 = 3, so 3
  10. 35 ÷ 16 = 2 remainder 3, so 2 3/16
  11. In a full day, 1/2 a set. In 1/4 of a day, 1/4 of that: 1/4 × 1/2 = 1/8 of a set
  12. Each quarter cut into thirds: 1/4 × 1/3 = 1/12 of a sheet. Check: 4 quarters × 3 pieces = 12 pieces.

Task 3 (level 2)

The note says 3 frames, so every count is multiplied by 3.

a) Legs: 4 × 3 = 12 legs. 12 × 17 1/4 in = 207 in b) Long rails: 2 × 3 = 6. 6 × 36 5/8 in = 6 × 293/8 = 1,758/8 = 219 3/4 in. Short rails: 6 × 14 3/8 in = 6 × 115/8 = 690/8 = 86 1/4 in c) 207 + 219 3/4 + 86 1/4 = 513 in. 513 ÷ 12 = 42 remainder 9, so 42 ft 9 in d) For one frame: 4 × 17 1/4 + 2 × 36 5/8 + 2 × 14 3/8 = 69 + 73 1/4 + 28 3/4 = 171 in (14 ft 3 in). The order would be 513 − 171 = 342 in short (28 ft 6 in).
