## Task 2 (level 2)

a) Profit: $500.00 − $400.00 = $100.00. Markup: $100.00 ÷ $400.00 × 100 = **25.00%**. Margin: $100.00 ÷ $500.00 × 100 = **20.00%**.
b) Profit: $2,000.00 − $1,500.00 = $500.00. Markup: $500.00 ÷ $1,500.00 × 100 = **33.33%**. Margin: $500.00 ÷ $2,000.00 × 100 = **25.00%**.
c) Profit: $900.00 − $750.00 = $150.00. Markup: $150.00 ÷ $750.00 × 100 = **20.00%**. Margin: $150.00 ÷ $900.00 × 100 = **16.67%**.

In each job the markup is bigger than the margin, because it is divided by the smaller number.
