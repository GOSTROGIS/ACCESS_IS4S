Markup, margin and pricing a job

Add up what a small job costs, set a price with a markup, and tell markup from margin so the job makes the money you planned.

Task 1 (level 1)

a) $200.00 × 1.25 = $250.00 b) $1,240.00 × 1.15 = $1,426.00 c) $86.40 × 1.50 = $129.60

Task 2 (level 2)

a) Profit: $500.00 − $400.00 = $100.00. Markup: $100.00 ÷ $400.00 × 100 = 25.00%. Margin: $100.00 ÷ $500.00 × 100 = 20.00%. b) Profit: $2,000.00 − $1,500.00 = $500.00. Markup: $500.00 ÷ $1,500.00 × 100 = 33.33%. Margin: $500.00 ÷ $2,000.00 × 100 = 25.00%. c) Profit: $900.00 − $750.00 = $150.00. Markup: $150.00 ÷ $750.00 × 100 = 20.00%. Margin: $150.00 ÷ $900.00 × 100 = 16.67%.

In each job the markup is bigger than the margin, because it is divided by the smaller number.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) $1,200.00 ÷ (1 − 0.20) = $1,200.00 ÷ 0.80 = $1,500.00. Check: profit $300.00, and $300.00 ÷ $1,500.00 = 20%. b) $1,200.00 × 1.20 = $1,440.00 c) $1,500.00 − $1,440.00 = $60.00 less.

b) Labour: 12 × $42.00 = $504.00. Direct cost: $334.70 + $504.00 = $838.70. c) Overhead: $838.70 × 0.12 = $100.644, which rounds to $100.64. Task 5 (level 3)

Markup: $517.50 ÷ $2,932.50 × 100 = 17.65% c) Margin: $517.50 ÷ $3,450.00 × 100 = 15.00% d) No. A 15% discount off the list price is a 15% margin when you sell at list, which is a 17.65% markup on what you paid.

Task 6 (level 3)

a) $2,100.00 × 1.30 = $2,730.00. Jordan used a 30% markup. b) Profit: $2,730.00 − $2,100.00 = $630.00. Margin: $630.00 ÷ $2,730.00 × 100 = 23.08%. c) $2,100.00 ÷ (1 − 0.30) = $2,100.00 ÷ 0.70 = $3,000.00. Check: $900.00 profit ÷ $3,000.00 = 30%.

Task 7 (level 3)

a) $2,500.00 × 0.80 = $2,000.00. That is the same as the cost, so the profit is $0.00. The crew would do the job for nothing. b) $2,000.00 × 1.10 = $2,200.00 c) Discount: $2,500.00 − $2,200.00 = $300.00. As a percent: $300.00 ÷ $2,500.00 × 100 = 12%.

Task 8 (level 3)

Open task. A good answer:

  • gives the price before GST: $1,080.24
  • gives the profit: $1,080.24 − $939.34 = $140.90
  • gives the margin: $140.90 ÷ $1,080.24 × 100 = 13.04%, and may note that the markup is 15%
  • may say the customer pays $1,134.25 with GST, and that the GST is not profit

Example: "The deck stairs come to $1,080.24 before GST. After material, labour and 12% overhead, the profit is $140.90, a 13.04% margin (15% markup). With GST the customer pays $1,134.25."
