# Payback and break-even

**Session:** Numeracy lesson 25 of 34. **Total length:** 90 minutes.

**Before the session:** learners need a calculator. Run this lesson after lesson 22 (markup and margin). Creativity and Innovation lesson 11 finds break-even in time saved per piece. This lesson does the same idea with money, so learners who have done lesson 11 can link the two.

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Ask: "Your crew rents the same tool every few weeks. When does it make sense to buy one?" Take answers and write them on the board. Most answers will be "when we use it a lot". Say: "Today you put a number on 'a lot'." Tell learners this is session 25 of the Numeracy set, and it runs 90 minutes.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- recognize a buy, rent or upgrade decision as a payback or break-even question (Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy)
- connect cost, saving, fixed cost, variable cost and price (Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information)
- calculate a payback period, rent-or-buy days, contribution and break-even units (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
- name the assumptions behind an answer and judge what happens if one is wrong (Interpret and evaluate the information)
- explain a break-even point to a business owner (Share the mathematical information, results and implications)

## Pre-assessment (3 min)

1. What is $2,400 ÷ $200? (12)
2. What is $26 − $14? ($12)

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. An upgrade costs $1,200 and saves $100 a month. What is the payback period? (12 months)
2. A tool rents for $60 a day and costs $1,800 to buy. How many days of rental is that? (30 days)
3. Fixed costs are $3,000. Each job brings in $250 and costs $150 in variable costs. How many jobs to break even? ($3,000 ÷ $100 = 30 jobs)

## Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

- payback period: cost ÷ saving per period
- net saving when a new tool also costs something to run
- rent or buy: the price ÷ the rental cost per day
- break-even: fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost)
- a running total for savings that change from year to year
- writing down the assumptions behind the answer
