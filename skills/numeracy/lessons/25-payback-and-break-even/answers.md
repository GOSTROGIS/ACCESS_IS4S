## Task 7 (level 3)

Open task. Any two well-explained assumptions, for example:

- **The crew uses the threader 3 days a month.** If it uses it less, payback takes longer. At 2 days a month it is 35 ÷ 2 = 17.5 months.
- **The rental rate stays at $120.00 a day.** If the rate goes up, buying pays back sooner. If it goes down, later.
- **The threader lasts longer than the payback period and needs little repair.** Repair costs or a short life make buying worth less.
- **Someone is free to pick up and return a rental.** If renting also costs travel time, buying pays back sooner.
