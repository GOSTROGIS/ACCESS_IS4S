Working out the monthly cost of a bank account

Read a fee schedule, count your transactions and work out what each account would cost you in a month and a year.

Everyday Chequing includes 30 debit transactions. Each extra one costs $1.25.

Task 2 (level 2)

22 + 6 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 34 debit transactions. The 2 pay deposits do not count, because note 1 says deposits are free and do not count. The withdrawal at another bank's ATM counts, because note 4 says so.

Task 3 (level 2)

ATM fees for the other bank's ATM: $2.00 (Riverbend) + $3.00 (owner) = $5.00 in every account.

Task 4 (level 2)

Everyday Chequing costs Jordan the least, at $20.95 a month.

  • Monthly saving compared with Basic: $29.00 − $20.95 = $8.05
  • Yearly saving: 12 × $8.05 = $96.60

A good answer points out that the account with the lowest monthly fee costs the most for this pattern of use.

Task 5 (level 2)

Basic Chequing with no monthly fee: $0.00 + $20.00 extra fees + $5.00 ATM fees = $25.00. Everyday Chequing is still $20.95, so Basic is still not cheaper for Jordan's month. It is $4.05 more.

Task 6 (level 2)

Alex's transactions: 10 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 15. That is under 18, so there are no extra fees in Basic Chequing.

  • Basic: $4.00. Everyday: $10.95. Unlimited: $16.95.
  • Cheapest: Basic Chequing, at 12 × $4.00 = $48.00 a year.

Task 7 (level 2)

The $10.95 monthly fee is waived. Extra fees still apply: 4 × $1.25 = $5.00. ATM fees: $5.00. Monthly cost: $0.00 + $5.00 + $5.00 = $10.00.

Task 8 (level 2)

a) The payment is $45.00 − $30.00 = $15.00 short. That is $10 or more, so an NSF fee can be charged, but no more than $10.00. Riverbend's fee is $10.00.

b) The payment is $62.00 − $57.00 = $5.00 short. That is under $10, so no NSF fee can be charged.

c) An NSF fee was already charged within the last 2 business days, so no second NSF fee can be charged for the second payment.

Task 9 (level 3)

Answers depend on what the tool shows on the day. A good answer:
