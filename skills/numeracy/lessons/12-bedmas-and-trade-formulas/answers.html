Order of operations in trade formulas

Use BEDMAS and the calculator's brackets to work out stud counts, stair totals, perimeters and areas without mixing up the order.

Task 1 (level 1)

a) Multiply first: 6 + 12 = 18 b) Divide first: 20 − 3 = 17 c) Both multiplications: 30 − 12 = 18 d) 6 + 14 = 20 e) 8 − 6 + 1. Left to right: 2 + 1 = 3 f) Left to right: 6 × 3 = 18

Task 2 (level 1-2)

a) 20 × 3 = 60 b) 2 × 750 = 1,500 mm c) Inner brackets: 2 × [15 − 10]. Outer brackets: 2 × 5 = 10 d) Exponent first: 100 − 25 = 75 e) Inside the brackets, multiply first: 1,350 − 150 = 1,200. Then 1,200 ÷ 8 = 150 mm

Task 3 (level 2)

a) 3,600 ÷ 400 + 1 = 9 + 1 = 10 studs b) 6,000 ÷ 600 + 1 = 10 + 1 = 11 studs c) 16 ft = 192 in. 192 ÷ 16 + 1 = 12 + 1 = 13 studs d) 4,100 ÷ 400 = 10.25. Round up to 11 spaces, so no space is wider than 400 mm. 11 + 1 = 12 studs

Task 4 (level 2)

a) 16 × 175 = 2,800 mm b) Brackets first: (16 − 1) × 255 = 15 × 255 = 3,825 mm c) Without brackets the calculator multiplies first: 16 − 255 = −239, which is not a length. Key ( 16 − 1 ) × 255 =.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) Multiply both, then add: 135,000 + 225,000 = 360,000 mm² b) 2 × 850 = 1,700 mm c) Brackets first, and inside them the exponents: 1,600 − 625 = 975. π × 975 = 3,063.05. 3,063.05 mm²
