# Reading between the lines of a chart

## Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) Fraction = (25 − 20) ÷ (30 − 20) = 0.5. Change = 180 − 140 = 40. Estimate = 140 + 0.5 × 40 = **160**
b) Fraction = (4 − 0) ÷ (10 − 0) = 0.4. Change = 3.5 − 2.0 = 1.5. Estimate = 2.0 + 0.4 × 1.5 = **2.6**
c) Fraction = (175 − 100) ÷ (200 − 100) = 0.75. Change = 10.8 − 12.4 = −1.6. Estimate = 12.4 + 0.75 × (−1.6) = 12.4 − 1.2 = **11.2**

## Task 2 (level 2)

a) Change over the whole run = 97.880 − 98.400 = −0.520 m.
- 13 m: fraction 13 ÷ 52 = 0.25. 98.400 + 0.25 × (−0.520) = **98.270**
- 26 m: fraction 0.5. 98.400 − 0.260 = **98.140**
- 39 m: fraction 0.75. 98.400 − 0.390 = **98.010**

b) Grade = 0.520 ÷ 52 × 100 = **1%**, falling from manhole 1 to manhole 2.
c) The pipe is laid at one steady grade, so its height really does change in a straight line between the manholes. The straight-line estimate is the true value.

## Task 3 (level 2)

a) 10 °C is halfway between 5 °C and 15 °C. Change = 24 − 48 = −24 h. 48 + 0.5 × (−24) = **36 h**
b) 20 °C is halfway between 15 °C and 25 °C. Change = 12 − 24 = −12 h. 24 + 0.5 × (−12) = **18 h**
c) Fraction = (12 − 5) ÷ (15 − 5) = 0.7. 48 + 0.7 × (−24) = 48 − 16.8 = 31.2 h. Round **up to 32 h**. A wait is rounded up so the sealant is not loaded before it has cured.

## Task 4 (level 2-3)

a) 10 psi is between 8 psi (50 ft) and 15 psi (100 ft). Fraction = (10 − 8) ÷ (15 − 8) = 2 ÷ 7 = 0.2857. Length = 50 + 0.2857 × 50 = 64.286 ft. 0.286 ft × 12 = 3.43 in, so 64 ft 3.43 in. It is a longest hose allowed, so round down: **64'-3"**.
b) 120 ft is between 100 ft (15 psi) and 150 ft (21 psi). Fraction = (120 − 100) ÷ (150 − 100) = 0.4. Change = 21 − 15 = 6 psi. 15 + 0.4 × 6 = 17.4 psi. Round **up to 18 psi**. Planning for a bit more loss than the estimate makes sure the sprayer still has enough pressure.

## Task 5 (level 2-3)

a) 18 m is between 16 m and 20 m. The note says to use the next larger radius, 20 m: **1,900 kg**.
b) Fraction = (18 − 16) ÷ (20 − 16) = 0.5. 2,400 + 0.5 × (1,900 − 2,400) = **2,150 kg**. You must not use it because the chart note says to use the load at the next larger radius. 2,150 kg is more than the 1,900 kg the note allows at 18 m, so using it could overload the crane.
c) The rated load must be at least 2,000 kg. 16 m gives 2,400 kg, which is enough. 20 m gives 1,900 kg, which is not enough. The largest radius in the chart is **16 m**. Any radius between 16 m and 20 m reads as 20 m under the note, so it is not allowed either.

## Task 6 (level 3)

a) Fraction = 6.0 ÷ 16.0 = 0.375. Change = 100.40 − 101.20 = −0.80. 101.20 + 0.375 × (−0.80) = 101.20 − 0.30 = **100.90**
b) 100.90 − 100.750 = +0.150. Positive means **cut, 150 mm** (estimated).
c) Open task. A good note:

- gives the estimated ground elevation at R, 100.90, and the cut, about 150 mm
- says it was interpolated between the spot elevations at P and Q, assuming an even slope
- says it is an estimate, and that a level shot at R (lesson 31) would confirm it before digging
