Metric prefixes from nano to giga

Read and convert the large and small metric prefixes found on nameplates, bills and data plans, and write their symbols correctly.

Task 1 (level 1)

Prefix Symbol Multiplies the unit by
giga G 1 000 000 000 (10⁹)
mega M 1 000 000 (10⁶)
kilo k 1 000 (10³)
milli m 0.001 (10⁻³)
micro µ 0.000 001 (10⁻⁶)
nano n 0.000 000 001 (10⁻⁹)

Task 2 (level 1)

a) 1 megawatt, 1 MW b) 1 milliampere, 1 mA c) 1 nanometre, 1 nm d) 1 kilopascal, 1 kPa

Task 3 (level 2)

a) MW to kW is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 kW b) mA to A is one step to a bigger unit, so divide by 1,000: 450 ÷ 1,000 = 0.45 A c) µF to nF is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 0.047 × 1,000 = 47 nF d) kΩ to Ω is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 3.3 × 1,000 = 3,300 Ω e) GHz to MHz is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 1.8 × 1,000 = 1,800 MHz f) nm to mm is two steps to a bigger unit (nano to micro to milli), so divide by 1,000,000: 650 ÷ 1,000,000 = 0.00065 mm

Task 4 (level 2)

From milli to the base unit is one step, then two more steps to mega (base to kilo to mega). Three steps of 1,000 is 1,000 × 1,000 × 1,000 = 10⁹. 5 MW is 1,000,000,000 times (10⁹) bigger than 5 mW.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) 1,500 W × 6 h = 9,000 Wh. 9,000 ÷ 1,000 = 9 kWh. Cost: 9 × $0.12 = $1.08 b) 9 W × 5 h × 30 days = 1,350 Wh. 1,350 ÷ 1,000 = 1.35 kWh. Cost: 1.35 × $0.12 = $0.162, which rounds to $0.16

Task 6 (level 2)

  • Plan: 5 GB = 5,000 MB. Used: 3 GB + 850 MB = 3,000 MB + 850 MB = 3,850 MB.
  • Left: 5,000 − 3,850 = 1,150 MB, which is 1,150 ÷ 1,000 = 1.15 GB

Task 7 (level 2)

  1. 250 MW is a correct symbol, but 250 megawatts is far too much power for a cordless drill. It was probably meant to be 250 W. Check the nameplate or the spec sheet.
  2. 5 KG should be 5 kg. Kilo is a lower-case k.
  3. 1.5 Km should be 1.5 km.
  4. 4.7 µF is written correctly.

Line 1 is the amount of the wrong size.
