# Metric prefixes from nano to giga

## Task 1 (level 1)

| Prefix | Symbol | Multiplies the unit by |
|---|---|---|
| giga | G | 1 000 000 000 (10⁹) |
| mega | M | 1 000 000 (10⁶) |
| kilo | k | 1 000 (10³) |
| milli | m | 0.001 (10⁻³) |
| micro | µ | 0.000 001 (10⁻⁶) |
| nano | n | 0.000 000 001 (10⁻⁹) |

## Task 2 (level 1)

a) 1 megawatt, **1 MW**
b) 1 milliampere, **1 mA**
c) 1 nanometre, **1 nm**
d) 1 kilopascal, **1 kPa**

## Task 3 (level 2)

a) MW to kW is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 2.5 × 1,000 = **2,500 kW**
b) mA to A is one step to a bigger unit, so divide by 1,000: 450 ÷ 1,000 = **0.45 A**
c) µF to nF is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 0.047 × 1,000 = **47 nF**
d) kΩ to Ω is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 3.3 × 1,000 = **3,300 Ω**
e) GHz to MHz is one step to a smaller unit, so multiply by 1,000: 1.8 × 1,000 = **1,800 MHz**
f) nm to mm is two steps to a bigger unit (nano to micro to milli), so divide by 1,000,000: 650 ÷ 1,000,000 = **0.00065 mm**

## Task 4 (level 2)

From milli to the base unit is one step, then two more steps to mega (base to kilo to mega). Three steps of 1,000 is 1,000 × 1,000 × 1,000 = 10⁹. 5 MW is **1,000,000,000 times** (10⁹) bigger than 5 mW.

## Task 5 (level 2)

a) 1,500 W × 6 h = 9,000 Wh. 9,000 ÷ 1,000 = **9 kWh**. Cost: 9 × $0.12 = **$1.08**
b) 9 W × 5 h × 30 days = 1,350 Wh. 1,350 ÷ 1,000 = **1.35 kWh**. Cost: 1.35 × $0.12 = $0.162, which rounds to **$0.16**
