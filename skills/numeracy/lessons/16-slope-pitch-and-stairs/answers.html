Roof slope, stair rise and stair run

Write a roof slope as a rise in 12 and as 1 in a number, work out the risers, unit rise and total run of a stair, and check them against the BC Building Code.

Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) 12 ÷ 6 = 2. 1 in 2 b) 12 ÷ 4 = 3. 3 in 12 c) 4,200 × 4 ÷ 12 = 1,400 mm d) 12 ÷ 5 = 2.4. 1 in 2.4

Task 2 (level 2)

a) 3 in 12 = 1 in 4. The minimum is 1 in 3, and 1 in 4 is less steep (the second number is bigger). No. b) 4 in 12 = 1 in 3, which is the minimum. Yes, it meets it exactly. c) 1 in 4 is steeper than 1 in 6. Yes.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Run = 7,200 ÷ 2 = 3,600 mm. Rise = 3,600 × 5 ÷ 12 = 1,500 mm. Rafter line = √(3,600² + 1,500²) = √15,210,000 = 3,900 mm. (12, 5 and 13 times 300 mm.) b) Run = 9,000 ÷ 2 = 4,500 mm. Rise = 4,500 × 8 ÷ 12 = 3,000 mm. Rafter line = √(4,500² + 3,000²) = √29,250,000 = 5,408.33 mm

Task 4 (level 2-3)

a) 2,720 ÷ 200 = 13.6. Round up: 14 risers. Unit rise: 2,720 ÷ 14 = 194.29 mm b) 194.29 mm is between 125 mm and 200 mm. 265 mm is between 255 mm and 355 mm. Both meet the private stair limits. c) Treads: 14 − 1 = 13. Total run: 13 × 265 = 3,445 mm d) 2,720 ÷ 13 = 209.23 mm. Not allowed: it is taller than the 200 mm maximum for a private stair.

Task 5 (level 2-3)

a) 3,150 mm is less than 3.7 m = 3,700 mm. Yes, one flight is allowed (Article 9.8.3.3). b) 3,150 ÷ 180 = 17.5. Round up: 18 risers. Unit rise: 3,150 ÷ 18 = 175 mm, between 125 mm and 180 mm. c) 300 mm is not less than the 280 mm minimum for a public stair. Treads: 18 − 1 = 17. Total run: 17 × 300 = 5,100 mm

Task 6 (level 2)

a) Total rise: 6 × 7.25 = 43.5 in. Total run: 5 × 10.5 = 52.5 in b) Unit rise: 7.25 × 25.4 = 184.15 mm. Unit run: 10.5 × 25.4 = 266.7 mm c) 184.15 mm is between 125 mm and 200 mm. 266.7 mm is between 255 mm and 355 mm. Yes, both are inside the private stair limits.

Task 7 (level 3)

a) 194 − 191 = 3, 194 − 189 = 5, 195 − 189 = 6, 195 − 192 = 3, 192 − 190 = 2 mm b) 195 − 189 = 6 mm c) No. The 6 mm difference between the third riser (189 mm) and the fourth (195 mm) is more than the 5 mm allowed between adjacent treads or landings. The flight passes the 10 mm check, because 6 mm is not more than 10 mm. d) Open task. A good report:

  • says the flight does not meet Sentence 9.8.4.4.(1)
  • names where: between the third and fourth risers from the bottom, 189 mm and 195 mm, a 6 mm difference against a 5 mm limit
  • says that the tallest and shortest risers are within the 10 mm limit
  • asks for direction or suggests a recheck before finishes go on
  • is short, polite and uses the numbers
