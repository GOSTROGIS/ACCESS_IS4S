# Checking a pay stub and filling in a TD1

## Task 1 (level 1)

- Pay period: October 5 to October 18, 2026
- Regular hours: 80. Overtime hours: 4
- Regular wage rate: $27.50 an hour
- Gross pay: $2,365.00. Net pay: $1,833.34

All of these are read straight from Document A.

## Task 2 (level 1)

Found on the stub: the employer's name and address, the hours worked, the wage rate, the overtime hours, each deduction with its purpose (CPP, EI, income tax, union dues), the gross pay and the net pay. Nothing on the BC list is missing for this pay. There is no allowance, vacation pay or time bank in this pay period, so those lines are not needed.

## Task 3 (level 2)

- Regular pay: 80 × $27.50 = $2,200.00
- Overtime rate: 1.5 × $27.50 = $41.25 an hour
- Overtime pay: 4 × $41.25 = $165.00
- Gross pay: $2,200.00 + $165.00 = **$2,365.00**, which matches the stub.

## Task 4 (level 2)

- Total deductions: $132.71 + $38.55 + $312.40 + $48.00 = **$531.66**
- Net pay: $2,365.00 − $531.66 = **$1,833.34**, which matches the stub.
- Check: $1,833.34 + $531.66 = $2,365.00

## Task 5 (level 2)

EI = 1.63% × $2,365.00 = 0.0163 × 2,365.00 = 38.5495, which rounds to **$38.55**. This matches the stub.

## Task 6 (level 2)

1. Exemption for one pay: $3,500 ÷ 26 = $134.6154 (keep the decimals)
2. Bracket: $2,365.00 − $134.6154 = $2,230.3846
3. CPP: 0.0595 × $2,230.3846 = 132.7079, which rounds to **$132.71**. This matches the stub.

A common error is to work left to right as (2,365.00 − 3,500) ÷ 26, which gives a negative number. BEDMAS does the division first.

## Task 7 (level 2)

## Task 8 (level 2)

- Federal line 1: $16,452
