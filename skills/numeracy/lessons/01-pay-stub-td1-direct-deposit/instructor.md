# Checking a pay stub and filling in a TD1

**Session:** Numeracy lesson 1 of 34. **Total length:** 90 minutes.

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Ask: "Who has started a new job and been handed a stack of forms on day one?" Name the three that come up most: the TD1, the TD1BC and a void cheque or direct deposit form. Tell a short story: an apprentice notices that four hours of overtime are missing from a stub, checks the math, and gets the money on the next pay. Ask learners to recall the location strategy for document use: decide what you need, scan the headings, find the row and column, then check.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- find the pay period, hours, rates, deductions, gross and net pay on a pay stub (Identify the mathematical information)
- connect the stub to the payroll rates and the TD1 lines to each other (Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information)
- recalculate gross pay, net pay, EI and CPP, and total a TD1 and TD1BC claim (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
- decide whether a stub is correct and which form and numbers an employer needs (Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy, Interpret and evaluate the information)

## Pre-assessment (5 min)

1. Gross pay is $800 and deductions are $150. What is net pay? ($650)
2. What is 1.5 × $24.00? ($36.00)

Learners who answer both quickly can move straight to Tasks 5 and 6 after Task 3.

## Post-assessment (10 min)

1. A stub shows 72 regular hours at $26.00 and no overtime. Deductions total $412.30. What is the net pay? ($1,872.00 − $412.30 = $1,459.70)
2. A new employee's only claims are the basic personal amount and $900 in tuition. What is federal line 13, and does the employee need a TD1BC? ($17,352, yes, because it is more than $16,452)
3. Which three numbers from a cheque go on a direct deposit form? (branch, institution, account)

Learners take turns giving answers and saying where on the documents they found each number. Make sure everyone participates.

## Summary (3 min)

During this session we covered:

- what a BC pay stub must show, and how to check gross pay, deductions and net pay
- how EI and CPP are worked out from the 2026 payroll rates
- how to total a TD1 and TD1BC, and what a claim code is
- which cheque numbers are needed for direct deposit, and which personal details are never needed
