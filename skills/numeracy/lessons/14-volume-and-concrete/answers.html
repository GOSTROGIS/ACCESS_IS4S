Volume, concrete and waste allowance

Find the volume of slabs, footings, walls and piers in cubic metres and cubic yards, add a waste allowance and work out how much to order.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information; Share the mathematical information, results and implications

Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) 2.4 × 1.2 × 0.6 = 1.728 m³ b) In metres: 1.2 × 0.6 × 0.5 = 0.36 m³. 0.36 × 1,000 = 360 L c) 0.8 × 0.5 × 0.45 = 0.18 m³. 0.18 × 1,000 = 180 L. 180 ÷ 20 = 9 pails. Check: 9 × 20 L = 180 L.

Task 2 (level 2)

a) 3 × 0.9144³ = 3 × 0.764 554 857 984 = 2.29 m³ b) 2 ÷ 0.9144³ = 2.62 yd³ c) 54 ÷ 27 = 2 yd³

Task 3 (level 2)

a) 85 × 1.10 = 93.5 m. 93.5 ÷ 6 = 15.58. Round up: 16 lengths b) 38 × 1.12 = 42.56. Round up: 43 sheets c) 28 × 1.15 = 32.2 m²

Task 4 (level 2-3)

a) 75 mm = 0.075 m. 9.6 × 7.2 × 0.075 = 5.184 m³ b) 100 mm = 0.1 m. 9.6 × 7.2 × 0.1 = 6.912 m³ c) 5.184 × 1.05 = 5.4432 m³. The next step of 0.5 m³ is 5.5 m³. d) Yes. 75 mm is not less than 75 mm, so it meets the minimum exactly. A good response notes there is no margin: a slab any thinner would not meet Sentence 9.16.4.3.(1).

Task 5 (level 2-3)

a) r = 300 ÷ 2 = 150 mm = 0.15 m. One pier: π × 0.15² × 1.2 = 0.0848 m³. Eight piers: 8 × π × 0.15² × 1.2 = 0.68 m³. In cubic yards: 0.6786 ÷ 0.9144³ = 0.89 yd³ b) 0.6786 × 1.10 = 0.7464, so 0.75 m³

Task 6 (level 3)

a) Footing: 0.35 × 0.1 × 40 = 1.40 m³. Wall: 0.2 × 2.4 × 40 = 19.20 m³ b) Total: 1.40 + 19.20 = 20.60 m³. 20.60 ÷ 0.9144³ = 26.94 yd³ c) 20.60 × 1.08 = 22.25 m³ (22.248). 22.248 ÷ 0.9144³ = 29.10 yd³ d) Open task. A good note:

  • gives the order with its unit, for example "Order 22.25 m³ (about 29.1 yd³) for the footing and the foundation wall."
  • says what it includes: 1.40 m³ of footing, 19.20 m³ of wall, and an 8% waste allowance
  • names one thing to check, for example that the footing and wall sizes on the drawings match the ones used, that the length of 40 m includes every wall, or the supplier's ordering unit
  • is short and uses plain words

Check yourself 1

1,000 L

Check yourself 2

All three lengths must be in the same unit. Mixing metres and millimetres gives an answer 1,000 times too big or too small.

Check yourself 3

1.10

Check yourself 4

Rounding first can leave the order short. Work out the full amount with waste, then round up once at the end.

Check yourself 5

The slab is too thin. Sentence 9.16.4.3.(1) says a concrete slab shall not be less than 75 mm thick, not counting any topping. Check the drawings and ask the supervisor.
