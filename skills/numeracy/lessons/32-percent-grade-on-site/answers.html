Percent grade between two points on a site

Work out the grade between two elevations as a percent, as 1 in a number and in degrees, find the fall a drain or path needs, check a ramp against the BC Building Code, and change lot areas between hectares and acres.

Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) 0.45 ÷ 18 × 100 = 2.5%. 18 ÷ 0.45 = 40, so 1 in 40 b) 0.12 ÷ 6 × 100 = 2%. 6 ÷ 0.12 = 50, so 1 in 50 c) 1.5 ÷ 12 × 100 = 12.5%. 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8, so 1 in 8 d) 0.36 ÷ 12 × 100 = 3%. 12 ÷ 0.36 = 33.33, so 1 in 33.33 e) 100 ÷ 50 = 2% f) 100 ÷ 5 = 20, so 1 in 20

Task 2 (level 2)

a) Rise = 48.920 − 48.380 = 0.540 m. Grade = 0.540 ÷ 27.0 × 100 = 2%. 27.0 ÷ 0.540 = 1 in 50. tan⁻¹(0.540 ÷ 27.0) = 1.15°. It falls from A to B. b) Rise = 103.150 − 102.400 = 0.750 m. Grade = 0.750 ÷ 15.0 × 100 = 5%. 15.0 ÷ 0.750 = 1 in 20. tan⁻¹(0.750 ÷ 15.0) = 2.86°. It rises toward the door, so it falls from the door toward the gate.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Fall = 23 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.46 m. Low end invert = 99.860 − 0.460 = 99.400 b) Fall = 36.5 × 1 ÷ 100 = 0.365 m = 365 mm c) Fall = 14 × 2.5 ÷ 100 = 0.35 m. Low end = 12.300 − 0.350 = 11.950

Task 4 (level 2-3)

a) 4.200 ÷ 0.450 = 9.33, so 1 in 9.33. The limit for an exterior ramp is 1 in 10. 1 in 9.33 is steeper (the second number is smaller), so it is not allowed. Shortest run: 0.450 × 10 = 4.500 m b) 5.400 ÷ 0.600 = 9, so 1 in 9. The limit for an interior ramp serving a residential occupancy is 1 in 10. Not allowed. Shortest run: 0.600 × 10 = 6.000 m c) 5.600 ÷ 0.750 = 7.47, so 1 in 7.47. An office is in "all other occupancies", limit 1 in 8. Not allowed. Shortest run: 0.750 × 8 = 6.000 m d) In an accessible path the limit is 1 in 12. Shortest run: 0.450 × 12 = 5.400 m

Task 5 (level 2)

a) tan⁻¹(10 ÷ 100) = tan⁻¹(0.1) = 5.71° b) tan⁻¹(100 ÷ 100) = tan⁻¹(1) = 45° c) tan(30°) × 100 = 57.74% d) A 45° bank rises 1 m for every 1 m across, which is a 100% grade, because percent grade is rise ÷ run × 100, not the angle.

Task 6 (level 3)

a) 0.30 ÷ 5.0 × 100 = 6%. 5.0 ÷ 0.30 = 16.67, so 1 in 16.67 b) Yes. 1 in 16.67 is steeper than 1 in 20 (6% is more than 5%). Clause 3.8.3.2.(3)(g) says it must "be designed as a ramp complying with this Section". c) Yes. 1 in 16.67 is gentler than 1 in 12 (6% is less than 8.33%). d) Open task. A good note:

  • gives the measurement: 0.30 m fall over 5.0 m, a 6% grade or 1 in 16.67
  • says this is steeper than 1 in 20, so under Clause 3.8.3.2.(3)(g) the walkway has to be designed as a ramp
  • says the slope itself is within the 1 in 12 limit of Clause 3.8.3.5.(1)(b)
  • asks whether the drawings show it as a ramp, or asks for direction
  • is short and uses the numbers

Task 7 (level 2)

a) 30 × 40 = 1,200 m². 1,200 ÷ 10,000 = 0.120 ha b) 5 × 4,046.856 = 20,234.28 m². 20,234.28 ÷ 10,000 = 2.023 ha c) 2.5 × 10,000 = 25,000 m². 25,000 ÷ 4,046.856 = 6.178 acres d) 20 × 36 = 720 m². 720 ÷ 10,000 = 0.072 ha. 720 ÷ 4,046.856 = 0.178 acres
