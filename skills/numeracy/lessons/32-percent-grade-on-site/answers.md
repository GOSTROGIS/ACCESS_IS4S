# Percent grade between two points on a site

## Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) 0.45 ÷ 18 × 100 = **2.5%**. 18 ÷ 0.45 = 40, so **1 in 40**
b) 0.12 ÷ 6 × 100 = **2%**. 6 ÷ 0.12 = 50, so **1 in 50**
c) 1.5 ÷ 12 × 100 = **12.5%**. 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8, so **1 in 8**
d) 0.36 ÷ 12 × 100 = **3%**. 12 ÷ 0.36 = 33.33, so **1 in 33.33**
e) 100 ÷ 50 = **2%**
f) 100 ÷ 5 = 20, so **1 in 20**

## Task 2 (level 2)

a) Rise = 48.920 − 48.380 = 0.540 m. Grade = 0.540 ÷ 27.0 × 100 = **2%**. 27.0 ÷ 0.540 = **1 in 50**. tan⁻¹(0.540 ÷ 27.0) = **1.15°**. It falls **from A to B**.
b) Rise = 103.150 − 102.400 = 0.750 m. Grade = 0.750 ÷ 15.0 × 100 = **5%**. 15.0 ÷ 0.750 = **1 in 20**. tan⁻¹(0.750 ÷ 15.0) = **2.86°**. It rises toward the door, so it **falls from the door toward the gate**.

## Task 4 (level 2-3)

a) 4.200 ÷ 0.450 = 9.33, so **1 in 9.33**. The limit for an exterior ramp is 1 in 10. 1 in 9.33 is steeper (the second number is smaller), so it is **not allowed**. Shortest run: 0.450 × 10 = **4.500 m**
b) 5.400 ÷ 0.600 = 9, so 1 in 9. The limit for an interior ramp serving a residential occupancy is 1 in 10. **Not allowed.** Shortest run: 0.600 × 10 = **6.000 m**
c) 5.600 ÷ 0.750 = 7.47, so 1 in 7.47. An office is in "all other occupancies", limit 1 in 8. **Not allowed.** Shortest run: 0.750 × 8 = **6.000 m**
d) In an accessible path the limit is 1 in 12. Shortest run: 0.450 × 12 = **5.400 m**
