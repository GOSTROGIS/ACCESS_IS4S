Credit card interest and minimum payments

Read a credit card information box, work out interest and minimum payments, and see what paying only the minimum costs over time.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 120 min
  • Components: Identify the mathematical information; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information; Share the mathematical information, results and implications

Task 1 (level 1)

  • Purchase rate: 20.99% a year
  • Cash advance rate: 22.99% a year
  • Minimum payment: 3% of the balance or $10, whichever is more, or the full balance if it is under $10
  • Grace period: 21 days on new purchases, if the full balance is paid by the due date

Task 2 (level 2)

October 31 + 21 days = November 21, 2026. If the full statement balance is paid by the due date, the interest on the boots is $0.00.

Task 3 (level 1)

$120 + $50 = $170 a year.

Task 4 (level 3)

Spending needed: $120 ÷ 0.015 = $8,000 a year. Each month: $8,000 ÷ 12 = $666.67, so about $667 a month. Check: 1.5% of $8,000 = $120. A good answer notes that this only breaks even if the balance is paid in full, because interest would cancel out the rewards.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) 3% × $1,200.00 = $36.00. That is more than $10, so $36.00. b) 3% × $250.00 = $7.50. That is less than $10, so $10.00. c) The balance is under $10, so pay the full $8.00.

Task 6 (level 3)

Month Balance at start Minimum payment Left after payment Interest New balance
1 $1,200.00 $36.00 $1,164.00 $20.36 $1,184.36
2 $1,184.36 $35.53 $1,148.83 $20.09 $1,168.92
3 $1,168.92 $35.07 $1,133.85 $19.83 $1,153.68

Steps for month 1: 3% × $1,200.00 = $36.00. $1,200.00 − $36.00 = $1,164.00. $1,164.00 × 0.2099 ÷ 12 = $20.36. $1,164.00 + $20.36 = $1,184.36.

Month 2: 3% × $1,184.36 = $35.53. Month 3: 3% × $1,168.92 = $35.07. The minimum payment gets smaller as the balance shrinks.

Task 7 (level 3)

  • Total paid: $36.00 + $35.53 + $35.07 = $106.60
  • Balance went down: $1,200.00 − $1,153.68 = $46.32
  • Paid in interest: $20.36 + $20.09 + $19.83 = $60.28

Check: $46.32 + $60.28 = $106.60. More than half of what was paid went to interest.

Task 8 (level 3)

a) 147 ÷ 12 = 12 remainder 3, so 12 years and 3 months. b) $1,285.76 − $132.57 = $1,153.19 more interest. c) $1,200.00 + $1,285.76 = $2,485.76, more than twice the price of the tool kit.

Task 9 (level 2)

  • $300.00 × 0.2299 = 68.97
  • 68.97 ÷ 365 = 0.188959
  • 0.188959 × 30 = 5.6688, which rounds to $5.67
  • Add the fee: $5.67 + $5.00 = $10.67

Task 10 (level 2)

a) $600.00 ÷ 4 = $150.00 b) 4 × $150.00 = $600.00 c) $600.00 + $10.00 = $610.00. If the missed payment was a pre-authorized debit that bounced at the bank, the bank could also charge an NSF fee. d) $600.00, because purchases paid in full by the due date have no interest.

Task 11 (level 3)

Open task. A good response:

  • states that paying only the minimum on $1,200 takes 147 months (12 years and 3 months) and costs $1,285.76 in interest in the classroom model
  • states that paying $100 a month takes 14 months and costs $132.57 in interest
  • names the source: the Riverbend Classic card information box and the classroom model, which is close to but not the same as how real cards calculate interest
  • uses plain language and does not tell the co-worker what to do
