Perimeter and area with openings and cut-outs

Find the distance around a building or room and the area of walls, floors and plates, take out doors, windows and cut-outs, and count the sheets to order.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the mathematical information; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information

Session: Numeracy lesson 13 of 16. Total length: 90 minutes.

Before the session: learners need a calculator and access to the Shape Canvas on a computer or tablet, one for each pair. Project the Shape Canvas and the Numeracy Hub Volume, Area, Layout and Measuring tab. This lesson follows the Intake 142 decks for Day 18 (perimeter and frame lengths), Day 19 (area, openings and coverage) and Day 24 (floor layout and sheeting).

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Point to a wall in the room. Ask: "If we were going to drywall this wall, what would you measure first?" Take answers. Then ask: "What about the door? Do we buy drywall for the door?" Say: "Today is about two numbers every takeoff starts with: the distance around, and the area inside, less what is cut out." Tell learners this is session 13, and it runs 90 minutes.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • pick out the lengths, the openings and the sheet size from a job description (Identify the mathematical information)
  • connect perimeter to plates and trim, and area to sheets (Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information)
  • find perimeter and area, break a shape down, subtract openings and cut-outs, and count sheets, by hand and with the Shape Canvas (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
  • check an answer two ways and round a sheet count up (Interpret and evaluate the information)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. Which unit is an area: m, m² or m³? (m²)
  2. Find the perimeter of a 2 m by 3 m rectangle. (10 m)

Learning tasks (70 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Teach perimeter as the outside edges. Link to plates, rim joists and baseboard. Spot the mistake: a dashed height inside a triangle is not part of the perimeter. 1.1 Complete Task 1. Learner page, Textbook p. 229 8 min
1.2 Teach area and square units. Show why 1 m² = 1,000,000 mm². Spot the mistake: changing m² to mm² by multiplying by 1,000. 1.2 Complete Task 2. Textbook p. 242 10 min
2.1 Teach "break it down" with the L-shaped plate, both ways. Spot the mistake: putting a diameter straight into π × r². 2.1 Complete Task 3. Textbook p. 251 12 min
2.2 Teach openings and sheet counts with the worked example. Show that rounding is up. 2.2 Work the example, then complete Task 4. Learner page, Textbook p. 255 14 min
3.1 Link perimeter to the plate order. Ask: "Why 26 and not 25?" 3.1 Complete Task 5. Learner page 8 min
3.2 Demonstrate the Shape Canvas: draw a wall, then a door inside it, and show that the door becomes a cut-out. Circulate as pairs complete Task 6. 3.2 Complete Task 6 in pairs on the Shape Canvas. Shape Canvas 15 min
3.3 Pairs report one place where their hand answer and the canvas differed, and why. 3.3 Share one check with the group. Shape Canvas 3 min

Post-assessment (5 min)

  1. A wall is 5 m by 2.4 m with a door 0.9 m by 2.1 m. Find the net area. (12 − 1.89 = 10.11 m²)
  2. How many 1.2 m by 2.4 m sheets cover the wall in question 1? (10.11 ÷ 2.88 = 3.51, so 4 sheets)
  3. Find the perimeter of a 9,760 mm by 7,320 mm floor frame. (2 × 17,080 = 34,160 mm)

Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

  • perimeter for plates, rim joists and trim
  • area and square units, and why 1 m² = 1,000,000 mm²
  • breaking a shape down and checking it two ways
  • subtracting doors, windows and cut-outs
  • rounding a sheet count up
  • checking hand calculations on the Shape Canvas

Further practice: Intake 142 lecture quizzes W05-L06, W06-L01, W06-L02 and W07-L02.
