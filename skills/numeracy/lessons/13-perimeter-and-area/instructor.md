# Perimeter and area with openings and cut-outs

**Session:** Numeracy lesson 13 of 16. **Total length:** 90 minutes.

**Before the session:** learners need a calculator and access to the [Shape Canvas](../../shape-canvas/index.html) on a computer or tablet, one for each pair. Project the Shape Canvas and the [Numeracy Hub Volume, Area, Layout and Measuring tab](../../hub/index.html#vlm). This lesson follows the Intake 142 decks for [Day 18](../../../../programs/carpentry/intake-142/PDF/Day-18_2026-11-27_Week-5.pdf) (perimeter and frame lengths), [Day 19](../../../../programs/carpentry/intake-142/PDF/Day-19_2026-11-30_Week-6.pdf) (area, openings and coverage) and [Day 24](../../../../programs/carpentry/intake-142/PDF/Day-24_2026-12-07_Week-7.pdf) (floor layout and sheeting).

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Point to a wall in the room. Ask: "If we were going to drywall this wall, what would you measure first?" Take answers. Then ask: "What about the door? Do we buy drywall for the door?" Say: "Today is about two numbers every takeoff starts with: the distance around, and the area inside, less what is cut out." Tell learners this is session 13, and it runs 90 minutes.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- pick out the lengths, the openings and the sheet size from a job description (Identify the mathematical information)
- connect perimeter to plates and trim, and area to sheets (Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information)
- find perimeter and area, break a shape down, subtract openings and cut-outs, and count sheets, by hand and with the Shape Canvas (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
- check an answer two ways and round a sheet count up (Interpret and evaluate the information)

## Pre-assessment (3 min)

1. Which unit is an area: m, m² or m³? (m²)
2. Find the perimeter of a 2 m by 3 m rectangle. (10 m)

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. A wall is 5 m by 2.4 m with a door 0.9 m by 2.1 m. Find the net area. (12 − 1.89 = 10.11 m²)
2. How many 1.2 m by 2.4 m sheets cover the wall in question 1? (10.11 ÷ 2.88 = 3.51, so 4 sheets)
3. Find the perimeter of a 9,760 mm by 7,320 mm floor frame. (2 × 17,080 = 34,160 mm)

## Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

- perimeter for plates, rim joists and trim
- area and square units, and why 1 m² = 1,000,000 mm²
- breaking a shape down and checking it two ways
- subtracting doors, windows and cut-outs
- rounding a sheet count up
- checking hand calculations on the Shape Canvas
