Perimeter and area with openings and cut-outs

Find the distance around a building or room and the area of walls, floors and plates, take out doors, windows and cut-outs, and count the sheets to order.

  • Components: Identify the mathematical information; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information

Task 1 (level 1)

a) P = 2 × (12,200 + 8,500) = 2 × 20,700 = 41,400 mm b) P = 2 × (4.2 + 3.6) = 2 × 7.8 = 15.6 m c) 15.6 − 0.9 = 14.7 m

Task 2 (level 1-2)

a) 600 × 450 = 270,000 mm². 270,000 ÷ 1,000,000 = 0.27 m². Check: 0.6 × 0.45 = 0.27 m². b) 4 × 8 = 32 ft². 48 in × 96 in = 4,608 in². Check: 32 × 144 = 4,608. c) 3.6 × 2.7 = 9.72 m²

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Two rectangles: 250 × 600 + 550 × 200 = 150,000 + 110,000 = 260,000 mm². Big rectangle less the corner: 800 × 600 − 550 × 400 = 480,000 − 220,000 = 260,000 mm². The answers agree. b) Plate: 400 × 300 = 120,000 mm². Hole: r = 160 ÷ 2 = 80 mm, and π × 80² = 20,106.19 mm². Net: 120,000 − 20,106.19 = 99,893.81 mm²

Task 4 (level 2)

a) Wall: 36 × 8 = 288 ft². Windows: 2 × 4 × 3 = 24 ft². Door: 6 ft 8 in = 80 in = 80/12 ft, so 3 × 80 ÷ 12 = 20 ft². Net: 288 − 24 − 20 = 244 ft² b) One sheet: 4 × 8 = 32 ft². 244 ÷ 32 = 7.625. Round up: 8 sheets

Task 5 (level 2)

a) Three plates all the way around: 3 × 41,400 = 124,200 mm (124.2 m) b) 124,200 ÷ 4,880 = 25.45. Round up: 26 lengths. 25 lengths would be about 2,200 mm short.

Task 6 (level 2)

a) Wall: 3 × 2.4 = 7.2 m². Door: 0.9 × 2.1 = 1.89 m². Window: 1.2 × 1.0 = 1.2 m². Net: 7.2 − 1.89 − 1.2 = 4.11 m² b) The area the canvas gives for the wall with its cut-outs should match 4.11 m², which is 4,110,000 mm² if the canvas reads in millimetres. A good response explains any difference, for example a cut-out drawn partly outside the wall, a size typed in the wrong unit, or a shape that was not inside the wall and so was not subtracted. c) 4.11 ÷ 2.88 = 1.43. Round up: 2 sheets

Check yourself 1

1,000,000, because 1 m² = 1,000 mm × 1,000 mm.

Check yourself 2

4 sheets. Sheets are sold whole, so round up.

Check yourself 3

Split a shape into simple shapes, find the area of each, then add them and subtract any openings or cut-outs.

Check yourself 4

1.2 × 2.4 = 2.88 m²

Check yourself 5

No. Perimeter is a length, the distance around, in m. Area is the surface inside, in m². They measure different things.
