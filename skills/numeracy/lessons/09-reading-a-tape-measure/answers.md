# Reading a tape measure to the sixteenth

## Task 1 (level 1)

Divide the top and the bottom by the same number until the numerator is odd or no number bigger than 1 goes into both.

a) 4/16: divide by 4. **1/4**
b) 8/16: divide by 8. **1/2**
c) 12/16: divide by 4. **3/4**
d) 6/16: divide by 2. **3/8**
e) 10/16: divide by 2. **5/8**
f) 14/16: divide by 2. **7/8**
g) 2/16: divide by 2. **1/8**
h) 24/32: divide by 8. **3/4**

## Task 2 (level 1)

Whole inch line, then the count over 16, then lowest terms.

a) 2 + 5/16. 5 is odd. **2 5/16 in**
b) 1 + 12/16 = 1 + 3/4. **1 3/4 in**
c) 46 + 7/16. **46 7/16 in**
d) 47 + 12/16 = 47 + 3/4. **47 3/4 in**
e) 48 + 10/16 = 48 + 5/8. **48 5/8 in**
f) 30 + 8/16 = 30 + 1/2. **30 1/2 in**
g) 92 + 14/16 = 92 + 7/8. **92 7/8 in**
h) 0 + 1/16. **1/16 in**

## Task 3 (level 1)

A: **9/16 in**, B: **1 5/8 in**, C: **2 1/4 in**, D: **2 13/16 in**. A good response says each reading out loud with its whole inches before writing it.

## Task 4 (level 1-2)

a) The denominator was misread. The tape has 16 spaces in an inch, so the count goes over 16: 6/16 = **3/8 in**.
b) The whole inches were dropped. The complete reading is 2 10/16 = **2 5/8 in**.
c) Only the numerator was divided, so the value changed. Divide both by 4: 12/16 = **3/4**.
d) Counting the inch line as a space adds one sixteenth. Six spaces past 5 in is 5 6/16 = **5 3/8 in**.

## Task 5 (level 2)

a) 3/4 = 12/16. 12/16 is more than 11/16 by 1/16. **3/4 in is longer by 1/16 in.**
b) In sixteenths: 3/8 = 6/16, 5/16, 1/4 = 4/16, 7/16. Order the numerators 4, 5, 6, 7: **1/4, 5/16, 3/8, 7/16 in**
c) 1/2 = 8/16. Add one sixteenth: **9/16 in**
d) 5/8 = 10/16. 10/16 − 9/16 = 1/16. **5/8 in is bigger by 1/16 in.**
e) 1 5/8 = 1 10/16. 1 10/16 − 1 9/16 = 1/16. **1 5/8 in is longer by 1/16 in.**

## Task 6 (level 2)

Open task. A good response:

- has four marks past the 12 in line, of the four kinds asked for

- shows each reading checked against the tape, and any correction written beside it
- explains a correction in words, for example "I counted the inch line" or "I left off the whole inches"
