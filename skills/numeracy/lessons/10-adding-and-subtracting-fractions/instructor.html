Adding and subtracting fractions on a cut list

Add and subtract fractional lengths and mixed numbers, allow for saw kerf and find the offcut on a cut list.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy; Identify the mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information

Session: Numeracy lesson 10 of 16. Total length: 90 minutes.

Before the session: learners need a fraction calculator (TI-30Xa or Sharp EL-531XT) or the ACCESS calculators page. A tape and a few offcuts help with the kerf demonstration. This lesson follows the Intake 142 decks for Day 08, Day 09, Day 10 and Day 11.

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Cut a short offcut in front of the group, or show a photo of a saw cut, and point to the sawdust. Say: "That sawdust used to be part of the board. On a cut list with ten cuts, it adds up." Ask: "Has anyone run short of material on a job, or cut a board and found the last piece too short?" Tell learners this is session 10, and it runs 90 minutes.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • read a cut list and say what the question is and what form the answer takes (Identify the task that will require you to use numeracy)
  • pick out the lengths, the number of pieces, the number of cuts and the kerf (Identify the mathematical information)
  • add and subtract fractions and mixed numbers, by hand and with the fraction key (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
  • check an answer by adding back and by asking if the offcut makes sense (Interpret and evaluate the information)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. 1/4 + 1/4 = ? (1/2)
  2. How many sixteenths are in 3/8? (6)

Learning tasks (70 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Teach same and different denominators. Use the spot-the-mistake item 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/6 and ask why the denominators cannot be added. 1.1 Explain in pairs why 2/6 is wrong. Board, learner page 7 min
1.2 Circulate as learners complete Task 1. 1.2 Complete Task 1. Learner page 7 min
2.1 Teach adding mixed numbers with the worked example. Show the fraction key on the TI-30Xa and the Sharp. 2.1 Work the example by hand, then check it on a calculator. Textbook p. 98, calculators 8 min
2.2 Teach borrowing with 5 1/4 − 2 7/8: 5 2/8 becomes 4 10/8. Show the check by adding back. 2.2 Follow the borrowing steps and check by adding back. Board 6 min
2.3 Circulate during Task 2. Ask each learner to check one subtraction by adding back. 2.3 Complete Task 2. Learner page 10 min
3.1 Teach kerf: count the cuts the question describes. Show textbook Example 4.14. 3.1 Listen, then count the cuts in Example 4.14. Textbook p. 103 6 min
3.2 Remind learners to read the whole cut list first. Circulate during Tasks 3 and 4. 3.2 Complete Tasks 3 and 4. Learner page 14 min
3.3 Run Task 5 as a whole-group discussion. 3.3 Complete Task 5 and explain each correction. Learner page 6 min
3.4 Link to feet and inches: carry 12 inches as 1 foot. 3.4 Complete Task 6. Learner page 6 min

Post-assessment (5 min)

  1. 7/8 + 3/16 = ? (1 1/16)
  2. 36 − 22 11/16 = ? (13 5/16)
  3. Two pieces of 40 1/4 in and 35 5/8 in are cut from a 96 in board with 2 cuts. The kerf is 1/8 in. How long is the offcut? (96 − 75 7/8 − 1/4 = 19 7/8 in)

Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

  • adding and subtracting fractions with a common denominator
  • adding mixed numbers and carrying a whole
  • borrowing when subtracting mixed numbers
  • allowing for saw kerf and finding the offcut
  • checking a subtraction by adding back

Further practice: Intake 142 lecture quizzes W03-L01, W03-L02 and W04-L01.
