# Adding and subtracting fractions on a cut list

## Task 1 (level 1)

a) 3/8 = 6/16. 6/16 + 7/16 = **13/16**
b) 5/8 + 6/8 = 11/8 = **1 3/8**
c) 15/16 − 6/16 = **9/16**
d) 11/16 − 4/16 = **7/16**
e) 2/4 + 1/4 = **3/4**

## Task 2 (level 1-2)

a) Wholes: 10 + 6 = 16. Fractions: 7/8 + 6/8 = 13/8 = 1 5/8. 16 + 1 5/8 = **17 5/8**
b) 12 3/16 − 5 10/16. 3/16 is smaller than 10/16, so borrow: 11 19/16 − 5 10/16 = **6 9/16**. Check: 6 9/16 + 5 5/8 = 12 3/16.
c) 6 = 5 8/8. 5 8/8 − 2 3/8 = **3 5/8**. Check: 3 5/8 + 2 3/8 = 6.
d) 5 2/8 − 2 7/8. Borrow: 4 10/8 − 2 7/8 = **2 3/8**. Check: 2 3/8 + 2 7/8 = 5 1/4.
e) Wholes: 18 + 24 + 9 = 51. Fractions: 3/8 + 4/8 = 7/8. **51 7/8 in**

## Task 3 (level 2)

a) 30 3/8 + 41 11/16 = 30 6/16 + 41 11/16 = 71 17/16 = **72 1/16 in**
b) 2 × 1/8 = **1/4 in**
c) 96 − 72 1/16 − 1/4 = 96 − 72 5/16 = **23 11/16 in**. Check: 72 1/16 + 1/4 + 23 11/16 = 96 in.

## Task 4 (level 2)

a) Half of 92 5/8: 92 5/8 = 741/8. 741/8 ÷ 2 = 741/16 = **46 5/16 in**
b) Blocks: 5 × 9 3/8 = 5 × 75/8 = 375/8 = 46 7/8 in. Cuts: 5 × 1/8 = 5/8 in. Offcut: 60 − 46 7/8 − 5/8 = 60 − 47 1/2 = **12 1/2 in**

## Task 5 (level 2)

a) The denominators were added. The parts are different sizes, so rename first: 2/4 + 1/4 = **3/4**.
b) The learner took 1/4 from 7/8 instead of 7/8 from 1/4. Borrow first: 4 10/8 − 2 7/8 = **2 3/8**. Check: 2 3/8 + 2 7/8 = 5 1/4.
c) Each saw cut removes one kerf of board. Leaving the cuts out makes the offcut look longer than it really is. Subtract the pieces and the number of cuts × the kerf. A good response names the missing quantity (cuts × kerf).

## Task 6 (level 2)

Feet: 6 + 2 = 8 ft. Inches: 4 3/4 + 10 1/2 = 15 1/4 in. 15 1/4 in is 1 ft 3 1/4 in, so carry 1 ft. **9 ft 3 1/4 in**

Check in inches: 6 ft 4 3/4 in = 76 3/4 in and 2 ft 10 1/2 in = 34 1/2 in. 76 3/4 + 34 1/2 = 111 1/4 in. 111 1/4 ÷ 12 = 9 remainder 3 1/4, so 9 ft 3 1/4 in.
