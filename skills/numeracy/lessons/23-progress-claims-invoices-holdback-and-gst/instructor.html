Progress claims, holdback and GST

Work out a monthly progress claim from percent complete, take off the 10% holdback the BC Builders Lien Act requires, add GST, and find when the holdback is paid.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the mathematical information; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information; Share the mathematical information, results and implications

Session: Numeracy lesson 23 of 34. Total length: 90 minutes.

Before the session: learners need a calculator and a calendar for 2027 (a phone calendar works). Open the BC Builders Lien Act on BC Laws and the Construction Prompt Payment Act status page, and check on the day whether the Prompt Payment Act or any change to the holdback period has come into force. If it has, change the Key ideas and Task 5 before class. Lesson 27 uses the holdback lag from this lesson.

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Tell this story: "A framing sub finishes a $124,000 job at the end of May. The crew has been paid. The lumber has been paid. In July, $12,400 of the money still has not arrived. Nobody did anything wrong." Ask: "Where is the money?" Take guesses. Say: "By the end of today you will be able to say where it is, and the date it can be paid." Tell learners this is session 23 of the Numeracy set, and it runs 90 minutes.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • find the line values, percents complete, holdback rate and dates in a claim (Identify the mathematical information)
  • connect value to date, last month's claim and this month's claim (Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information)
  • calculate a progress claim, the holdback, the GST and the amount due, and count the holdback days (Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question)
  • check a claim for errors and judge when the holdback will arrive (Interpret and evaluate the information)
  • explain the holdback to a new crew lead (Share the mathematical information, results and implications)

This lesson explains the rules in plain words. It is not legal advice. On a real job, the contract and a lawyer or accountant decide.

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. What is 40% of $45,000? ($18,000)
  2. What is 10% of $48,000? ($4,800)

Post-assessment (5 min)

  1. A claim is $36,000. What is the holdback? ($3,600)
  2. What GST is billed with that claim? ($36,000 − $3,600 = $32,400, × 0.05 = $1,620)
  3. A certificate of completion is issued on September 10. When does the 55-day holdback period end? (September has 30 days: 20 days to September 30, then 35 more, which is November 4)

Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

  • the schedule of values and this month's claim as value to date minus last month
  • the 10% holdback in section 4 of the Builders Lien Act, and that it is not kept from workers or material suppliers
  • GST on the net claim now, and on the holdback when it is released
  • the certificate of completion, the 45-day lien filing time and the 55-day holdback period
  • the coming change to 46 days under the Construction Prompt Payment Act
