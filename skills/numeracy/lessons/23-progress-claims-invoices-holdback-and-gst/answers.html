Progress claims, holdback and GST

Work out a monthly progress claim from percent complete, take off the 10% holdback the BC Builders Lien Act requires, add GST, and find when the holdback is paid.

Task 1 (level 1)

a) 10% (Builders Lien Act, section 4 (1)). b) No. Section 4 (6) says a holdback must not be kept from a worker or a material supplier. c) 55 days (section 8). When the Construction Prompt Payment Act comes into force, this changes to 46 days.

Task 3 (level 2-3)

a) $120,000.00 + $4,000.00 = $124,000.00 b) - This month's claim: $124,000.00 − $95,500.00 = $28,500.00 - Holdback: $28,500.00 × 0.10 = $2,850.00 - Net claim: $28,500.00 − $2,850.00 = $25,650.00 - GST: $25,650.00 × 0.05 = $1,282.50 - Amount due: $25,650.00 + $1,282.50 = $26,932.50

c) $4,800.00 + $4,750.00 + $2,850.00 = $12,400.00. Check: $124,000.00 × 0.10 = $12,400.00. It matches.

Task 4 (level 3)

Mistake 1: the net claim is wrong. $47,500.00 − $4,750.00 = $42,750.00, not $42,570.00 (two digits swapped). Mistake 2: the GST was worked out on the whole claim. It should be on the net claim: $42,750.00 × 0.05 = $2,137.50, not $2,375.00.

Correct amount due: $42,750.00 + $2,137.50 = $44,887.50. The clerk's sheet asked for $57.50 more than it should.

Task 5 (level 3)

a) June 4 + 45 days = Monday, July 19, 2027 b) June 4 + 55 days = Thursday, July 29, 2027. The holdback can be paid after that day if no lien has been filed. c) Holdback: $12,400.00. GST: $12,400.00 × 0.05 = $620.00. Invoice: $12,400.00 + $620.00 = $13,020.00 d) June 4 + 46 days = Tuesday, July 20, 2027

Counting: June has 30 days, so June 4 to June 30 is 26 days. 55 − 26 = 29, which is July 29.

Task 6 (level 3)

The work was done at the end of May (May 31). The earliest the holdback can be paid is the day after the holdback period ends, July 30. May 31 to July 30 is 60 days, which is 60 ÷ 7 = 8.57, about 8 and a half weeks, and often later.

A good answer explains, in plain words:

  • the law makes the payer keep back 10% of every claim
  • the holdback is only paid after the certificate of completion and the 55-day holdback period, and only if no lien has been filed
  • so the company has to pay its crew and suppliers for weeks before the last 10% arrives (lesson 27 shows what this does to cash)
