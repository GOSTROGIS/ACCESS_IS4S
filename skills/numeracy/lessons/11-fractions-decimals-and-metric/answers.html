Task 3 (level 2)

Divide by 25.4, multiply by 16, round to the nearest whole number of sixteenths.

a) 762 ÷ 25.4 = 30 in exactly. 30 in b) 2,000 ÷ 25.4 × 16 = 1,259.84, which rounds to 1,260 sixteenths. 1,260 ÷ 16 = 78.75. 78 3/4 in c) 100 ÷ 25.4 × 16 = 62.99, which rounds to 63 sixteenths. 63/16 = 3 15/16 in d) 50 ÷ 25.4 × 16 = 31.496, which rounds down to 31 sixteenths. 31/16 = 1 15/16 in e) 1,830 ÷ 25.4 × 16 = 1,152.76, which rounds to 1,153 sixteenths. 1,153/16 = 72 1/16 in. 72 in = 6 ft, so 6 ft 1/16 in.
