Loans and interest for a truck or tools

Work out simple and compound interest, follow a loan payment month by month, and compare the total cost of loans of different lengths before you borrow for a truck or tools.

  • Components: Identify the mathematical information; Make connections between related pieces of mathematical information; Apply mathematical operations and tools you will need to answer the question; Interpret and evaluate the information; Share the mathematical information, results and implications

Task 1 (level 1-2)

a) 6 months = 6 ÷ 12 = 0.5 years. $1,800.00 × 0.09 × 0.5 = $81.00 b) $3,000.00 × 0.065 × 2 = $390.00. Total paid back: $3,000.00 + $390.00 = $3,390.00

Task 2 (level 2)

Each month the lender first charges interest on the balance: $25,000.00 × 0.079 ÷ 12 = $164.58. The payment pays that interest first. Only what is left, $505.71 − $164.58 = $341.13, comes off what Sam owes. A good answer uses the words interest and principal (or balance).

Task 3 (level 2-3)

Month Balance at start Interest Payment Principal paid Balance at end
1 $25,000.00 $164.58 $505.71 $341.13 $24,658.87
2 $24,658.87 $162.34 $505.71 $343.37 $24,315.50
3 $24,315.50 $160.08 $505.71 $345.63 $23,969.87

Month 2: $24,658.87 × 0.079 ÷ 12 = $162.34. $505.71 − $162.34 = $343.37. $24,658.87 − $343.37 = $24,315.50. Month 3: $24,315.50 × 0.079 ÷ 12 = $160.08. $505.71 − $160.08 = $345.63. $24,315.50 − $345.63 = $23,969.87.

Each month a little less goes to interest and a little more to principal.

Task 4 (level 2)

a)

Term Monthly payment Total paid Interest paid
48 months $609.15 $29,239.20 $4,239.20
60 months $505.71 $30,342.60 $5,342.60
84 months $388.41 $32,626.44 $7,626.44

48 months: $609.15 × 48 = $29,239.20, and $29,239.20 − $25,000.00 = $4,239.20. 84 months: $388.41 × 84 = $32,626.44, and $32,626.44 − $25,000.00 = $7,626.44.

b) $7,626.44 − $4,239.20 = $3,387.24 more. c) The 84-month loan. FCAC calls terms of 72 months or more long-term.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) Total paid: $404.57 × 60 = $24,274.20. Interest: $24,274.20 − $20,000.00 = $4,274.20 b) $5,342.60 − $4,274.20 = $1,068.40 less interest.

Task 6 (level 3)

a)

Month Balance at start Interest (1%) Balance at end
1 $5,000.00 $50.00 $5,050.00
2 $5,050.00 $50.50 $5,100.50
3 $5,100.50 $51.01 $5,151.51

Month 3: $5,100.50 × 0.01 = $51.005, which rounds to $51.01.

b) $5,634.13 − $5,000.00 = $634.13 c) $5,000.00 × 0.12 × 1 = $600.00. The compound amount is $34.13 more because each month's interest is added to the balance, and the next month's interest is charged on that interest too.

Task 7 (level 3)

a) Dealer: $323.57 × 72 = $23,297.04, plus the $350.00 fee = $23,647.04. Credit union: $571.56 × 36 = $20,576.16. b) Dealer: $23,647.04 − $18,000.00 = $5,647.04 (interest $5,297.04 plus the $350.00 fee). Credit union: $20,576.16 − $18,000.00 = $2,576.16. c) Only the dealer loan fits a $400 budget ($323.57 is under $400, $571.56 is not). It costs $5,647.04 − $2,576.16 = $3,070.88 more than the credit union loan. A good answer may add that Sam could wait and save a bigger down payment, or look for a cheaper trailer, so the shorter loan fits.

Task 8 (level 3, HP 10bII+)

a) The screen shows −505.71. It is negative because it is money Sam pays out. The $25,000.00 in PV is positive because it is money Sam received. b) PMT shows −388.41, which matches the 84-month payment in Task 4.

Task 9 (level 3)

Open task. A good answer:

  • says a longer term lowers the payment: $388.41 over 84 months against $609.15 over 48 months for the same $25,000.00
  • says the longer term costs more in total: $7,626.44 interest against $4,239.20, or $3,387.24 more
  • may use Task 7 to show that a fee adds to the cost of borrowing
  • names one thing to ask, such as the APR, the total cost of borrowing, any fees, or whether there is a penalty for paying the loan off early
  • gives facts and numbers and does not tell the friend what to buy
