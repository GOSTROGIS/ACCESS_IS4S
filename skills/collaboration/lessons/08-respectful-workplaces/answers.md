# Respectful workplaces, bullying and harassment

## Task 1 (level 2)

| Situation | Answer | Matches |
|---|---|---|
| a) Welds failed, redo them | Not bullying and harassment | Reasonable management action, constructive feedback about work |
| b) Yelling and name-calling every morning | Likely bullying and harassment | Verbal aggression or yelling, calling someone derogatory names |
| c) Two journeypersons disagree, then agree | Not bullying and harassment | Expressing a different opinion |
| d) Rumour about how she got hired | Likely bullying and harassment | Spreading malicious rumours |
| e) Apprentices taped to a post | Likely bullying and harassment | Humiliating initiation practices or hazing |
| f) Assigned to cleanup for the afternoon | Not bullying and harassment | Reasonable management action (assigning work) |
| g) Customer's repeated comments about her body | Likely bullying and harassment | Inappropriate comment that would humiliate or intimidate. The "person" can be a customer. It may also be sexual harassment under the *Human Rights Code* |
| h) Shown a safer way to hold the grinder | Not bullying and harassment | Guidance or advice about work |

**Steps:** compare each situation with the "can be" and "is not" table. Ask: would a reasonable person know this would humiliate or intimidate the worker? Is it a reasonable part of directing the work?

## Task 3 (level 2)

**Open task.** A good plan, in order:

1. Does not like, share, laugh at or add to the video.
2. If safe, posts something short in the chat or says it in person, such as "Not cool. Take it down." Or chooses not to post and goes straight to reporting.
3. Checks on Nico privately: "Are you okay? That wasn't fair to you."
4. Reports it to the supervisor or the person named in the employer's procedure, as soon as possible, because reporting is a worker duty.
5. Keeps a note of what they saw and when.

The plan should not include getting even with the crew members or confronting them in a way that could start a fight.

## Task 4 (level 2)

**What a good report covers:** facts only, no labels or guesses about why people did it.

- what happened: Nico was taped to a column during lunch, it was filmed, and the video was posted in the crew group chat with "Welcome, rookie"
- when and where: the day and time, the location on site, the time of the post
- who was involved: the people seen taping and filming, if known
- who else saw it: people in the group chat or at lunch
- what the writer has done: did not share the video, checked on Nico, is reporting now

**Example:** "On Tuesday at about 12:15, during lunch at the level 2 laydown area, two crew members taped Nico to a column. One filmed it. At 12:40 the video was posted in the crew group chat with the words 'Welcome, rookie.' About 12 people are in the chat. I did not share it. I spoke to Nico at 1:00. I'm reporting it under our bullying and harassment policy."
