Collaboration · Lesson 06 · Answers

Plan and build together, then debrief

Share information, agree on a plan, build a model bridge within a budget, handle a change order and look back on how the team worked.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 100 min
  • Components: Achieve a common goal with others; Facilitate an environment where you can collaborate with others; Reflect and improve on teamwork

Task 1 (level 2)

A complete spec list has every item from all four cards:

  1. Crosses a 30 cm gap.
  2. Paper and masking tape only.
  3. Rests on the two sides, not taped to them, does not touch the floor.
  4. Holds a cup of washers at the centre of the deck for 10 seconds. Score is washers held.
  5. Total cost $30 or less: paper $2 a sheet, tape $1 for each 10 cm, no refunds.
  6. Deck at least 8 cm wide.
  7. A 30-second explanation for the client before the test.

After the change order: 8. A handrail on one side, at least 3 cm high, in place for the test.

Steps: each person reads their card. One person writes. Before moving on, someone reads the list back and each card holder confirms their items are there.

Task 2 (level 2)

Open task. A good response shows two different sketches, a short note of what each side said, a paraphrase from the other side, and a final plan that names what was taken from each idea (for example, "folded beams from idea 1, a wider taped deck from idea 2"). Every member should be able to say what the plan is.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Open. Check the arithmetic: (sheets × $2) + (tape in cm ÷ 10 × $1) must be $30 or less.

b) Team Pine - Paper: 9 × $2 = $18 - Tape: 80 ÷ 10 = 8, and 8 × $1 = $8 - Total: $18 + $8 = $26 - Within budget, with $30 − $26 = $4 left.

c) Team Fir - Paper: 12 × $2 = $24 - Tape: 70 ÷ 10 = 7, and 7 × $1 = $7 - Total: $24 + $7 = $31 - Over budget by $31 − $30 = $1. They need to drop 1 sheet (saves $2) or 10 cm of tape (saves $1).

d) Open. Every member has a named role. No one is "just watching".

Task 4 (level 2-3)

Open task. A good result:

  • the bridge meets every spec on the list, including the handrail from the change order
  • the team stayed within budget, including anything bought for the change order
  • the 30-second explanation says how the load is carried (for example, "folded paper beams under the deck carry the weight to each side")
  • the score and spec check are recorded honestly

Task 5 (level 3)

Open task. A strong debrief:

  • a) and b) name specific actions, not people's personalities ("we read every card before sketching", not "Sam is bossy")
  • c) uses evidence, such as the observer's notes on who spoke and who had a job
  • d) says how the team decided and whether everyone knew about the change
  • e) gives one specific, doable suggestion, such as "Next time we will test a small piece before building the whole deck"
  • f) names real feedback and a real action in response

Task 6 (level 2)

a) 2,400 ÷ 400 = 6 spaces. A stud at each end means 6 + 1 = 7 studs. b) 2,400 − 38 − 38 = 2,324 mm. c) 7 studs + 2 plates = 9 lengths. Each stud and each plate needs its own 2,440 mm length. d) 9 × $5.25 = $47.25. Left: $50.00 − $47.25 = $2.75. e) Open. Every person has a named role.

Task 7 (level 3)

a) No. Each stud offcut is 2,440 − 2,324 = 116 mm. Each plate offcut is 2,440 − 2,400 = 40 mm. Neither is long enough for a 2,324 mm stud. b) One more length costs $5.25. The team has $2.75 left. $5.25 − $2.75 = $2.50 over budget. c) Sample: "We can add the stud. It needs one more 2x4, and that takes us $2.50 over the $50 budget. Do you approve the extra $2.50?" A good answer says yes to the work, states the cost clearly and asks for approval before buying. A change order usually changes the price or the time.

Check yourself 1

Each card had only part of the brief. A person with only Card A would not know the budget, the load test, the deck width or the client explanation. Missing any card means missing specs.

Check yourself 2

Everyone can live with the plan and will work on it, even if it is not each person's first choice. It does not mean everyone thinks it is the best plan.

Check yourself 3

  • Paper: 7 × $2 = $14
  • Tape: 90 ÷ 10 = 9, and 9 × $1 = $9
  • Total: $14 + $9 = $23
  • Within budget, with $7 left.

Check yourself 4

Any two of: What went well? What did not go well? Did everyone get a chance to contribute? How did we handle the change? What will we do differently next time? What feedback did we get, and what will we do with it?

Check yourself 5

It is specific, about the work or the process rather than the person, and something the team can actually do. Example: "Next time we will read the spec list back before we buy materials," instead of "We need to communicate better."
