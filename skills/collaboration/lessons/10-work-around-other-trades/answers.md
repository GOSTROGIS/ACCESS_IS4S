## Task 4 (level 2)

## Task 8 (level 3)

**a)** No. Boarding covers the insulation and vapour barrier. There is no sign it passed inspection, and the barrier is torn, so it may fail. Covering it now could mean tearing the board off later.
**b)** Your foreman, right away. Sample: "Day 9, unit 3 washroom. No insulation inspection sticker, and the vapour barrier is torn in two places. We're not boarding it. Can you check with the super if it passed and who fixes the barrier?"
**c)** Work in another area that is ready, stock board, set up, or prep corners in rooms that passed. A good answer keeps the crew working without covering the held wall.

## Task 9 (level 3)

1 Framing, 2 Plumbing rough-in, 3 Electrical rough-in, 4 Rough-in inspection (H), 5 Insulation and vapour barrier, 6 Drywall, 7 Cabinets and finish.

Plumbing and electrical rough-in can be in either order or overlap. Many BC cities also inspect insulation and vapour barrier before drywall, so a second H between steps 5 and 6 is also correct.

## Task 10 (level 3)

**a)** Sample: "Please hold off on that wall. My boxes aren't done and they haven't been inspected. I'll get my foreman to talk to yours right now."
**b)** Your foreman or lead hand, right away. Your foreman talks to the drywall foreman or the superintendent.
**c)** Electrical rough wiring must be inspected before it is concealed. Boarding over it means the board may have to come off for the inspection. That costs the drywall company, the GC and the schedule, not just you.

## Check yourself 1

When one piece of work must be finished before the next can start.

## Check yourself 2

A point where work must stop until an inspection passes. Nobody covers the work until then.

## Check yourself 3

Any two: know who is next, say when you are done, say early when you will be late, protect other trades' work, do not move their material, never cover work that has not passed inspection.

## Check yourself 4

The foremen of the two companies, or the superintendent.

## Check yourself 5

The finish date moves later by one day too.
