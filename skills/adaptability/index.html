Adaptability

This small fictional example shows the skill in use. ORIGINAL: 24 participants. UPDATE: 26 participants.

Lessons

8 ACCESS lessons for adaptability, each with a learner page, an instructor lesson plan and answers.

1Showing up and managing your time on a job site

3Responding to change on a project

4Staying calm under pressure and handling anger
