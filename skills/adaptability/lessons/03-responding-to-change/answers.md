# Responding to change on a project

## Task 1 (level 1)

| Changed | Stays the same | Still do not know |
|---|---|---|
| Drawing A-301 is now revision C. | All other Unit 4 walls go ahead. | The new header size or type. |
| Unit 4 window W3 is 1525 mm wide, not 1220 mm. | The project, the crew and the superintendent. | Exactly what time Thursday the header arrives. |
| The W3 header changes. | Questions still go to Rob. | What the crew does with the time freed on Wednesday. |
| The east wall is on hold until Thursday. | | Whether any other drawings changed. |
| Lumber must be covered by noon Wednesday. | | Whether the stud layout on the east wall also changes. |
| Revision B copies must be marked SUPERSEDED. | | |

Accept any accurate items, at least two per column.

## Task 2 (level 2)

a. Framing the Unit 4 east wall (7:15 to 10:15).

b. 10:15 − 7:15 = **3 hours**.

c. Open answer. One plan that follows the memo:

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 7:00 to 7:15 | Toolbox talk, including the memo |
| 7:15 to 9:00 | Frame Unit 4 north wall |
| 9:00 to 11:30 | Frame Unit 4 west wall (first part) |
| 11:30 to 12:00 | Cover the lumber stack |
| 12:00 to 12:30 | Lunch |
| 12:30 to 1:00 | Finish Unit 4 west wall |
| 1:00 to 3:30 | Gap: ask Rob. Possible tasks include helping another unit or cleaning up. |

Check that the plan:

- does not frame the east wall
- covers the lumber before noon
- keeps the north and west walls
- names a gap and who to ask, instead of guessing

## Task 3 (level 2)

1. **Change the approach.** The same practice is not working. Try a conversion chart, worked examples, or ask for help.
2. **Change the approach.** Calling again will not help. Tell the foreman, try the supplier's dispatch line or send an email.
3. **Change the approach.** The wind is outside your control. Switch to a task that does not need the crane and tell your supervisor.
4. **Stop and report.** A cracked ladder is unsafe. Take it out of use and report it.
5. **Keep trying.** You are making progress.

## Task 4 (level 1)

Open and private. Examples: a grandparent, a Sunday family call, a morning coffee routine, a sports team, a home community, cultural or spiritual practice, a pet. Do not mark the content. Check only that the learner understood the idea.

## Task 5 (level 2)

Open and private. A complete answer says who started the change, what the learner did at first and later, and names something that helped. Do not mark the content of the experience.

## Check yourself

1. Notice, sort, ask, adjust, check.
2. Any two of: the weather forecast, the schedule, the revision letter on the drawings, delivery times, who is on the crew.
3. When it is unsafe. Stop and report it.
4. A person, place, routine or belief that stays steady while other parts of life change.
