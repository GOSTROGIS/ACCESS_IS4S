Looking after yourself with wellness and support in BC

Look after your sleep, energy and boundaries, map your supports, and know which BC help line to call and when.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Regulate your emotions when appropriate; Seek self-improvement; Demonstrate responsibility

Task 1 (level 1)

Open and private. Do not collect or mark the ratings. A complete answer has a rating and one small, doable action for each of the four areas. Examples: mental (do 15 minutes of trade math practice), emotional (call a relative), spiritual (spend time on the land, attend a ceremony or gathering, or whatever the learner names), physical (go to bed by 10 p.m.).

Task 2 (level 1)

  1. Call or text 9-8-8, or call 1-800-784-2433. If they are in immediate danger, call 9-1-1. Stay with them if it is safe.
  2. 9-1-1.
  3. HealthLink BC, 8-1-1. A nurse can advise whether to get care. If bleeding will not stop, call 9-1-1.
  4. Hope for Wellness Helpline, 1-855-242-3310, or the KUU-US Crisis Line, 1-800-588-8717.
  5. 310-6789 (no area code). Accept Hope for Wellness or KUU-US for an Indigenous learner who prefers them.
  6. Opioid Treatment Access Line, 1-833-804-8111.

Task 3 (level 2)

Open answer. Each "no" should be clear, keep any reason short, and offer something else where possible. Samples:

  1. "I can't lend you the truck tonight. I start my new job at 6:30 and I need it. I can drive you on Saturday."
  2. "I can stay late Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, but not Wednesday. I have class that night."
  3. "I'm not coming out tonight. My Level 1 exam is tomorrow. Let's go out Friday to celebrate."

A reply that gives in ("I guess I could skip class") or attacks ("Stop always asking me") should be revised.

Task 4 (level 2)

Open and private. A complete map has at least one name or service in each circle and a realistic backup plan for getting to work, such as a second ride, a bus route and its first departure time, or a crew member who lives nearby.

Task 5 (level 2)

a. 6:30 − 1:00 = 5:30. 5:30 − 0:45 = 4:45 a.m.

b. 4:45 a.m. − 7 h = 9:45 p.m.

c. 4:45 a.m. − 8 h = 8:45 p.m.

Check yourself

  1. 7 to 9 hours of good-quality sleep.
  2. Mental, emotional, spiritual and physical.
  3. Say no clearly, give a short reason if you want to, offer something else if you can.
  4. 9-8-8 (call or text).
  5. The KUU-US Crisis Line, 1-800-588-8717.
