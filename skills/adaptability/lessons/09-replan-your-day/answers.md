b. With the plan above:

- crane time planned: 7:15 to 9:30 (135) + 9:45 to 12:00 (135) = **270 minutes**
- crane time in the new plan: 11:00 to 12:00 (60) + 12:30 to 3:30 (180) = **240 minutes**
- beam setting that moves: 270 − 240 = **30 minutes**

The bolt-up of today's beams also moves to another day, because the afternoon is now used for setting.

c. Example: "Crane's down till 11, so we're bolting up Level 2 and shaking out the next load till then. We'll set beams from 11 through the afternoon and run about half an hour short on setting, with today's bolt-up moving to tomorrow. Okay?"

## Task 8 (level 3)

a. Protect what can be **finished before the rain at 1:00**. Pave complete sections in an order that leaves no half-done area, starting with the part the client needs most (for example, the entrance and drive lanes). Reason: from 7:30 to 1:00 is 330 minutes. At one truck every 20 minutes that is 17 loads (7:30, 7:50, ... 12:50). At one every 40 minutes it is 9 loads (7:30, 8:10, ... 12:50). So the crew can place only about half of the planned asphalt.

b. Any two sensible tasks, for example: prepare the next section (sweep, check grades), finish and roll edges, set up or adjust traffic control, check and clean equipment.

c. Tell the client **this morning, as soon as the crew knows**, not at 1:00. Say how much will be paved today, which part, and when the rest will be done.
