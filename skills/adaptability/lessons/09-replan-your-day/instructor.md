# Re-plan your day when the job changes

Adaptability lesson 9 of 12. Total time: 90 minutes.

## Opening

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Tell a short story about a day on a job when a crane, lift, truck or inspector did not show up. What did the crew do for the first hour? Ask: "When a plan breaks, who decides what you do next?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 9 of 12, that it builds on lesson 3 (responding to change), and that the session runs 90 minutes.

## Objective

**Time:** included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- read a day plan drawn as a bar chart and work out task lengths in minutes and hours (Plan and prioritize)
- sort tasks into fixed and movable, and check whether a task is ready to start (Plan and prioritize)
- rebuild a day plan after a change, keeping every part that still works (Persist and persevere)
- tell a foreman what changed, what they will do instead and what will be late (Demonstrate responsibility)

## Pre-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.2)

1. "A task runs from 9:45 to 12:00. How many minutes is that?"
2. "The crane breaks down at 7:00. Name one thing a framing crew could still do."

### Notes for the instructor

- This lesson is a first step toward schedules. The bar chart of a day is a small version of the bar (Gantt) charts that superintendents use for a whole job. Keep it to one day here.
- Task 8 uses a job spec that says no paving in rain. Real paving specs differ by owner and by material. Treat it as the rule for this fictional job only.
- Some learners may be shift workers or parents who re-plan every day. Invite them to share how they do it.

## Post-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 4.1)

1. "The lift is down until 1:00. Your plan had lift work from 7:15 to 9:30 and 9:45 to 12:00. How many minutes of lift work cannot happen this morning?" (270)
2. "A task has material, drawings, tools and a clear area, but the hot work permit is not issued. Is it ready?" (No.)
3. "Say the five questions to re-plan your day in order."

## Summary

**Time:** 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- reading a day plan as a bar chart, in minutes and hours
- fixed and movable items
- the five checks for whether a task is ready to start
- the five questions to re-plan a day, and checking that the new plan adds up
- telling the foreman what will be late
