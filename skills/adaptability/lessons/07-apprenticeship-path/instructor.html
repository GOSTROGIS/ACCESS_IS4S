Adaptability · Lesson 07 · Instructor

Keep learning as an apprentice in BC

Map the BC apprenticeship path from sponsor to Red Seal, research a trade, and build a learning plan you can check.

  • Components: Set or adjust your goals and expectations; Plan and prioritize; Seek self-improvement

Adaptability lesson 7 of 14. Total time: 90 minutes.

Opening

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Share how you got into your trade: the first job, the sponsor, a level you found hard. Ask: "How is this session going to be useful to you?" (the opening question from the ACCESS occupations lesson). Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 7 of 14, and that the session is 90 minutes. Learners will need a computer or phone for step 3.1.

Objective

Time: included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • read the TEER category from a NOC code and explain what it means (Set or adjust your goals and expectations)
  • put the BC apprenticeship steps in order and name the credentials (Plan and prioritize)
  • research a trade using current official sources and record where each fact came from (Seek self-improvement)
  • write a short, mid and long-term learning plan with checkable goals (Set or adjust your goals and expectations, Plan and prioritize)

Pre-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.2)

  1. "What is the difference between a registered apprentice and someone working as a helper?"
  2. "What do you need to get a Red Seal?"

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Opening story, objectives, session length. Answer the opening question. 5 min
1.2 Pre-assessment questions. Answer aloud. 5 min
2.1 From the ACCESS occupations lesson: brainstorm all the jobs learners can think of. Then sort them on the whiteboard into jobs needing no training, some training, an apprenticeship, and college or university. Stress that all of these jobs need hard work and experience. 2.2 Map the sorted groups onto the TEER table. Share the WorkBC outlook figures. Complete Task 2. Learner page 10 min
2.3 Teach the 8 apprenticeship steps and the 80/20 split. Show the SkilledTradesBC "Start an Apprenticeship" page if a projector is available. Complete Task 1 in pairs with cut-up step cards. Step cards, projector 13 min
2.4 Explain compulsory trades, the 2:1 supervision ratio and the crane trades coming in 2027. Ask questions. Note any compulsory trade they are interested in. Learner page 7 min
3.1 Help learners find their trade on WorkBC and SkilledTradesBC. Remind them to record the source and to use today's figures, not old ones. Complete Task 3. Computers or phones 15 min

Total: 5 + 5 + 12 + 10 + 13 + 7 + 15 + 5 + 10 + 5 + 3 = 90 minutes.

The learner page also has a figure of the eight apprenticeship steps and a second worked example on hours to the next level (1,200 of 1,500 hours at 38 hours a week). Use it with Task 4. These fit inside the existing step times.

Notes for the instructor

  • The old ACCESS occupations handout used United States wage figures from 2005, and the occupations lesson referred to the Industry Training Authority (ITA). The ITA is now SkilledTradesBC. Neither the old figures nor the old name are used here.
  • The old Individual Enhancement Plan referred to TOWES scores and the older skills profiles. That framework was replaced by Skills for Success in 2021. Keep the good part of the IEP: short, mid and long-term goals, and an individual record that is shared with the learner.
  • The old career exploration lesson matched personality types to jobs. This lesson uses interests, working conditions and research instead. Encourage learners to think about what they like doing (working outdoors, at heights, with machines, with people) and about practical conditions (camp work, shift work, travel).
  • Learners may ask about program-specific funding. Refer them to ACCESS program staff for ACCESS supports.

Post-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 4.1)

  1. "A friend works as an unregistered helper for a sheet metal contractor. Is there a problem? What should they do?"
  2. "Put these in order: certification exam, find a sponsor, Recommendation for Certification, register in the Portal, technical training. Which step needs your sponsor to act?"
  3. "Read one of your goals from Task 5. How will you know it is done?"

Summary

Time: 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • NOC codes and TEER categories, and where BC job openings are
  • the 8 steps of a BC apprenticeship, and the credentials
  • BC's compulsory trades
  • the Canada Apprentice Loan and EI for technical training
  • writing a learning plan with goals you can check

Offline option for Task 3: Prepare dated copies of a WorkBC career profile and the matching SkilledTradesBC trade profile. Learners can complete the same table from those copies, recording the page title, URL and date printed. Flag any fact not supplied for later checking instead of guessing.
