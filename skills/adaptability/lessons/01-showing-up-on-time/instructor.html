Adaptability · Lesson 01 · Instructor

Showing up and managing your time on a job site

Plan your arrival, tell your supervisor early when plans change, and sort your tasks so the important ones get done.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Demonstrate responsibility; Plan and prioritize

Adaptability lesson 1 of 14. Total time: 75 minutes.

Opening

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Tell a short story from your own work life about a day when one late person, or one early call, changed how a crew's morning went. Keep it to two minutes. Then ask: "Who has waited on someone who did not show up? How did it feel?" Explain that this is the first of fourteen Adaptability lessons, that Adaptability now includes what used to be called Continuous Learning, and that today's session runs 75 minutes.

Start from strength. Learners already juggle buses, family, appointments and money. Use language of building on what they do well rather than fixing a problem.

Objective

Time: included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • work back from a start time to plan arrival with a buffer (Plan and prioritize)
  • notify a supervisor of a late arrival or absence clearly and early (Demonstrate responsibility)
  • name what a first-day safety orientation in BC must cover (Demonstrate responsibility)
  • sort tasks by urgency and importance and plan time for important tasks (Plan and prioritize)

Pre-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.2)

  1. "What do you do now to make sure you get somewhere on time?"
  2. "If you were going to be late for work, who would you tell, and how?"

Write the answers on the whiteboard. Return to them at the post-assessment.

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Opening story, objectives, session length. Listen. Share one experience of waiting for someone. Whiteboard 5 min
1.2 Ask the two pre-assessment questions. Answer aloud or on a sticky note. Sticky notes 5 min
2.1 Walk through Dale's worked example on the board, one subtraction at a time. Ask learners to check each time. Show the timeline figure and point out that it is read from right to left. Point fast finishers to the second worked example (Marie's cancelled bus) as a model for Task 2. Follow the example. Complete Task 1 and compare with a partner. Learner page, whiteboard 12 min
2.2 Explain what a good late or sick message contains. Share the BC sick leave facts: 5 paid and 3 unpaid days after 90 days, proof on request, no doctor's note for the first two short absences. Remind learners that union agreements and employer policies can add rules. Write Task 2. Two volunteers read theirs aloud. Learner page 10 min
2.3 Explain the young and new worker orientation required in BC. Ask who has had one and what it covered. List what they were told at a past orientation, or what they would ask. Learner page 8 min
3.1 Draw the four boxes. Use the definitions from the ACCESS time management lesson: urgent is "of critical time value", important is "of great value". Stress that no box is "bad". The aim is balance, so box B gets time. Complete Task 3 in pairs. Share one item the pair disagreed on. Learner page 12 min
3.2 Connect sleep to safety: WorkSafeBC says workers must not work while overly tired. Share the 7 to 9 hour sleep guideline. Complete Task 4. Learner page 8 min
3.3 Lead the group agreements discussion from the ACCESS first-day lesson. Agree on a short list and post it. Complete Task 5. Vote on the group list. Flip chart 7 min
4.1 Ask the post-assessment questions. Answer. Whiteboard 5 min
4.2 Summarize. Name one change they will make this week. 3 min

Total: 5 + 5 + 12 + 10 + 8 + 12 + 8 + 7 + 5 + 3 = 75 minutes.

Post-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 4.1)

  1. "Your start is 7:00 a.m., gearing up takes 10 minutes and your drive is 30 minutes. With a 10-minute buffer, when do you leave?" (6:10 a.m.)
  2. "What goes into a good message to your supervisor when you will be late?"
  3. "Which box is 'register for technical training before seats fill', and why?"

Summary

Time: 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • working back from a start time, with a buffer
  • telling your supervisor early, and BC sick leave basics
  • what a first-day safety orientation must include
  • urgent versus important, and why box B needs planned time
