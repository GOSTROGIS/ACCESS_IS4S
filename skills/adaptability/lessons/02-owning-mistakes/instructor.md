# Owning your work and your mistakes

Adaptability lesson 2 of 8. Total time: 75 minutes.

## Opening

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Share a time you made a mistake at work and what happened when you reported it, or when you did not. Keep it short and honest. Ask: "Who would you rather work beside: someone who never admits a mistake, or someone who tells you right away?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 2 of 8, and that the session is 75 minutes.

## Objective

**Time:** included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- tell owning a mistake apart from excuses and blame (Demonstrate responsibility)
- state the BC duty to report unsafe conditions and acts (Demonstrate responsibility)
- report a mistake in four parts, including one change for next time (Demonstrate responsibility, Seek self-improvement)
- answer criticism with the A B C D tools (Seek self-improvement)

## Pre-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.2)

1. "What does it mean to be accountable at work?"
2. "Who are we accountable to, and what for?"

These are the brainstorm questions from the original ACCESS lesson. Write answers on the whiteboard in two columns: "to whom" and "for what".

### Notes for the instructor

- Some learners have been blamed unfairly in school, work or the justice system. Keep the role plays about work statements, not personal history.
- In step 3.3, stop any role play that turns into real insults. The boundary tool is for real situations, and learners should not have to sit through name-calling to practise it.
- If a learner describes ongoing humiliation or intimidation at a job, refer them to lesson 4 (bullying and harassment) and to program support staff.

## Post-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 4.1)

1. "You see a guardrail missing on the second floor. What must you do?"
2. "Give the four parts of a mistake report for this: you cut a door header 50 mm short."
3. "Your foreman says 'This is wrong' and walks off. Which A B C D tool would you use, and what would you say?"

## Summary

**Time:** 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- what accountability is, and what it is not
- the BC duty to report unsafe conditions and acts
- control, influence and no control
- reporting a mistake in four parts
- the A B C D tools for answering criticism
