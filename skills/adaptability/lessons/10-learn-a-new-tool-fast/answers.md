# Learn a new tool or method fast

## Task 1 (level 1)

Order: **read, watch, try, check.**

1. Ask the journeyperson why they hold the tool that way: **watch**.
2. Compare your cut with the line on the drawing: **check**.
3. Read the safety section of the manual: **read**.
4. Make three practice cuts on scrap: **try**.

## Task 4 (level 2)

Open answer. Good questions are specific to the tool and ask about safety, checks and mistakes. Examples for a rotary laser level: "How do I check that it is levelled before I take readings?", "What do I do if it is bumped during use?", "How far away does the detector still read?"

## Task 6 (level 2)

Open answer. The four times must add to 45. Example:

| Step | Minutes | What I will do |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 10 | Safety page, set-up steps, how to check level |
| Watch | 5 | Journeyperson sets it up and takes one reading |
| Try | 20 | Set it up myself twice and take readings at 3 marks |
| Check | 10 | Compare my readings with the journeyperson's and ask what I missed |
| **Total** | **45** | |

10 + 5 + 20 + 10 = 45.

## Task 7 (level 3)

a. Jesse: 2 passes out of 5 = **2/5 = 40%**. Kim: 5 passes out of 5 = **5/5 = 100%**.

b. Jesse is faster (1.8 minutes on the last try against Kim's 3.8). Kim is more ready for the job, because every one of Kim's joints passed. Three of Jesse's five joints failed and would have to be redone.

c. Jesse: slow down and get five passes in a row before trying to go faster. Kim: keep the same routine and practise for speed. Kim's time is still dropping each try.

## Task 8 (level 3)

a. Any three sensible actions, for example:

- find the manufacturer's manual online or call the shop for it, and read the safety section
- check the saw: guard, blade, controls, and the right blade for the material
- confirm the dust control and respirator procedure for the job, and get the right PPE
- find out whether someone qualified can show you, even by phone or video call
- mark the cuts and set up the area so others stay clear

b. Example: "I've never run a cut-off saw and there's no manual here. Can I get the manual and a demo before I start, or can I do the layout and prep until the journeyperson gets here at noon?"

c. Stop and refuse if you believe the work would create an undue hazard to you or others, for example if you have no training on the saw, no guard, the wrong blade or no dust control. Report it to your supervisor. This is the right to refuse unsafe work named in Adaptability lesson 1. Communication lesson 4 shows the steps.

## Task 9 (level 2)

Open answer. Good steps are in order, start with a verb, include any safety step, and name the check at the end. The partner should be able to follow without asking questions.

## Task 10 (level 3)

a. The insertion mark showed a gap. The pipe was not all the way into the fitting.

b. She marked each pipe carefully and pushed it fully home before pressing.

c. Examples: check the mark at the fitting edge before pressing, not only after. Have a second person check the first ten joints on the job.

## Check yourself

1. Read, watch, try, check.
2. The safety section.
3. Instruction and demonstration of the worker's work task or work process.
4. The time for each try, and whether it passed the check.
5. A failed joint or cut has to be redone, so it does not save any time.
