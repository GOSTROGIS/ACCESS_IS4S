# Learn a new tool or method fast

Adaptability lesson 10 of 12. Total time: 90 minutes.

## Opening

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Hold up a tool most learners have not used, or show a photo of one. Ask: "Who has been handed a tool and told to figure it out? What did you do?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 10 of 12, that it links to Reading lesson 8 (Use an equipment manual) and Adaptability lesson 6 (learning strategies), and that it runs 90 minutes.

## Objective

**Time:** included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- use a read, watch, try and check loop to learn a new tool or method (Seek self-improvement)
- plan a short practice session and keep a practice log with times and pass rates (Plan and prioritize)
- explain what BC law says about orientation and training for young and new workers, and ask for training clearly (Demonstrate responsibility)

## Pre-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.2)

1. "When you get a new tool, what do you do first?"
2. "Is it okay to ask to be shown a tool before you use it on the job? Why?"

### Notes for the instructor

- The PX-20 tool, its manual and all its steps are fictional. Real press tools have their own manuals with their own steps. Tell learners to follow the manual for the tool in their hands.
- For Task 8, the cut-off saw makes concrete dust. Remind learners that their employer's procedures for dust control and respirators apply. Do not teach a saw procedure from this lesson.
- The right to refuse unsafe work is covered in Adaptability lesson 1 and in WorkSafeBC's Part 3 guidance. Link Task 8c to it.

## Post-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 4.1)

1. "Say the four steps of the loop."
2. "Five tries took 6, 5, 4, 4 and 3 minutes. Tries 1 and 2 failed. What is the pass rate?" (3 out of 5, 60%)
3. "Your new employer gives you a tool with no demo. What can you ask for, and why?"

## Summary

**Time:** 2 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- the read, watch, try and check loop
- quality first, then speed
- practice logs with times and pass rates
- what sections 4.3, 3.23 and 3.24 of the BC OHS Regulation say about tools and training
- good questions to ask during a demonstration
