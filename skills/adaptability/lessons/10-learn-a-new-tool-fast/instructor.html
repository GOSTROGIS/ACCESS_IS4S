Adaptability · Lesson 10 · Instructor

Learn a new tool or method fast

Use a read, watch, try and check loop to learn a new tool safely, track your practice, and ask for the training you are owed.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Seek self-improvement; Plan and prioritize; Demonstrate responsibility

Adaptability lesson 10 of 14. Total time: 90 minutes.

Opening

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Hold up a tool most learners have not used, or show a photo of one. Ask: "Who has been handed a tool and told to figure it out? What did you do?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 10 of 14, that it links to Reading lesson 8 (Use an equipment manual) and Adaptability lesson 6 (learning strategies), and that it runs 90 minutes.

Objective

Time: included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • use a read, watch, try and check loop to learn a new tool or method (Seek self-improvement)
  • plan a short practice session and keep a practice log with times and pass rates (Plan and prioritize)
  • explain what BC law says about orientation and training for young and new workers, and ask for training clearly (Demonstrate responsibility)

Pre-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.2)

  1. "When you get a new tool, what do you do first?"
  2. "Is it okay to ask to be shown a tool before you use it on the job? Why?"

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Opening question, objectives, session length. Share one experience of learning a new tool. A tool or photo 5 min
1.2 Ask the pre-assessment questions. Answer aloud. 5 min
2.1 Teach the learning loop. Stress "quality first, then speed". Complete Task 1. Learner page 8 min
2.2 Show the PX-20 contents page. Remind learners how to use a contents page (Reading 8). If you have a real manual, use it too. Complete Task 2. Learner page, a real tool manual 7 min
2.3 Teach sections 4.3, 3.23 and 3.24 of the OHS Regulation in plain words. Read the definitions of young and new worker. Complete Task 5 in pairs. Two pairs read their Task 5c line aloud. Learner page, WorkSafeBC Part 3 page on screen 12 min
2.4 Walk through Dana's worked example. Do the average and percent on the board with BEDMAS: brackets first. Follow along. Complete Task 3. Learner page, calculator 12 min
3.1 Demonstrate a simple shop tool or task using the loop. Invite questions from Task 4. Complete Task 4, then ask one question during the demo. A simple tool from the shop, scrap 10 min
3.2 Set Tasks 6 and 7. Plan the laser level session. Compare Jesse and Kim. Learner page, calculator 12 min
3.3 Set Task 8 in small groups. Take one answer from each group. Discuss and write Task 8. Learner page 8 min
3.4 Set Task 9 in pairs. Write and test the 5 steps. Learner page 4 min
4.1 Ask the post-assessment questions. Answer. 5 min
4.2 Summarize. Assign Task 10 as a short written reflection. Name the next tool they want to learn. 2 min

Total: 5 + 5 + 8 + 7 + 12 + 12 + 10 + 12 + 8 + 4 + 5 + 2 = 90 minutes.

Notes for the instructor

  • The PX-20 tool, its manual and all its steps are fictional. Real press tools have their own manuals with their own steps. Tell learners to follow the manual for the tool in their hands.
  • For Task 8, the cut-off saw makes concrete dust. Remind learners that their employer's procedures for dust control and respirators apply. Do not teach a saw procedure from this lesson.
  • The right to refuse unsafe work is covered in Adaptability lesson 1 and in WorkSafeBC's Part 3 guidance. Link Task 8c to it.

Post-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 4.1)

  1. "Say the four steps of the loop."
  2. "Five tries took 6, 5, 4, 4 and 3 minutes. Tries 1 and 2 failed. What is the pass rate?" (3 out of 5, 60%)
  3. "Your new employer gives you a tool with no demo. What can you ask for, and why?"

Summary

Time: 2 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • the read, watch, try and check loop
  • quality first, then speed
  • practice logs with times and pass rates
  • what sections 4.3, 3.23 and 3.24 of the BC OHS Regulation say about tools and training
  • good questions to ask during a demonstration
