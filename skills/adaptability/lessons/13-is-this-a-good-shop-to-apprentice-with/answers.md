## Task 4 (level 2)

**Yes, it is a red flag.** It is about area 4 (the shop's commitment: a way to report that works) and area 1 (whether you can speak up safely). The red flag rule says to treat the whole shop as a risk whatever the average says, and to deal with that area first. A good answer adds a next step: find out the shop's written reporting procedure, and keep notes of what was said.

## Task 5 (level 2)

**Open task.** Examples:

- Area 1: "If an apprentice makes a mistake, how does the crew handle it?"
- Area 2: "Who would I be working with day to day, and how often are hours reported?"
- Area 3: "Do you stock PPE in different sizes, or do I order my own?"
- Area 4: "Who would I talk to if I had a problem with someone on the crew?"
- Area 5: "What kind of work do first-year apprentices do here? How do pay raises work?"

A good answer has three questions, each about a different area, polite and specific.

## Task 6 (level 3)

**Open task.** A good answer names two specific signs and two steps for each. For example:

- Sign: my hours have not shown up in the Portal after three months. First: ask my journeyperson or the office. Next: a SkilledTradesBC Apprenticeship Advisor.
- Sign: I have only swept and carried material for six weeks. First: ask my journeyperson for one new task a week. Next: talk to the owner, using my program outline.
- Sign: jokes on the crew are aimed at me or someone else and keep going. First: report it through the shop's written procedure. Next: ACCESS staff, and WorkSafeBC's Prevention Information Line if the employer does not deal with it.

## Task 8 (level 3)

a. $1.50 × 40 = **$60 more a week.**

b. $1.50 × 2,000 = **$3,000 more a year.**

c. **Open answer.** A strong answer weighs the $3,000 against what Jesse risks at Stonefield: a score of 1.4, a red flag, no named journeyperson, hours reported "when we get around to it", and mostly sweeping and grinding. Alder Creek scores 2.8, with hours on a schedule and real trade work, which is what helps apprentices finish. Many learners will pick Alder Creek.
