## Task 8 (level 3)

**Open task.** A good list has at least six steps in a sensible order and names where each is done. For example:

1. Talk to an accountant about structure, tax and how much to set aside (accountant).
2. Choose a structure and a name (own decision, with advice).
3. Get the name approved and register, if not using your own legal name (BC Registries).
4. Check permits and licences for your trade and city (BizPaL, local government).
5. Get a business licence in each city you will work in, or an inter-municipal licence where one covers your area (local government).
6. Open a separate bank account and set up a way to track receipts and invoices.
7. Decide on WorkSafeBC Personal Coverage, and register if you will hire (WorkSafeBC).
8. Get the insurance your contracts ask for (insurance broker).
9. Track sales by quarter and register for GST before you go over $30,000, or by choice (CRA).
