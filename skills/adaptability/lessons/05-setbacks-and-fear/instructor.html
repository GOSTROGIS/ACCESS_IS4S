Adaptability · Lesson 05 · Instructor

Setbacks, fear of failure and trying again

Use fear as a signal, turn a failed attempt into a plan, and know how exam rewrites work for BC apprentices.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Persist and persevere; Seek self-improvement; Regulate your emotions when appropriate

Adaptability lesson 5 of 14. Total time: 75 minutes.

Opening

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. The original ACCESS growth mindset lesson opened with the instructor's own story, and that still works best. Share a time you failed something on the way to your trade (a test, a job, a first attempt at a skill) and what you did next. Ask: "Who here has failed something and gone back and done it?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 5 of 14, and that the session is 75 minutes.

Objective

Time: included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • tell healthy fear from unhealthy fear (Regulate your emotions when appropriate)
  • replace a label with a specific skill to practise (Seek self-improvement)
  • describe honestly what growth mindset research shows (Seek self-improvement)
  • turn a setback into a dated plan, including SkilledTradesBC rewrite rules (Persist and persevere)
  • write a fear action plan (Persist and persevere)

Pre-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 1.2)

  1. "What words come to mind when you hear FEAR?" (the warm-up from the ACCESS "Coping With Fear" lesson)
  2. "If you failed a trade exam, what would you do the next day?"

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Opening story, objectives, session length. Answer the opening question. 5 min
1.2 Write FEAR on the flip chart and record words. Ask question 2. Call out words. Answer question 2. Flip chart 5 min
2.1 Teach healthy and unhealthy fear. Link the trench example to the right to refuse unsafe work. Complete Task 2. Learner page 10 min
2.2 From the ACCESS personality traits lecture: one trait or label does not define a person. Model turning a label into a specific skill. Complete Task 1. Share one rewrite. Learner page 8 min
2.3 Explain the growth mindset research honestly: small or no effects from messages alone, a small benefit for lower-achieving students in one large study, and stronger effects from practice, feedback and support. Discuss: "What helps you more, a pep talk or a plan?" Learner page 8 min
3.1 Teach the five setback steps. Walk through Priya's example, including the date count from March 3 to April 2. Complete Task 3. Learner page, calendar 12 min
3.2 Explain the SkilledTradesBC rewrite table. Show skilledtradesbc.ca/exam-rewrites on screen if available. Complete Task 5. Learner page, projector 7 min
3.3 Introduce the five-step fear action plan from the ACCESS lesson. Model one with a volunteer or your own example. Offer the interview example for learners who prefer not to use a personal fear. Complete Task 4. Choose a buddy if they wish. Learner page 12 min
4.1 Ask the post-assessment questions. Answer. 5 min
4.2 Summarize. Name the first small step in their fear action plan. 3 min

Total: 5 + 5 + 10 + 8 + 8 + 12 + 7 + 12 + 5 + 3 = 75 minutes.

The learner page also has a staircase figure of the five steps and a second worked example: a welding apprentice who missed by 4 percent and builds a 4-week plan around the 30-day rewrite wait. Use it with Task 3. These fit inside the existing step times.

Notes for the instructor

  • The original ACCESS growth mindset lesson used a film clip with heavy profanity and links that may no longer work. This lesson leaves out film clips.
  • Fear of failure can be tied to past experiences in school, including residential and day school experiences in some families. Keep sharing optional and avoid asking why someone is afraid.
  • If a learner's fear is stopping them from daily life (going out, working, sleeping), speak with them privately and refer them to program support staff and the supports in lesson 8.

Post-assessment

Time: 5 minutes (step 4.1)

  1. "Turn this label into something specific: 'I'm no good at code books.'"
  2. "An apprentice wrote a level exam on May 10 and scored 66 percent. What is the earliest date for the rewrite?" (June 9)
  3. "Is a growth mindset enough on its own? What else do you need?"

Summary

Time: 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • healthy and unhealthy fear
  • swapping labels for specific skills
  • what the research says about growth mindset
  • five steps after a setback
  • SkilledTradesBC exam rewrite rules
  • a five-step fear action plan
