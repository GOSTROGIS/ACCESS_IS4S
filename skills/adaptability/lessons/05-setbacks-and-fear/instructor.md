# Setbacks, fear of failure and trying again

Adaptability lesson 5 of 8. Total time: 75 minutes.

## Opening

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. The original ACCESS growth mindset lesson opened with the instructor's own story, and that still works best. Share a time you failed something on the way to your trade (a test, a job, a first attempt at a skill) and what you did next. Ask: "Who here has failed something and gone back and done it?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 5 of 8, and that the session is 75 minutes.

## Objective

**Time:** included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- tell healthy fear from unhealthy fear (Regulate your emotions when appropriate)
- replace a label with a specific skill to practise (Seek self-improvement)
- describe honestly what growth mindset research shows (Seek self-improvement)
- turn a setback into a dated plan, including SkilledTradesBC rewrite rules (Persist and persevere)
- write a fear action plan (Persist and persevere)

## Pre-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.2)

1. "What words come to mind when you hear FEAR?" (the warm-up from the ACCESS "Coping With Fear" lesson)
2. "If you failed a trade exam, what would you do the next day?"

### Notes for the instructor

- The original ACCESS growth mindset lesson used a film clip with heavy profanity and links that may no longer work. This lesson leaves out film clips.
- Fear of failure can be tied to past experiences in school, including residential and day school experiences in some families. Keep sharing optional and avoid asking why someone is afraid.
- If a learner's fear is stopping them from daily life (going out, working, sleeping), speak with them privately and refer them to program support staff and the supports in lesson 8.

## Post-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 4.1)

1. "Turn this label into something specific: 'I'm no good at code books.'"
2. "An apprentice wrote a level exam on May 10 and scored 66 percent. What is the earliest date for the rewrite?" (June 9)
3. "Is a growth mindset enough on its own? What else do you need?"

## Summary

**Time:** 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- healthy and unhealthy fear
- swapping labels for specific skills
- what the research says about growth mindset
- five steps after a setback
- SkilledTradesBC exam rewrite rules
- a five-step fear action plan
