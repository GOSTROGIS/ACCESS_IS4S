## Task 2 (level 1)

Open answer. Good practice questions:

- make the learner pull the answer from memory (not yes/no, not copied straight from a heading)
- have one clear answer that can be checked
- cover different parts of the topic

Weak: "What is PPE?" Better: "Name three pieces of PPE you need for overhead drilling into concrete, and say what each one protects."

## Task 3 (level 2)

a. 25 × 10 = **250 minutes**. 250 ÷ 60 = 4 remainder 10, so **4 hours 10 minutes**.

b. Open answer. Good plans spread the 10 days across both weeks, with no long gaps, and include the last day or two before the exam. Example: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat in week 1 and Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat in week 2.

c. Open answer. Example: 10 minutes answering mixed practice questions from memory, 5 minutes checking answers, 10 minutes reworking the questions that were wrong. A plan that is mostly rereading should be revised.

## Task 4 (level 2)

**Answer:** 1066.8 mm.

Steps and reasons:

1. Change feet to inches, because the conversion factor is per inch: 3 ft × 12 = 36 in.
2. Add the extra inches: 36 + 6 = 42 in.
3. Multiply by 25.4, because 1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm: 42 × 25.4 = 1066.8 mm.

The partner checks both the answer and that each "why" was said.

## Task 5 (level 2)

Open answer. A good reply covers some of these points:

- Research does not support learning only in your preferred style.
- What you learn should decide how you learn it. Code rules are written down, so you need to read them.
- The certification exam tests knowledge from all levels, including written material.
- You can still use hands-on practice, and add practice questions and spaced review for the code book.

The reply should be respectful, not "you're wrong".
