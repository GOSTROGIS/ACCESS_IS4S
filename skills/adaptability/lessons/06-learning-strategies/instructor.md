# Learning strategies that work

Adaptability lesson 6 of 14. Total time: 75 minutes.

## Opening

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Tell them about something you learned the hard way in your trade, and the method that finally made it stick. Ask: "How do you usually study for a test?" Explain that this is Adaptability lesson 6 of 14, and that the session is 75 minutes.

## Objective

**Time:** included in step 1.1

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- explain why matching teaching to learning styles is not supported by research (Seek self-improvement)
- name study methods with strong and weak evidence (Seek self-improvement)
- write practice questions and use them to quiz themselves (Seek self-improvement)
- build a spaced study plan for an exam (Plan and prioritize)

## Pre-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 1.2)

1. "Are you a visual, auditory or hands-on learner? How do you know?"
2. "What study method do you use most?"

Tally answers on the board. Do not correct them yet.

### Notes for the instructor

- Learners may feel that being told learning styles are not real dismisses how they learn. Acknowledge that preferences are real, and that many learners were labelled "hands-on" in school in ways that were not always fair. The point is that they can learn in many ways, not that their preferences are wrong.
- The old "Learning styles inventory ik" appears to reproduce a published inventory with no source given. It is not used here.
- Teaching through demonstration, practice, discussion and story together is good trades teaching. It works because it suits the content and gives several ways in, not because it matches each learner's "type".

## Post-assessment

**Time:** 5 minutes (step 4.1)

1. "A friend says she is a visual learner, so she only watches videos. What would you tell her?"
2. "You have 4 hours to study before an exam in 8 days. How would you split it?"
3. "Which is better after reading a chapter: reading it again, or closing the book and writing what you remember? Why?"

## Summary

**Time:** 3 minutes (step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- what research shows about learning styles
- study methods with strong, moderate and weak evidence
- writing and using practice questions
- building a spaced study plan
- explaining steps out loud
