# Make and give a short presentation

ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success · Digital · Lesson plan

| Session | Total length |
|---|---|
| Digital 10 | 75 min |

## Opening (3 min)

Welcome learners to the last Digital session in this set. Hook: ask who has sat through a toolbox talk where someone read a wall of slides word for word. Then ask what the best safety talk they ever heard looked like. Usually: short, a real example, and someone who knew the tool. Today each learner builds four slides about a tool or part from their own trade and talks about it for two minutes.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- build a short PowerPoint from a theme with new slides, notes and a source slide (Use common digital tools to complete tasks: use software for a purpose, for example PowerPoint)
- check a presentation with the Accessibility Checker and add alt text (Use common digital tools to complete tasks: use digital tools to enhance accessibility for yourself and others)
- name where their facts came from (Use digital information: evaluate the relevance and reliability of digital information)
- learn a new feature from the vendor's help pages (Update and upgrade digital skills)
- present with the slide show controls (Use common digital tools to complete tasks)

Complexity: levels 1 and 2.

## Pre-assessment (2 min)

1. Have you ever made or given a presentation? What tool did you use?
2. What makes a slide hard to read from the back of a room?

## Learning tasks (59 min)

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Go through "A short presentation that works". Stress own photos only and naming sources. | 1.1 Complete Task 1. | Learner page | 5 min |
| 1.2 | Model the worked example: **File** > **New**, theme, **Create**, **New Slide** arrow, layouts, the thumbnail pane. | 1.2 Watch, then start Task 2. | Projector, PowerPoint | 7 min |
| 2.1 | Circulate. Push learners to cut words from slides into notes. | 2.1 Finish Task 2 and complete Task 3. | PowerPoint, learners' own phone photos if they have them | 15 min |
| 2.2 | Demonstrate **Review** > **Check Accessibility** and adding alt text from the recommended action. | 2.2 Complete Task 4. | PowerPoint | 7 min |
| 2.3 | Show a `site:support.microsoft.com` search for a feature. | 2.3 Complete Task 5. | Browser | 5 min |
| 3.1 | Demonstrate F5, N, P, Esc and the **Use Presenter View** checkbox. | 3.1 Complete Task 6 and practise once out loud. | PowerPoint, timer | 5 min |
| 3.2 | Split the class into groups of four. Keep time. | 3.2 Complete Task 7. Each learner presents for up to 2 minutes and gives feedback to one other. | Projector or laptops | 15 min |

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. Show me your source slide. How do you know that source is reliable?
2. If you used a picture, show me its alt text. If you used no pictures, explain when alt text would be needed.
3. Which feature did you learn from the help pages, and did it work the first time?

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- one idea per slide, details in the notes, good contrast
- using your own pictures and naming your sources
- building slides from a theme, adding, moving and deleting slides
- speaker notes and Presenter View
- the Accessibility Checker and alt text
- learning a new feature from the vendor's help pages
- giving a two-minute talk with feedback

Time check: 3 + 2 + 2 + 59 + 5 + 4 = 75 min.

## Notes for the instructor

- The old ESAF files for this topic were a picture-only PowerPoint demo and two lists of YouTube links to "how it's made" videos about tools, metals and car parts. The links are not reused here because they have not been checked. The idea is kept: learners present how a tool or part works, using a source they can name.
- If a learner wants to talk about a cultural object, tool or practice, they present only what they have the right to share and name the person, family or Nation it comes from. Offer a trade topic as the default.
- Learners who are nervous about presenting can present to the instructor only, or record a voice-over. Record what they did.
- This lesson links back to Lesson 6 (sharing a PowerPoint in Teams) and forward to the [AI literacy sequence](../../ai-literacy/learner.md), where learners use AI to prepare documents and then check them against the facts.

## Evidence to check

Reopen the final presentation and inspect slide text, notes and source details. Save changes after the new-feature task. Record the timed presentation and specific peer feedback.
