Email, calendar invites and video calls

Write a clear work email with an attachment, put a meeting in your calendar, answer an invitation and join a Teams video call ready to talk.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Use online tools and platforms; Apply safe and responsible practices online; Use digital devices including computers, tablets, smart phones, and other handheld devices

ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success · Digital · Lesson plan

Session Total length
Digital 6 75 min

Opening (3 min)

Welcome learners. Hook: read out two real-style subject lines an employer might receive, hey and Application: Pipe trades helper, Sam Joe. Ask which one gets opened first. Many first contacts with an employer now happen by email, calendar invite and video call before anyone meets in person. Today learners practise all three in a safe practice account.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • write and send a clear work email with a subject, attachment and signature (Use online tools and platforms: use online communication platforms, for example emails)
  • choose between Reply and Reply all, and use appropriate language (Apply safe and responsible practices online: use appropriate language and behaviour online)
  • create a calendar event and answer an invitation (Use online tools and platforms)
  • join a Teams meeting and use mic, camera, blur, raise hand, chat and share (Use online tools and platforms, for example Zoom. Use digital devices)

Complexity: levels 1 and 2.

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. What email address would you put on a job application?
  2. Have you joined a video call before? What went wrong or right?

Learning tasks (57 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Explain the professional address and the parts of an email using the table. 1.1 Complete Task 1. Share one rewritten subject line. Learner page 7 min
1.2 Demonstrate New > Mail, To, subject, Insert > Attach file, Send. Show the signature setting. 1.2 Complete Task 2. Practice Outlook accounts, Lesson 4 PDF 12 min
1.3 Send the "Reply to me only" class email. Explain Reply versus Reply all. 1.3 Complete Task 3. Instructor practice account 5 min
2.1 Demonstrate Calendar > New event, title, time, location, Teams meeting toggle. 2.1 Complete Task 4. Outlook calendar 10 min
2.2 Send each learner an invitation to the practice meeting. 2.2 Complete Task 5. Instructor calendar 5 min
3.1 Start the practice Teams meeting. Show the pre-join screen, Effects and avatars, blur and mic check on the projector. 3.1 Complete the pre-join part of Task 6 and join. Teams, webcams or headsets 8 min
3.2 Run the meeting: call each learner to unmute, raise hand, chat, and share. Model leaving. 3.2 Complete the rest of Task 6, and Task 7. Teams 10 min

Post-assessment (6 min)

  1. Show me your sent Task 2 email. Read me the subject line and say who will read it.
  2. You get a meeting invite for a time you might be able to make. Which answer do you choose?
  3. You are about to share your screen in an interview. Name two things you check first.

Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

  • a professional email address for job searching
  • To, Cc, Bcc, subject, body, attachment and signature
  • Reply and Reply all
  • creating calendar events and answering invitations
  • joining a Teams meeting and the main meeting controls
  • video call habits: mute, check what is behind you, close private windows before sharing

Time check: 3 + 2 + 3 + 57 + 6 + 4 = 75 min.

Notes for the instructor

  • Agree the exact Monday date and time zone before Task 4. Check the saved event against both, not just its displayed start time.
  • Set up practice accounts or use ACCESS training accounts. Learners should not send practice emails from personal accounts to real employers, and should not have to share personal email addresses with the class.
  • Scheduling a Teams meeting from Outlook works with work or school Microsoft 365 accounts. Personal Outlook.com, Gmail and other POP or IMAP accounts cannot use the Outlook Teams add-in to create Teams meetings. Learners on personal accounts can still join the practice meeting by link.
  • If Teams is not available, the same skills apply in Zoom or Google Meet. Use the vendor's own help page for button names.
  • Learners without a webcam or with privacy concerns can keep the camera off. Record that they joined, unmuted, used chat and raised a hand.
  • The old ESAF CP Rail exercise pointed out that employers reply only to the address you applied from and that ESAF staff would help learners set up a business email. Offer the same help.

Evidence to check

Observe the sent practice email and open its attachment. Check the calendar date and zone, then observe mic, chat and stop-sharing actions without collecting private account screens.
