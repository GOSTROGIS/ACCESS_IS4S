# Email, calendar invites and video calls

Steps checked in the new Outlook for Windows and Microsoft Teams on Windows 11, 2026-11-06. Classic Outlook, Gmail and phone apps have the same parts with different buttons. Scheduling a Teams meeting from Outlook needs a work or school Microsoft 365 account.

## Why this matters on the job

Employers, unions and training programs in BC send job offers, start dates, safety orientations and interview times by email and calendar invite. Many interviews and apprenticeship check-ins now happen on video. A clear email and a smooth video call tell an employer you are organized before you ever pick up a tool.

## Key ideas

### Your email address

For job applications, use an address with your name in it, such as `jordan.lee@...`. ACCESS has advised this since its first job-search lessons: employers often reply only to the address you applied from, and a nickname address can cost you the call.

### The parts of an email

| Part | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| To | The person who needs to act or answer |
| Cc | People who should see it but do not need to act |
| Bcc | People who get a copy without others seeing their address |
| Subject | A short, specific line: what and when, such as `Time sheet for week of Nov 2, Jordan Lee` |
| Body | Greeting, the point in the first line, details, what you need, your name |
| Attachment | A file, such as a PDF of your time sheet |
| Signature | Your name, trade or program, and phone number |

**Reply** answers only the sender. **Reply all** answers everyone on the message. Use Reply all only when every person on the list needs your answer.

### Write and send in the new Outlook

1. Select **New** > **Mail**, or press Ctrl + N.
2. Type the address in **To** (and **Cc** or **Bcc** if needed).
3. Type a subject in **Add a subject**.
4. Write the message.
5. To attach a file, select **Insert** > **Attach file**. If you mention an attachment and forget it, Outlook may warn you before sending.
6. Select **Send**.

To set up a signature: **Settings** > **Accounts** > **Signatures** > **Add signature**, type it, **Save**.

### Calendar events and invitations

To put a meeting in your calendar in the new Outlook:

1. Go to **Calendar** and select **New event**.
2. Add a title.
3. Select **Invite attendees** to add people (or leave it empty for a personal reminder).
4. Set the date, start and end time.
5. Add a location. Turn on the **Teams meeting** toggle if it will be a video call (work or school accounts).
6. Select **Send** (or **Save** if no one is invited).

When someone invites you, the invitation offers **Accept**, **Tentative** and **Decline**. Answer it. If you just ignore it, the organizer cannot tell whether you are coming.

### Joining a Teams call

- Open the meeting from your Teams or Outlook calendar and select **Join**, or select the join link in the invitation email.
- If you do not have a Teams account and the organizer allows it, you can type your name and join.
- On the screen before you join, check your camera and microphone. Select **Effects and avatars** to blur your background (**Standard blur** or **Portrait blur**). Blur may not hide everything behind you, so check what the camera can see.

During the meeting, the controls bar has:

| Control | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Mic** | Mute and unmute yourself |
| **Camera** | Turn your video on and off |
| **Raise** (or **React** > **Raise hand**) | Show you want to speak without interrupting |
| **Chat** | Type a question or share a link |
| **Share** | Show your screen, a window or a PowerPoint file |
| **Leave** | Leave the meeting |

Good habits: mute when you are not talking, join a few minutes early, check what the camera shows behind you, and mute smart speakers, TV or music nearby.

### Worked example

Jordan is a fictional ACCESS learner with a carpentry interview on Thursday at 10:00 a.m. The employer's email asks Jordan to confirm and bring a résumé.

Jordan replies (Reply, not Reply all):

> Subject: RE: Interview Thursday 10:00 a.m., Jordan Lee
>
> Hello Ms. Grant,
>
> Thank you for the invitation. I confirm I will attend the interview on Thursday at 10:00 a.m. My résumé is attached as a PDF.
>
> Jordan Lee
> Carpentry Foundation learner, ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success
> 604-555-0137

Then Jordan accepts the calendar invite, so the interview shows in the calendar with a reminder.

## Practice

Use the practice account and addresses your instructor gives you. Send messages only to your instructor or your own address. Do not use real employer addresses.

**Task 1** (level 1) Rewrite each subject line so the reader knows what the email is about.

- `hi` ______________________________________________
- `question` ______________________________________________
- `stuff for monday` ______________________________________________

**Task 2** (level 2) Write an email to your instructor's practice address. Your fictional foreman asked for your time sheet for this week. Use a clear subject, a greeting, one or two sentences, and your name. Attach a PDF from Lesson 4 as if it were your time sheet. Send it.

**Task 3** (level 1) Your instructor sends one email to the whole class asking: "Who can stay late Friday for cleanup? Reply to me only." Do you use Reply or Reply all? Reply with your answer.

**Task 4** (level 2) Put this in your calendar: `Site safety orientation`, next Monday 7:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. in the training site's time zone (agree the exact date and zone with your instructor), location `Riverbend Builders yard (fictional)`. Add a reminder note in the body: `Bring steel-toe boots and hard hat`.

**Task 5** (level 1) Your instructor will send you a calendar invitation. Answer it with **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**, and say why you chose that one.

**Task 6** (level 2) Join the practice Teams meeting from the invitation. Before you join: blur your background and check your mic. In the meeting: unmute, say your name, mute again, raise your hand, type `Ready` in the chat, then lower your hand.

**Task 7** (level 2) In the practice meeting, share one window (your Lesson 4 PDF) for ten seconds, then stop sharing. Tell the group one thing to close or hide before sharing your screen at work.

## Check yourself

1. What is the difference between Cc and Bcc?
2. When should you use Reply all?
3. Why should you answer a calendar invitation instead of ignoring it?
4. What does background blur not guarantee?
5. Which control do you use to ask to speak without interrupting?
