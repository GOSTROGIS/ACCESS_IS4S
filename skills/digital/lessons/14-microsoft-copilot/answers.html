Using Microsoft Copilot

Tell the personal Copilot app apart from Copilot in a work account for Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams, find its privacy settings, and use it for three trades tasks, checking each answer.

Task 1 (level 1)

Question Answer

Web address copilot.microsoft.com (personal). Work: Copilot in a browser with your work account, or in Teams, Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app.
Apps Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android
Account Personal: Microsoft account (Apple ID or Google account also accepted on web, phone and Mac). Work: work or school (Entra) account.

Check that the learner used the company's own pages, not a blog or a video. Plan names and features change, so accept a newer answer if it comes from the official page and the learner can show where.

Task 2 (level 1)

  • (a) Personal app: the controls page checked for this lesson lists Memory, Shared experiences, Chat history, Web search and Personalized advertising, but no training switch. Accept what the learner finds in their own Settings if it is named clearly. A strong answer notes that Microsoft says work-account Copilot Chat prompts and responses are not used to train foundation models.
  • (b) Settings > Personalization > Saved memories (switch), Manage > Delete all memories. One chat: Chats list > ...More > Delete.

Task 3 (level 2)

Answer: 10 posts, 18 rails, 20 bags of concrete mix.

Steps:

  • Sections: 21.6 ÷ 2.4 = 9 sections.
  • Posts: one more post than sections, because there is a post at each end. Posts = 21.6 ÷ 2.4 + 1. By BEDMAS, divide first: 9 + 1 = 10 posts.
  • Rails: 9 sections × 2 rails = 18 rails, each 2.4 m.
  • Concrete: 10 posts × 2 bags = 20 bags.

The common mistake, by people and by chatbots, is 9 posts (forgetting the end post). If Microsoft Copilot said 9 posts, the learner should catch it. A strong answer also notes that waste and gate hardware are not included and asks the journeyperson about extra.

Task 4 (level 2)

Correct order from section 11.2:

  1. Guardrails, or another similar means of fall restraint, when practicable (11.2(2)).
  2. If that is not practicable, another fall restraint system (11.2(3)).
  3. If that is not practicable, a fall arrest system or a rope access system that meets Part 34 (11.2(4)).
  4. If that is not practicable, or would create a greater hazard, work procedures acceptable to WorkSafeBC that minimize the risk of injury from a fall (11.2(5)).

A good response names one specific thing Microsoft Copilot got right or wrong, for example: it put the steps in the right order, it left out rope access, it mixed up fall restraint and fall arrest, or it gave no section numbers. Fall restraint stops a worker from falling. Fall arrest stops a fall after it starts (definitions in section 11.1).

Task 5 (level 2)

Open task. A good message:

  • says the time (7:10 a.m.), the site (Alder Street) and the numbers (40 studs came, the slip says 60)
  • does not blame the driver or the yard
  • asks one clear question, such as whether to call the supplier or keep working with what is there
  • is short enough to read on a phone
  • has no private information

Example: "Hi Dale, the lumber for Alder Street came at 7:10. The slip says 60 studs but only 40 came. Do you want me to call the yard, or keep going with what we have? Thanks, Sam"

If Microsoft Copilot added facts that were not in the scenario (a driver's name, a reason for the shortage, a new delivery time), the learner should take them out.

Task 6 (level 1)

Open task. A complete card names the claim (for example "9 posts" or the order of the fall protection options), the place checked (own math, or section 11.2 on worksafebc.com), whether it matched, and what the learner kept or changed. Accept any task where the learner shows real checking.

Check yourself

  1. Microsoft. Official help: support.microsoft.com.
  2. Personal: a Microsoft account (or Apple ID or Google account on web, phone and Mac), age 13 or older depending on country. Work: a work or school (Entra) account from the employer.
  3. Any two of: chat and write, search the web, Copilot Voice, Copilot Vision or image upload, create images.
  4. Personal: Settings, Personalization, with memory under Saved memories. At work, the employer's IT controls most settings.
  5. Copilot predicts likely words and can be wrong or incomplete while sounding sure. A wrong quantity or rule costs money or causes injury, so you check it against your own math and the official source.
