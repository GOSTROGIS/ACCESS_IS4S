Using Google Gemini

Tell the Gemini app apart from Gemini in Gmail and Docs, find its activity settings, and use it for a materials list, a code explanation and a message to your foreman, checking each answer.

Task 1 (level 1)

Question Answer
Who makes it Google
Web address gemini.google.com
Apps Gemini mobile app for Android, iPhone and iPad
Account Some features without signing in. Personal Google Account, work Google Account (qualifying Workspace, 18+), or school account.
Minimum age 13 or the applicable age in your country (with a Google AI plan), 18 for a work account
Plan names No paid plan needed to start. Paid: Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra. Work: Google Workspace editions.

Check that the learner used the company's own pages, not a blog or a video. Plan names and features change, so accept a newer answer if it comes from the official page and the learner can show where.

Task 2 (level 1)

  • (a) Settings & help > Activity, the Keep activity setting (or myactivity.google.com/product/gemini). On by default for users 18 and over.
  • (b) Delete activity on the same Activity page. Auto-delete is set to 18 months by default and can be changed there.

Task 3 (level 2)

Answer: 10 posts, 18 rails, 20 bags of concrete mix.

Steps:

  • Sections: 21.6 ÷ 2.4 = 9 sections.
  • Posts: one more post than sections, because there is a post at each end. Posts = 21.6 ÷ 2.4 + 1. By BEDMAS, divide first: 9 + 1 = 10 posts.
  • Rails: 9 sections × 2 rails = 18 rails, each 2.4 m.
  • Concrete: 10 posts × 2 bags = 20 bags.

The common mistake, by people and by chatbots, is 9 posts (forgetting the end post). If Gemini said 9 posts, the learner should catch it. A strong answer also notes that waste and gate hardware are not included and asks the journeyperson about extra.

Task 4 (level 2)

Correct order from section 11.2:

  1. Guardrails, or another similar means of fall restraint, when practicable (11.2(2)).
  2. If that is not practicable, another fall restraint system (11.2(3)).
  3. If that is not practicable, a fall arrest system or a rope access system that meets Part 34 (11.2(4)).
  4. If that is not practicable, or would create a greater hazard, work procedures acceptable to WorkSafeBC that minimize the risk of injury from a fall (11.2(5)).

A good response names one specific thing Gemini got right or wrong, for example: it put the steps in the right order, it left out rope access, it mixed up fall restraint and fall arrest, or it gave no section numbers. Fall restraint stops a worker from falling. Fall arrest stops a fall after it starts (definitions in section 11.1).

Task 5 (level 2)

Open task. A good message:

  • says the time (7:10 a.m.), the site (Alder Street) and the numbers (40 studs came, the slip says 60)
  • does not blame the driver or the yard
  • asks one clear question, such as whether to call the supplier or keep working with what is there
  • is short enough to read on a phone
  • has no private information

Example: "Hi Dale, the lumber for Alder Street came at 7:10. The slip says 60 studs but only 40 came. Do you want me to call the yard, or keep going with what we have? Thanks, [your practice name]"

If Gemini added facts that were not in the scenario (a driver's name, a reason for the shortage, a new delivery time), the learner should take them out.

Task 6 (level 1)

Open task. A complete card names the claim (for example "9 posts" or the order of the fall protection options), the place checked (own math, or section 11.2 on worksafebc.com), whether it matched, and what the learner kept or changed. Accept any task where the learner shows real checking.

Check yourself

  1. Google. Official help: support.google.com/gemini.
  2. A Google Account (personal, work or school), though some features work without signing in. Age 13 or the applicable age in your country for Google AI plans, 18 for a work account.
  3. Any two of: upload files, create files, generate images, Gemini Live voice, share camera or screen, Sources links, double-check.
  4. Settings & help > Activity, the Keep activity setting.
  5. Gemini predicts likely words and can be wrong or incomplete while sounding sure. A wrong quantity or rule costs money or causes injury, so you check it against your own math and the official source.
