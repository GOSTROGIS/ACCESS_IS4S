# Build a materials list in Excel

ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success · Digital · Lesson plan

| Session | Total length |
|---|---|
| Digital 5 | 90 min |

## Opening (3 min)

Welcome learners. Hook: "You price a bathroom reno by hand. The client adds a second sink. How much of your math do you redo?" With a spreadsheet, you change one number and the totals redo themselves. This session replaces the three old ESAF Excel sessions with one hands-on job: a materials list that updates itself.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- name a cell by its reference and read the formula bar (Use common digital tools to complete tasks: use software for a purpose, for example Excel)
- write formulas with `+ - * /` and predict their results with BEDMAS (Use common digital tools to complete tasks)
- use SUM, AutoSum, AVERAGE and the fill handle (Use common digital tools to complete tasks)
- format money and save versions of a workbook (Use digital information: store and organize digital information in a logical way)

Complexity: levels 1 and 2.

## Pre-assessment (5 min)

1. On paper, add these line totals without a calculator: 230.40 + 75.00 + 87.00 + 540.00.
2. What is 5 + 2 × 3? Explain the order you used.

(Answers: 932.40. 11, multiplication before addition, BEDMAS.)

## Learning tasks (70 min)

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Open Excel on the projector. Point to a cell, a column letter, a row number, the formula bar and the sheet tabs. Click B3 and read its reference aloud. | 1.1 Complete Task 1. | Projector, Excel | 8 min |
| 1.2 | Show how Tab and Enter move between cells while typing. Warn about fractions turning into dates. | 1.2 Complete Task 2 and save `v1`. | Excel, learner page | 10 min |
| 2.1 | Model the worked example. Type `=B2*D2`, press Enter, show the formula bar. Drag the fill handle to E5 and show `=B3*D3` in E3. | 2.1 Complete Task 3. | Excel | 12 min |
| 2.2 | Demonstrate **Home** > **AutoSum** in E6 and show the range Excel picked. Show that SUM can also be typed. | 2.2 Complete Task 4. | Excel | 8 min |
| 2.3 | Demonstrate `=AVERAGE(D2:D5)` and explain average as total divided by count. | 2.3 Complete Task 5. | Excel | 5 min |
| 2.4 | Write BEDMAS on the board. Work `=5+2*3` and `=(5+2)*3` together. | 2.4 Complete Task 6. Predict first, then check. | Whiteboard, Excel | 10 min |
| 3.1 | Demonstrate **Accounting Number Format** and Ctrl + 1 > **Currency**. Show `#####` and widen the column. | 3.1 Complete Task 7. | Excel | 7 min |
| 3.2 | Ask the class to change B2 to 52 at the same time and watch E2, E6 and E8. | 3.2 Complete Task 8 and save `v2`. | Excel | 10 min |

## Post-assessment (6 min)

1. Click E4 in your workbook. Read me the formula in the formula bar and explain it.
2. Without Excel, what does `=10+20/5` give? What would you change to make it give 6?
3. Why did D7 not change in Task 8 when E6 did?

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- cells, columns, rows, cell references, the formula bar and sheet tabs
- formulas starting with `=` and the four operators
- BEDMAS in Excel, and brackets to control the order
- SUM, AutoSum, AVERAGE and the fill handle
- money formatting and `#####`
- saving `v1` and `v2` and letting formulas update totals

Time check: 3 + 2 + 5 + 70 + 6 + 4 = 90 min.

## Notes for the instructor

- The old Excel part 2 plan listed the PRODUCT function and the "formula palette". This lesson uses `*` for multiplying because it is the form learners will see in trade estimates and the numeracy lessons.
- Prices are practice numbers. If a learner asks for real prices, send them to a supplier's website and treat it as a Lesson 7 source-checking exercise.
- For a level 3 extension, ask learners to add a waste allowance column that multiplies each quantity by 1.1 and to explain why the result for studs is not a whole number of studs.
- The [numeracy chapter](../../../numeracy/learner.md) covers BEDMAS in more depth.
