Find and measure a site on a map

Find a job site by address and by coordinates, measure a distance, look up property and zoning information in VanMap, and know what an online map cannot tell you before you dig.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Use online tools and platforms; Use digital information

ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success · Digital · Lesson plan

Session Total length
Digital 9 60 min

Opening (3 min)

Welcome learners. Hook: "Your first day on a new crew. The text from the foreman says only 49.2610, -123.1139, 6:30. Where do you go?" Today learners turn coordinates into a place, measure on a map, look up a Vancouver property in VanMap, and learn the one thing online maps can never tell you: what is buried under the ground.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

  • read and use latitude and longitude in Google Maps (Use online tools and platforms. Use digital information: navigate digital content)
  • measure a straight-line distance and compare it with a route distance (Use online tools and platforms)
  • open the VanMap Viewer, agree to the Terms of Use, read a Property Viewer Report, turn on a layer and use the Measurement tool (Use online tools and platforms)
  • explain the limits of online maps and when BC 1 Call must be contacted (Use digital information: evaluate the relevance and reliability of digital information)

Complexity: level 2, with Tasks 1 and 5 at level 1.

Pre-assessment (2 min)

  1. What app do you use to find an address you have never been to?
  2. Can a map on your phone tell you where a gas line is buried?

Learning tasks (46 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Explain latitude and longitude, latitude first, negative longitude in BC. Model the worked example in Google Maps. 1.1 Watch. Say which number is latitude. Projector, Google Maps 7 min
1.2 Show right-click to read coordinates. 1.2 Complete Task 1. Browser 5 min
1.3 Demonstrate Measure distance and walking directions. 1.3 Complete Task 2. Browser 7 min
2.1 Open maps.vancouver.ca and the VanMap Viewer. Read the Terms of Use agreement aloud before agreeing. Tour the tool list. 2.1 Open the VanMap Viewer and agree to the terms. Browser 6 min
2.2 Demonstrate searching an address and opening the Property Viewer Report. 2.2 Complete Task 3. VanMap Viewer 8 min
2.3 Demonstrate Measurement, Map Layers and Legend. Read the City's note that measurements are approximate. 2.3 Complete Tasks 4 and 5. VanMap Viewer 8 min
3.1 Show bc1c.ca. Explain that a locate is free and required before any digging, and that members respond within 3 business days. 3.1 Complete Task 6. bc1c.ca 5 min

Post-assessment (4 min)

  1. Read me these coordinates and say which is latitude: 49.2610, -123.1139.
  2. A co-worker measured 41.6 m in VanMap and wants to order exactly 41.6 m of fence. What do you say?
  3. What is the phone number or website for BC 1 Call?

Summary (3 min)

During this session we covered:

  • latitude and longitude in decimal degrees
  • finding coordinates and going to coordinates in Google Maps
  • measuring straight-line distance and why it differs from a route
  • the VanMap Viewer: Terms of Use, Property Viewer Report, Map Layers, Legend, Measurement
  • the limits of online map data
  • BC 1 Call locates before digging

Time check: 3 + 2 + 2 + 46 + 4 + 3 = 60 min.

Notes for the instructor

  • The old ESAF VanMap lesson was screenshots of the legacy VanMap. The City has replaced it twice since. The old public VanMap Viewer was scheduled for retirement in the third quarter of 2026, and the new one is at vanmapviewer.vancouver.ca. Check both addresses before class.
  • VanMap covers the City of Vancouver only. For a learner group from Surrey, Burnaby or a First Nation community, add that local government's own map site after checking its address.
  • Do not ask learners to look up their own home address on screen in front of the class.
  • Before class, take your own screenshots of the VanMap Viewer tool bar and a Property Viewer Report so learners can match the tool names in this lesson to the screen.
  • Links: the GIS and numeracy work in the numeracy chapter uses the same distances and scale ideas.
