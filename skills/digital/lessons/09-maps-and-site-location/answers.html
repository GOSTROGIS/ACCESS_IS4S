Find and measure a site on a map

Find a job site by address and by coordinates, measure a distance, look up property and zoning information in VanMap, and know what an online map cannot tell you before you dig.

  • Components: Use online tools and platforms; Use digital information

Task 1 (level 1)

Answer: about 49.2610, -123.1139. Any reading from 49.2608 to 49.2613 for latitude and -123.1136 to -123.1142 for longitude is correct, because it depends on where on the building the learner clicked.

Check: latitude first and positive, longitude second and negative, decimals written with a period.

Task 2 (level 2)

Answer: the nearest station is Broadway–City Hall on the Canada Line. The exact distances depend on the points clicked, but the walking route is always at least as long as the straight line, and usually longer.

A good response gives both numbers with units and explains that the straight line goes across buildings and lots, while the walking route follows sidewalks and crossings. Accept any reasonable numbers that show the walking distance is greater.

Task 3 (level 2)

Answer: the zoning shown in the Property Viewer Report for 453 W 12th Ave.

Check the answer live in the VanMap Viewer before class, because zoning data can change. A good response copies the zoning exactly as shown and notes that the Viewer had to be opened through the Terms of Use agreement first.

Task 4 (level 2)

Answer: a length with units (metres or feet) for one side of the property, read from the Measurement tool.

A good sentence for a client quote: "This measurement was taken from the City of Vancouver's online map and is approximate. Final quantities will be confirmed on site, and property lines can only be confirmed by a BC Land Surveyor."

Task 5 (level 1)

Answer: one water or sewer layer turned on in Map Layers and one symbol explained from the Legend, for example "the blue line is a water main". Accept any symbol the learner reads correctly from the legend.

Task 6 (level 2)

What to tell the homeowner: a city map is not a locate. It may not show every buried line (gas, hydro, telecom, private lines) and is not exact. Nobody digs until BC 1 Call has been contacted and every member has responded.

Before Monday:

  1. Submit a locate request at bc1c.ca or call 1-800-474-6886 well ahead of Monday. Members respond within 3 business days, so a request on Friday may be too late for Monday. Move the start date if needed.
  2. Wait for all responses and follow their instructions.

Information to have ready (from the BC 1 Call checklist): street address, nearest cross street, type of work (digging post holes), activity (fence installation), where on the property (rear), whether the dig area is marked, and how to reach you.

Check yourself

  1. Latitude.
  2. Because BC is west of Greenwich, England, and Google Maps writes west as negative.
  3. No. A straight line ignores roads, so the driving or walking distance is longer.
  4. A BC Land Surveyor.
  5. A locate through BC 1 Call, with responses from every member, before any digging starts.
