# Format a work document in Word

ACCESS Indigenous Skills4Success · Digital · Lesson plan

| Session | Total length |
|---|---|
| Digital 3 | 90 min |

## Opening (3 min)

Welcome learners. Hook: hand out two printed copies of the same safe work procedure, one as a single block of text and one with headings, spacing and bold. Ask which one they would read on a cold morning before starting work. Today learners turn the plain version into the readable one in Word, using the tools Word gives you instead of fighting it.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session learners will be able to:

- find the Home tab groups for font, paragraph, styles and editing (Use common digital tools to complete tasks: use software for a purpose, for example Word)
- apply heading styles, set line spacing and space after, bold key text and use find and replace (Use common digital tools to complete tasks)
- add a title page with a page break and change page orientation (Use common digital tools to complete tasks)
- run the Accessibility Checker and save with a clear name (Use common digital tools to complete tasks: use digital tools to enhance accessibility for yourself and others)

Complexity: levels 1 and 2.

## Pre-assessment (3 min)

1. If you wanted a heading to stand out, what would you normally do?
2. A supervisor asks for a report "double-spaced". What would you do?

## Learning tasks (72 min)

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Tour the ribbon on the projector. Open each tab once. Point out the Font, Paragraph, Styles and Editing groups on Home. Show that resting the pointer shows a button's name. | 1.1 Follow along. Complete Task 1. | Projector, Word | 8 min |
| 1.2 | Model the worked example (Heading 2, then **Modify** the style). Explain why styles help screen readers. | 1.2 Watch. Tell a partner one reason to use styles. | Projector | 6 min |
| 2.1 | Hand out or display the Tool Crib text. Remind learners to type it exactly. | 2.1 Type or paste the practice text into a new document. | Learner page, Word | 8 min |
| 2.2 | Demonstrate Heading 1 and Heading 2 from the Styles gallery and with Ctrl + Alt + 1 and 2. Circulate. | 2.2 Complete Task 2. | Word | 10 min |
| 2.3 | Demonstrate **Line and Paragraph Spacing** > **Line Spacing Options**, 1.5 lines and 6 pt After. Show why deleting blank lines matters. | 2.3 Complete Task 3. | Word | 10 min |
| 3.1 | Demonstrate bold (Ctrl + B), Ctrl + H and Ctrl + F with the Navigation pane result count. | 3.1 Complete Tasks 4 and 5. | Word | 8 min |
| 3.2 | Demonstrate the title page: type at the top, Ctrl + E, Ctrl + Enter. | 3.2 Complete Task 6. | Word | 10 min |
| 3.3 | Demonstrate **Review** > **Check Accessibility** and reading the Accessibility pane. Remind learners of the Lesson 1 naming pattern. | 3.3 Complete Task 8 and save `v1`. | Word | 7 min |
| 3.4 | Demonstrate **Layout** > **Orientation**. Ask when a sideways page helps (wide tables, drawings). | 3.4 Complete Task 7. | Word | 5 min |

## Post-assessment (6 min)

1. Show me page 2 of your document. Point to a Heading 1, a Heading 2 and a Normal paragraph.
2. Change one body paragraph to double spacing without pressing Enter. Which shortcut or menu did you use?
3. What did the Accessibility Checker find in your document, and what did you do about it?

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- the ribbon, its tabs and the main Home tab groups
- heading styles and why they beat hand formatting
- line spacing and space after instead of extra Enter presses
- bold, find, and find and replace
- a title page with centred lines and a page break
- page orientation
- the Accessibility Checker and saving with a clear name

Time check: 3 + 2 + 3 + 72 + 6 + 4 = 90 min.

## Notes for the instructor

- The old ESAF exercises (CP Rail document, title page, spacing) taught menu paths from Word 2003 to 2010, such as **Format** > **Paragraph**. Those menus no longer exist. This lesson keeps the same skills with current buttons and uses an original fictional procedure instead of the Canadian Pacific corporate text.
- The old instructions inserted clip art and page borders. This lesson leaves pictures to Lesson 10, where alt text is taught with them.
- Learners who finish early can modify Heading 2 to a new colour and watch all three headings change, or add a numbered list for the three steps.
- Collect `v1` for Lesson 4, where learners add a table and save a PDF.

## Evidence to check

Reopen the saved procedure and inspect heading styles, spacing, the replacement time and the page break. A clean Accessibility Checker report alone does not prove accessibility.
