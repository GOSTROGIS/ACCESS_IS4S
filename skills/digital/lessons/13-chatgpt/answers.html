Using ChatGPT

Task 2 (level 1)

  • (a) Web: account menu > Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone. Phone: sidebar > profile icon > Data controls.
  • (b) Memory: Settings > Personalization ("Reference saved memories" and "Reference chat history"). A Temporary Chat stays out of history and memory.

Task 3 (level 2)

Answer: 10 posts, 18 rails, 20 bags of concrete mix.

Steps:

  • Sections: 21.6 ÷ 2.4 = 9 sections.
  • Posts: one more post than sections, because there is a post at each end. Posts = 21.6 ÷ 2.4 + 1. By BEDMAS, divide first: 9 + 1 = 10 posts.
  • Rails: 9 sections × 2 rails = 18 rails, each 2.4 m.
  • Concrete: 10 posts × 2 bags = 20 bags.

The common mistake, by people and by chatbots, is 9 posts (forgetting the end post). If ChatGPT said 9 posts, the learner should catch it. A strong answer also notes that waste and gate hardware are not included and asks the journeyperson about extra.

Task 4 (level 2)

Correct order from section 11.2:

  1. Guardrails, or another similar means of fall restraint, when practicable (11.2(2)).
  2. If that is not practicable, another fall restraint system (11.2(3)).
  3. If that is not practicable, a fall arrest system or a rope access system that meets Part 34 (11.2(4)).
  4. If that is not practicable, or would create a greater hazard, work procedures acceptable to WorkSafeBC that minimize the risk of injury from a fall (11.2(5)).

A good response names one specific thing ChatGPT got right or wrong, for example: it put the steps in the right order, it left out rope access, it mixed up fall restraint and fall arrest, or it gave no section numbers. Fall restraint stops a worker from falling. Fall arrest stops a fall after it starts (definitions in section 11.1).

Task 5 (level 2)

Open task. A good message:

  • says the time (7:10 a.m.), the site (Alder Street) and the numbers (40 studs came, the slip says 60)
  • does not blame the driver or the yard
  • asks one clear question, such as whether to call the supplier or keep working with what is there
  • is short enough to read on a phone
  • has no private information

Example: "Hi Dale, the lumber for Alder Street came at 7:10. The slip says 60 studs but only 40 came. Do you want me to call the yard, or keep going with what we have? Thanks, [your practice name]"

If ChatGPT added facts that were not in the scenario (a driver's name, a reason for the shortage, a new delivery time), the learner should take them out.

Task 6 (level 1)

Open task. A complete card names the claim (for example "9 posts" or the order of the fall protection options), the place checked (own math, or section 11.2 on worksafebc.com), whether it matched, and what the learner kept or changed. Accept any task where the learner shows real checking.

Check yourself

  1. OpenAI. Official help: help.openai.com.
  2. You can try it without an account in supported countries. An account keeps your history. Minimum age 13, with permission under 18.
  3. Any two of: search the web, upload images or files, analyze data, create images, use GPTs, Library, limited voice.
  4. Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone.
  5. ChatGPT predicts likely words and can be wrong or incomplete while sounding sure. A wrong quantity or rule costs money or causes injury, so you check it against your own math and the official source.
