Build a site through time

Place real Slipcurve catalogue objects in a fictional site, rotate them, then decide when they are present. The fourth dimension is time.

Learning model, not a site safety plan. Checks compare overall rectangular footprints on level ground during overlapping times. They do not check access widths, moving equipment, lifting, clearances, ground conditions or regulations. “No overlap” does not mean “safe”.

Try three activities (30–45 minutes)
  1. Place and read: add a 20 ft container and a cabin. Read their dimensions, move them and rotate one by 90°. Explain why a digital drawing needs a scale.
  2. Find and fix: load the overlap challenge. Identify the two objects and time window. Move one so their footprints no longer overlap. Explain what this check still cannot tell you.
  3. Change the schedule: load the challenge again. Keep the positions but set the cabin’s start to hour 4 and end to hour 8. Compare hours 3 and 4. Save both versions with different file names and explain your change to a partner.
