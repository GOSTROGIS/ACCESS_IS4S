# Use an equipment manual

Session 8 of 10 in the Reading lessons · 75 minutes

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Put a real tool manual on the table, the thicker the better. Ask: "Who has ever read one of these cover to cover?" Then tell a short story, like the one in the old ACCESS electrical lesson: a boss at the top of a ladder yells "Check my power!" and the helper has no idea where to start. The manual's troubleshooting table would have told them, in order. Today is about getting to the right page of a manual fast.

This is session 8 of 10 in the Reading lessons. It runs 75 minutes.

## Objective (3 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- find a section with the contents page and a topic with the index (Identify the information)
- connect a nameplate, a specifications table, a warning and a troubleshooting table (Make connections between different parts of the document(s))
- use a troubleshooting table and a parts list to decide what to do and what to order (Understand and apply the information)
- choose the manual over an unreliable source when they disagree (Evaluate the document(s))

## Pre-assessment (5 min)

1. "Where in a manual would you look to find out how fast a tool spins?"
2. "What is the difference between a contents page and an index?"

## Post-assessment (6 min)

1. "The grinder will not start. Using the troubleshooting table, what is the first thing you check?"
2. "You need a new side handle. What part number do you order?"
3. "Give one reason to trust the manual for your model over a video."

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- the contents page and the index, and when to use each
- reading safety warnings first
- specifications and the nameplate
- reading a troubleshooting table row by row
- ordering by part number
- choosing the manual when other sources disagree

Total time: 5 + 3 + 5 + 52 + 6 + 4 = 75 minutes.
