Use an equipment manual

Find specifications, safety warnings, troubleshooting steps and part numbers in a tool manual by using its contents page, index and tables.

Task 2 (level 1)

a) 11 000 r/min (revolutions per minute). The nameplate writes it as 11 000 /min. b) 9 A c) Yes. Both say 120 V AC, 60 Hz, 9 A and 11 000 per minute.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) No. 8 500 is less than 11 000. b) Yes. 13 300 is greater than 11 000. c) Yes. 11 000 is equal to 11 000.

The warning: "Use only discs and accessories with a maximum speed marked on them that is equal to or greater than the no-load speed marked on this grinder." The size, 125 mm, also matches the specifications.

Task 4 (level 2)

Likely cause: the extension cord is too long or too thin. What to do: use a shorter or heavier cord. The table sends you to section 5, Operating, which says a cord that is too long or too thin can make the motor slow down and overheat.

Also accept: check that the air vents are not blocked, which is the other possible cause in the same row. A good answer notices that the long, thin cord matches the situation best.

Task 5 (level 2)

Order AG125-01 (wheel guard) and AG125-02 (clamp nut).

No, you cannot keep using it. The safety warning says never use the grinder if the guard is missing or damaged. Without the clamp nut you cannot fit a disc anyway. Tag the grinder out of service and tell your supervisor.

Task 6 (level 2)

About 3 1/3 weeks, so during the fourth week.

Hours per week: 6 × 5 = 30 hours. Weeks to 100 hours: 100 ÷ 30 = 3.33 weeks.

As one line with BEDMAS: 100 ÷ (6 × 5) = 100 ÷ 30 ≈ 3.3. The brackets make sure the multiplication is done first.

Task 7 (level 3)

Use the index. It lists "Brushes, carbon" on pages 10, 11 and 12: - page 10, Maintenance: the 100-hour brush check - page 11, Troubleshooting: worn brushes can stop the grinder starting or cause heavy sparking - page 12, Parts list: the part number for brushes, AG125-05

The contents page has no section called "brushes". You would have to guess that brushes appear under maintenance, and you could miss the troubleshooting rows and the parts list.

Task 8 (level 3)

The manual says "Always use the wheel guard. Never use the grinder if the guard is missing or damaged." CCOHS also says not to use a grinder with missing or damaged guards.

What to do: do not take the guard off. Tell the co-worker the manual for this grinder says to always use the guard. A video does not change the manual, and you cannot tell who made the video or whether it is the same tool. If the guard gets in the way, ask the supervisor about a different tool or method for tight corners.

Points a good response covers: quotes or points to the warning, explains why the manual for this model is the better source, and gives a safe action that does not involve removing the guard.
