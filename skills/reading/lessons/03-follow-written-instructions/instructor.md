# Follow written instructions step by step

Session 3 of 26 in the Reading lessons · 60 minutes

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Ask: "Has anyone had a job go wrong because a step was skipped or done in the wrong order? What happened?" Take two or three stories. Then ask: "How many ways can a ten-step procedure go wrong?" Lead learners to the answer the old ACCESS lesson used: at least as many as there are steps.

This is session 3 of 26 in the Reading lessons. It runs 60 minutes.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- find the order words, numbers, conditions and warnings in a set of instructions (Identify the information)
- connect a condition or warning to the step it changes (Make connections between different parts of the document(s))
- put steps in order and decide whether a job can go ahead (Understand and apply the information)

## Pre-assessment (5 min)

1. "When you get a new set of instructions, what do you do first?"
2. Show the worked example sentence. "How many separate actions are in this sentence?"

## Post-assessment (6 min)

1. "In Document B, which step comes right after the dry fit check?"
2. "What would happen if you followed only the first condition in Document C and ignored the other two?"
3. "In the sentence 'Put the cans away, but first wipe them clean', which action comes first?"

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- reading all steps before starting
- order words, numbers, conditions and warnings
- steps that hold two actions
- checking that every condition is true before going ahead
- turning a paragraph into numbered steps

Total time: 5 + 2 + 5 + 38 + 6 + 4 = 60 minutes.
