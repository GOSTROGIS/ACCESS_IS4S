Follow written instructions step by step

Read a set of instructions all the way through, find the order, the numbers and the conditions, and carry them out without skipping a step.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the information; Make connections between different parts of the document(s); Understand and apply the information

Task 1 (level 1)

The correct result: only your name is written at the top of the page, and the pencil is down. Nothing else is marked.

Step 6 tells you to do only step 1. A learner who wrote the date, counted bolts or drew a square started before reading all the steps. This is not a trick to embarrass anyone. It shows why the last line of a procedure matters. Talk about a real case, such as a warning at the end of a manual that says to unplug the tool before step 1.

Task 2 (level 1)

Order: D, B, F, G, C, E, A, H

  1. D. Look at the cement and throw it out if it has turned to jelly.
  2. B. Cut the pipe square and remove burrs.
  3. F. Check the dry fit.
  4. G. Apply a thin coat inside the fitting.
  5. C. Apply a heavier coat to the pipe.
  6. E. Push the pipe in with a 1/4 turn until it bottoms out.
  7. A. Hold the pipe and fitting together for 30 seconds.
  8. H. Wait 24 to 48 hours before applying pressure.

Steps: follow the order in Document B. G before C comes from the order of the bullets and the worked example ("then").

Task 3 (level 2)

a) 30 seconds b) 15 minutes c) 24 to 48 hours d) About 1/4 of the way e) About 1 1/2 inches. The label says about 1/2 the diameter. 1/2 × 3 = 1 1/2.

Steps: scan for each number, then check the unit. Learners who answer "30 minutes" for a) have the right number with the wrong unit.

Task 4 (level 2)

  1. He thinned the cement with primer. The label says "Do not use primer or cleaner to thin the cement." Also, if the cement was getting thick, he should have checked whether it had turned to jelly, and thrown it out if it had.
  2. He left the cement to "set up" before joining. The label says "Assemble quickly while the cement is still wet."
  3. He pressure tested after one hour. The label says "Allow 24 to 48 hours before applying pressure."

Accept also: he did not say he put a thin coat in the fitting and a heavier coat on the pipe, or that he used a 1/4 turn and held the joint for 30 seconds. Those are things he did not mention, not things he clearly did wrong.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) Yes. Check each condition. - The wood is dry: yes. - At least 10 °C now: yes, 14 °C. Stays at or above 10 °C until dry to the touch: if you start at 9:00 a.m., the stain is dry to the touch at about 1:00 p.m. (9 + 4 = 13:00). The forecast for 1:00 p.m. is 12 °C, so yes. The 6 °C overnight comes after the stain is dry to the touch. - No rain for 24 hours: rain starts in 30 hours, so yes.

All three are true, so you can stain. A learner who says no because of the 6 °C overnight has not connected the temperature condition to the drying time. Point back to the words "until the stain is dry to the touch".

b) No. Rain in 12 hours breaks the third condition. The card says if any one condition is not true, do other work and tell the foreman.

Task 6 (level 2)

  1. Close every can of cement and primer tightly.
  2. Wipe any cement off the outside of each can with a dry rag.
  3. Put the cans back in the flammables cabinet.
  4. Put the used rags in the metal rag bin and close the lid. Do not put them in the regular garbage.
  5. Write on the job board which joints are less than 24 hours old.

Steps: "but first" moves the wiping before putting the cans away. "Last" puts the job board step at the end. Steps 1 and 2 can be swapped if the learner wipes each can as it is closed, as long as wiping comes before the cabinet.

Check yourself 1

A later step or warning can change what you do first, or tell you to get a tool or protective equipment before you begin. Document A shows this.

Check yourself 2

Any four of: first, next, then, after, before, while, until, finally, last, step numbers or letters.

Check yourself 3

It moves the step that follows "but first" ahead of the step just named.

Check yourself 4

Three conditions must all be true. If any one is not true, do other work and tell the foreman.
