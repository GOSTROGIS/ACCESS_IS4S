## Task 7 (level 2)

a) **First-angle projection.** The top view below the front view is the first-angle layout.
b) The cone is on the left and the circles are on the right. The **narrow end of the cone points away from the circles.**
c) The fabricator would read the views on the wrong sides. The top view would be read as a bottom view, and the side view as the opposite side. Holes, notches or brackets could be built on the wrong side, making a mirror image of the part. Always check the symbol before you read the views.

## Task 10 (level 3)

**No, the sketch is wrong.** In third-angle projection the front of the object faces the front view. In the right side view the front of the object is on the left, next to the front view. The upright leg is at the back of the bracket, so it must be on the **right** side of the view, as in Figure 3.

What could happen: a fitter working from the sketch could weld the upright leg to the wrong edge of the base. The hole would then be on the wrong side. The bracket would be a mirror image and might not bolt up. Check a sketch against the front view and top view before it goes to the shop.

## Task 11 (level 3)

1. Start with the piece 150 mm wide, 90 mm deep, 100 mm high. Mark the front face.
2. Step: at the right end, cut away a block 50 mm wide and 40 mm down from the top. Cut all the way through from the front face to the back face.

4. Check: from the front you should see the step but not the groove.
