## Task 7 (level 2)

a) **1.4 km.** The bridge is at easting 53.4 and the west edge is at easting 52.0. 53.4 − 52.0 = 1.4 km.
b) **2.8 cm.**
- 1.4 km = 1400 m = 140 000 cm.
- Map distance = real distance ÷ scale number: 140 000 ÷ 50 000 = 2.8 cm.
- Check: 2 cm is 1 km, so 1.4 km is 1.4 × 2 = 2.8 cm.

c) **3.5 km.**
- Real distance = map distance × scale number: 7 × 50 000 = 350 000 cm.
- 350 000 cm ÷ 100 = 3500 m, and 3500 m ÷ 1000 = 3.5 km.
- Check: 2 cm is 1 km, so 7 cm is 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5 km.

## Task 11 (level 3)

a) **1:50 000.**
- At 1:25 000: 10 km = 1 000 000 cm ÷ 25 000 = 40 cm wide. 14 km = 1 400 000 cm ÷ 25 000 = 56 cm tall. **Too big** for 20 cm × 28 cm.
- At 1:50 000: 1 000 000 ÷ 50 000 = 20 cm wide. 1 400 000 ÷ 50 000 = 28 cm tall. **Fits exactly.**
- At 1:250 000: 1 000 000 ÷ 250 000 = 4 cm wide. 1 400 000 ÷ 250 000 = 5.6 cm tall. It fits, but it is tiny.
- So 1:50 000 is the biggest scale that fits.

b) **No at 1:100. Yes at 1:200.**
- At 1:100: 20 m = 2000 cm ÷ 100 = 20 cm wide, and 25 m = 2500 cm ÷ 100 = 25 cm tall. Too big for 12 cm × 15 cm.
- At 1:200: 2000 ÷ 200 = 10 cm wide, and 2500 ÷ 200 = 12.5 cm tall. It fits.

## Task 12 (level 3)

a) Points a good answer covers:
- The map is **not wrong. It is old.** It shows the land as it was when its data was collected. NRCan tells users to check the map data and publication date in the bottom left corner.
- Things built after that date, such as new roads, clearings and buildings, may be missing. Things that were removed may still be shown.
- Check: the newest map or a current online map, the company's own directions to the site, and ask the foreman or someone who has driven the road. Never use a map to decide where it is safe to dig. Use BC 1 Call (Digital lesson 9).

b) The note says **"Do not scale this drawing."** Use the written dimensions: the lot is 20.0 m wide, the house is 5.0 m from the west lot line and 10.0 m wide. 20.0 − 5.0 − 10.0 = **5.0 m** from the house to the east lot line.
