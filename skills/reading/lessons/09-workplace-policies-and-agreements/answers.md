## Task 6 (level 3)

**Yes, there is a problem.** Section 6 lets employees agree in writing to be paid their regular rate for extra hours instead of overtime rates. Section 4 of the BC Employment Standards Act says its requirements are minimum requirements and an agreement to waive them has no effect. The Province's own interpretation guide uses almost this exact case: an agreement to work extra hours at the regular rate has no effect, and the employer must still pay the overtime rates the Act requires.

Points a good response covers: names the clause, names the Act's rule that minimums cannot be waived, and says that signing would not make it valid. Learners do not need to know the overtime rates themselves for this task.

## Task 7 (level 3)

a) It allows the employer to **deduct from your pay the cost of a company tool that is lost, stolen or damaged** while signed out to you.

b) The Province of BC says **it is illegal to make employees pay for business expenses, even if they agree to it**, and lists **stolen or damaged company property** as an example. So the deduction clause goes against what the Province says, and signing it would not make such deductions allowed.

c) Any of these:
- Do not sign on the spot. Ask for a copy to read.
- Ask the person what the clause means and point out the Province's rule politely.
- Look up "Deductions" on the BC Employment Standards website, or contact Employment Standards.
- If you are in a union, ask your union representative.
- You can still agree to take good care of tools. That part of the agreement is reasonable.

## Task 8 (level 3)

c) Any sensible answer, for example: how much the pay is and when payday is, what the overtime rates are, how to report a safety concern or refuse unsafe work, how to report harassment, how holidays and vacation work, or who to contact with a question about the handbook.
