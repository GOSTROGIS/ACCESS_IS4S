## Task 7 (level 3)

a) **3.7 m.** The vertical height of any flight of stairs shall not exceed 3.7 m.
b) **Not more than 100 mm.** Handrails and constructions below handrails, including handrail supports and stair stringers, shall not project more than 100 mm into the required width of a stair or ramp.
c) In **Article 1.4.1.2. of Division A** (Volume 1). Article 9.2.1.1. says so, and Part 9 repeats it.

For the opening story: neither carpenter was exactly right. The limit is 100 mm. Four inches is about 102 mm (4 × 25.4 = 101.6), so even 4 inches is slightly over the limit, and 6 inches is well over.

## Task 8 (level 3)

Points a good response covers:

- The handout was written for an **older edition** of the Code. The current edition is the **BC Building Code 2024**, in effect since March 8, 2024.
- **Code numbers and rules can change** between editions. An old number may now point to a different rule, or the rule may have changed.
- **Look it up in the current Code**, and check the revisions page.
- Which edition applies to a project depends on when its building permit was applied for, so on a real job ask the supervisor or the building official which edition the permit is under.

A learner who says "the old handout is wrong" has gone too far. The point is that it has not been checked against the current edition, not that it is known to be wrong.
