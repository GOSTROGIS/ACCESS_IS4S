## Task 3 (level 2)

a) **76'-6".**
Grid 5 to 4 = 25'-6". Grid 4 to 3 = 25'-6". Grid 3 to 2 = 25'-6".
25'-6" × 3 = 75' + 18" = 75' + 1'-6" = **76'-6"**.

b) **3 11/16"** (to the nearest 1/16").
29'-8" = 29 + 8/12 = 29.667 ft.
29.667 × 1/8" = 29.667 ÷ 8 = 3.708".
0.708 × 16 = 11.33, so about 11/16. 3 11/16" = 3.6875", the nearest sixteenth.

## Task 4 (level 2)

a) **756.5 sq ft.**
Grid 4 to 3 = 25'-6" = 25.5 ft. Grid C to B = 29'-8" = 29.667 ft (29 2/3 ft).
Area = 25.5 × 29 2/3 = 756.5 sq ft.

b) **70.3 m².**
25.5 ft × 0.3048 = 7.7724 m. 29 2/3 ft × 0.3048 = 9.0424 m.
7.7724 × 9.0424 = 70.28 m², which rounds to **70.3 m²**.
(Or: 1 sq ft = 0.3048 × 0.3048 = 0.09290304 m², and 756.5 × 0.09290304 = 70.28 m².)

This is grid-to-grid, not the finished floor area. Columns and walls take some of it.

## Task 9 (level 3)

a) The left plan shows **units 401 to 404** (and the rest of the 400s) and **General Office 401** with a floor elevation of **276.17'**.
b) **Level 4.** Building Section 1 shows Level 4 at 276'-2", and 276.17 ft = 276'-2".
c) **The view titles on P-6 are swapped.** The plan titled "Level 3" shows Level 4 (units 401 and up, 276.17'), and the plan titled "Level 4" shows Level 3 (units 301 and up, 264.17'). A plumber could rough in a floor from the wrong plan. On this sheet both floors look almost the same, but the pipe routes differ: for example the right plan shows 3" roof drains (RD) in units 301 and 303 that the left plan does not.
d) Sample RFI:

> **RFI: P-6 plan titles**
> To: Oslo Mechanical. From: (your name, company). Date: (YYYY-MM-DD).
> On P-6, the left plan is titled "5 Level 3" but shows units 401 to 411 and a floor elevation of 276.17'. The right plan is titled "6 Level 4" but shows units 301 to 311 and 264.17'. A-401 Building Section 1 gives Level 4 at 276'-2" and Level 3 at 264'-2". Please confirm which plan is Level 3 and which is Level 4, and reissue P-6 with corrected titles. We will not rough in plumbing on Levels 3 and 4 until we have your answer.

Points a good answer covers: names the sheet, quotes both titles, gives the evidence from a second sheet, asks one clear question, says what work is on hold.
