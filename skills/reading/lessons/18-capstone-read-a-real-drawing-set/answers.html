Capstone: read a real drawing set

Work a real job through one drawing set, from the drawing list to the plan, section, wall section, schedules and plumbing sheets, then do the math and flag the problems you find.

Every answer below was read from the real London Square sheets shown in the figures. Every number was computed and checked.

Task 1 (level 2)

a) A-241 Floor plan - Level L3 & L4 office plan b) A-401 and A-402 Building sections c) A-901 Door schedule d) A-101 "Construction schedule". The name sounds like a time schedule, but Figure 8 shows it holds the wall, floor and roof assemblies (such as W2). e) P-6 Level 3 & 4 floor plans - plumbing f) P-9 Wet standpipe diagram / water riser diagram / washroom details - plumbing g) E-2.3 Floor plan Level 3 & 4

Task 2 (level 2)

a) Level 4 plan: 1/8" = 1'-0" (view title under the plan). Wall section 07: 1/2" = 1'-0". b) P-8 and P-9 are N.T.S. N.T.S. means not to scale. You cannot measure them with a scale rule at all. Use only the written information.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) 76'-6". Grid 5 to 4 = 25'-6". Grid 4 to 3 = 25'-6". Grid 3 to 2 = 25'-6". 25'-6" × 3 = 75' + 18" = 75' + 1'-6" = 76'-6".

b) 3 11/16" (to the nearest 1/16"). 29'-8" = 29 + 8/12 = 29.667 ft. 29.667 × 1/8" = 29.667 ÷ 8 = 3.708". 0.708 × 16 = 11.33, so about 11/16. 3 11/16" = 3.6875", the nearest sixteenth.

Task 4 (level 2)

a) 756.5 sq ft. Grid 4 to 3 = 25'-6" = 25.5 ft. Grid C to B = 29'-8" = 29.667 ft (29 2/3 ft). Area = 25.5 × 29 2/3 = 756.5 sq ft.

b) 70.3 m². 25.5 ft × 0.3048 = 7.7724 m. 29 2/3 ft × 0.3048 = 9.0424 m. 7.7724 × 9.0424 = 70.28 m², which rounds to 70.3 m². (Or: 1 sq ft = 0.3048 × 0.3048 = 0.09290304 m², and 756.5 × 0.09290304 = 70.28 m².)

This is grid-to-grid, not the finished floor area. Columns and walls take some of it.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) It is a building section marker: section 01, drawn on sheet A-401. The cut runs across the building near grid D. b) Level 4 = 276'-2". Level 3 = 264'-2". c) 12'-0". 276'-2" − 264'-2" = 12'-0". The section also prints 12'-0" between those levels. d) 12'-4". Roof plan 288'-6" − Level 4 276'-2" = 12'-4". The section prints 12'-4".

Task 6 (level 2)

a) 07 over A-602, at the top right of Building Section 1, at the east wall. Sheet A-602. (The plan in Figure 5 shows a 07 over A-602 marker on the same east wall.) b) "Wall - EAST - Brick Pier @ 137th St.", scale 1/2" = 1'-0". c) W2. d) Outside to inside: 3 1/2" brick veneer, 1" air space, 1 1/2" foil face polyisocyanurate insulation, 1/2" exterior sheathing, 6" engineered metal studs with 6" batt insulation, 6 mil poly vapour barrier, 1/2" gypsum board. Total, not counting the poly: 3 1/2 + 1 + 1 1/2 + 1/2 + 6 + 1/2 = 13".

Task 7 (level 2)

a) Womens WC, 3'-0" wide × 7'-0" high. In millimetres: 36" × 25.4 = 914.4 mm, 84" × 25.4 = 2133.6 mm (about 914 × 2134). b) Hardware type 6B, hardware finish Type A, door type A, fire rating none ("-"). Door material PS (pressed steel), door finish PT (paint), frame PS, frame finish PT (from the legend on A-901 and lesson 17). c) Hardware 6B, typical lockset: 1 1/2 pair NRP hinges, lever, lockset, door closer, kickplate and pushplate, floor stop or wall stop. (The sheet prints it "1 1/2" PAIR NRP HINGES", with an inch mark after the 1 1/2.) d) Door 402 is a 5'-0" opening with door type "2 x A", so it is most likely a pair of type A doors (two leaves). The schedule does not say so in words, so confirm before ordering.

Task 9 (level 3)

a) The left plan shows units 401 to 404 (and the rest of the 400s) and General Office 401 with a floor elevation of 276.17'. b) Level 4. Building Section 1 shows Level 4 at 276'-2", and 276.17 ft = 276'-2". c) The view titles on P-6 are swapped. The plan titled "Level 3" shows Level 4 (units 401 and up, 276.17'), and the plan titled "Level 4" shows Level 3 (units 301 and up, 264.17'). A plumber could rough in a floor from the wrong plan. On this sheet both floors look almost the same, but the pipe routes differ: for example the right plan shows 3" roof drains (RD) in units 301 and 303 that the left plan does not. d) Sample RFI:

RFI: P-6 plan titles To: Oslo Mechanical. From: (your name, company). Date: (YYYY-MM-DD). On P-6, the left plan is titled "5 Level 3" but shows units 401 to 411 and a floor elevation of 276.17'. The right plan is titled "6 Level 4" but shows units 301 to 311 and 264.17'. A-401 Building Section 1 gives Level 4 at 276'-2" and Level 3 at 264'-2". Please confirm which plan is Level 3 and which is Level 4, and reissue P-6 with corrected titles. We will not rough in plumbing on Levels 3 and 4 until we have your answer.

Points a good answer covers: names the sheet, quotes both titles, gives the evidence from a second sheet, asks one clear question, says what work is on hold.

Task 10 (level 3)

a) 828 Kingston Blvd. The plan shows it along the east side (grid 1 side). b) The plan marker 07 over A-602 on the east wall (Figure 5), and the callout 07 over A-602 on Building Section 1 (Figure 6), both point to it. The wall section title also says EAST, and it is cut at grid 1. c) Use the markers, not the street name, to find the wall, and note the mismatch. Tell your supervisor and ask the architect in writing to confirm that section 07 is the Kingston Blvd. east wall and to correct the title.

Task 11 (level 3)

Sample job card:

Level 4, Women's washroom 403. London Square. - Door 403: 3'-0" × 7'-0" (914 × 2134 mm), door type A, pressed steel, painted, pressed steel frame, hardware 6B typical lockset (1 1/2 pair NRP hinges, lever, lockset, closer, kickplate and pushplate, stop). No fire rating. (A-901) - Fixtures: 2 toilets WC-1, 1 toilet WC-2, 2 basins WB-1 and WB-2, 1 floor drain FD-1 (epoxy coated cast iron, 6" adjustable nickel bronze strainer). (P-6, P-1, matches A-241) - Height: Level 4 at 276'-2", roof at 288'-6", so 12'-4" floor to floor. (A-401) - Sheets used: A-100, A-241, A-401, A-901, P-1, P-6 (and P-9 for washroom details). - Ask first: P-6 has its Level 3 and Level 4 titles swapped. Confirm before rough-in.

Points a good answer covers: correct door and fixture data, the right sheet for each fact, at least five sheets, and one real question.
