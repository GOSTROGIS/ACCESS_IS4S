# What is in a construction contract

## Why this matters on the job

Every job runs on a contract, even a small one. The contract says what the work is, what it pays, when the money comes and what happens when something changes. Lead hands, foremen and small contractors read contracts all the time. One day you may sign one for your own crew. In BC, a law called the *Builders Lien Act* also decides part of how money moves on a job, so some of the money is held back until the end. Knowing where to look saves arguments and lost pay.

This lesson teaches reading. It is not legal advice. For a real contract or a real unpaid bill, talk to a lawyer.

## Key ideas

**1. Find the nine parts.** Most construction contracts and subcontracts cover:

| Part | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| Parties | Who is agreeing with whom? |
| The work | What exactly will be done? (see [Reading lesson 19](../19-read-a-scope-of-work/learner.html)) |
| Price | How much, for all of it? |
| Schedule | When does it start and finish? |
| Payment | When do you invoice, and when are you paid? |
| Changes | How is extra work agreed and priced? |
| Holdback | How much is kept back until the end? |
| Warranty | What do you fix later, and for how long? |
| Signatures and dates | Who agreed, and when? |

**2. Get it in writing.** If something was agreed out loud, ask for it to be added in writing before you start. A contract that says "changes must be in writing" means a handshake on site will not count.

**3. Holdback in BC.** Under section 4 of the *Builders Lien Act*, the person paying on a construction contract or subcontract must keep back a **holdback of 10%** of the work done. The holdback must not be kept back from a worker's wages or from a material supplier.

> Work done this month: $10,000
> Holdback: 10% of $10,000 = $1,000
> Payment now: $10,000 − $1,000 = $9,000

**4. When holdback is paid out.** When the work is complete, the contractor can ask for a *certificate of completion*. "Complete" in this Act means substantially complete, not every last item. The **holdback period ends 55 days after** that certificate is issued (section 8). After the holdback period, the holdback can be paid out if no liens have been filed.

**5. What a builders lien is.** Under section 2 of the Act, a contractor, subcontractor or **worker** who does work or supplies material on a building or land has a **lien** for the price that is still unpaid. The lien is a claim on the owner's interest in the building, the building itself, the land it stands on, and material delivered to that land.
- Deadline: when a certificate of completion has been issued, a lien must be filed **no later than 45 days after** the date it was issued (section 20).
- A lien cannot be filed for less than $200 (section 17).
- **You cannot sign your lien rights away.** Section 42 says an agreement that the Act will not apply, or that its remedies will not be available to a person, is void (it has no effect).

**6. The law can change.** BC passed a new law, the *Construction Prompt Payment Act*, in November 2025. When this lesson was checked, it was not yet in force. When it comes into force, some payment and lien rules will change. Always check the current Act on BC Laws: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca

### Worked example

**Question:** Who supplies the lumber on the garage job?

1. Find the section about the work: section 2 of Document A.
2. Read it closely: "Cedar Line supplies all framing labour and fasteners. Kestrel Point supplies all lumber."
3. **Kestrel Point supplies the lumber.** Cedar Line supplies labour and fasteners.

## Practice documents

**Document A: A short subcontract**

> **Subcontract KP-G7 (fictional)**
>
> **1. Parties.** This subcontract is between Kestrel Point Builders Ltd. (fictional company), the contractor, and Cedar Line Framing Ltd. (fictional company), the subcontractor.
>
> **2. The work.** Cedar Line will frame the walls and roof of a detached garage, 6.0 m by 7.2 m, at Lot 7 (fictional), as shown on drawings A1 to A4, revision 2. Cedar Line supplies all framing labour and fasteners. Kestrel Point supplies all lumber. Not included: roofing, siding, installing windows and doors, and electrical.
>
> **3. Price.** A fixed price of $18,000 for the work in section 2.
>
> **4. Schedule.** Cedar Line will start within 3 working days after Kestrel Point signs off the slab, and finish within 15 working days after starting.
>
> **5. Payment.** Cedar Line invoices on the last working day of each month for work done that month. Kestrel Point pays within 30 days of receiving the invoice, less the 10% holdback required by the BC *Builders Lien Act*. Kestrel Point pays the holdback after the holdback period ends, if no liens have been filed.
>
> **6. Changes.** Cedar Line will not do extra work without a written change order signed by both parties. The change order states the price of the extra work.
>
> **7. Safety.** Cedar Line follows the WorkSafeBC regulation and Kestrel Point's site safety plan. Before starting, Cedar Line gives Kestrel Point a WorkSafeBC clearance letter.
>
> **8. Warranty.** Cedar Line will fix, at no charge, any defect in its work that is reported within 1 year after the work is complete.
>
> **9. Clean-up.** Cedar Line keeps its work area clean. If it does not, Kestrel Point may give 24 hours' written notice, then clean the area and deduct the cost from Cedar Line's next payment.
>
> **10. Liens.** Cedar Line agrees not to file a builders lien on this job for any reason.
>
> Signed: _______________ for Kestrel Point   Signed: _______________ for Cedar Line   Date: _______

**Document B: What happened on the job**

> - **End of month 1:** Cedar Line has done $10,000 of the work.
> - **Month 2:** The owner asks for an extra window opening in the back wall. Both companies sign change order CO-1 for $1,500. Cedar Line frames it.
> - **End of month 2:** Cedar Line finishes the rest of the original work, worth $8,000, plus CO-1.
> - **June 10:** A certificate of completion is issued for Cedar Line's subcontract.

## Practice

**Task 1** (level 1)
Use Document A.
a) Who are the two parties?
b) What is the price?
c) Name two things that are not included in the work.

**Task 2** (level 1)
When does Cedar Line send an invoice, and how long does Kestrel Point have to pay it?

**Task 3** (level 2)
The owner walks up to the Cedar Line foreman and says, "Can you frame an extra window in the back wall?" What must happen before the crew frames it? Name the section.

**Task 4** (level 2)
Use Document B and section 5.
a) How much holdback is kept from the month 1 invoice, and how much is Cedar Line paid?
b) The month 2 invoice is for the $8,000 of original work plus change order CO-1. What is the invoice total, the holdback and the payment?
c) What is the total holdback kept on the job?

**Task 5** (level 2)
The certificate of completion is issued on June 10.
a) On what date does the holdback period end?
b) What is the last day a lien could be filed for this subcontract?

**Task 6** (level 3)
Read section 10 of Document A. Is it valid in BC? Explain using Key idea 5.

**Task 7** (level 3)
A framer employed by Cedar Line has not been paid wages for his last two weeks on the garage.
a) Does the *Builders Lien Act* give a worker lien rights?
b) Can a holdback be kept back from his wages?

**Task 8** (level 3)
Look at Document A as a whole.
a) Who probably wrote it, and whose side does section 9 favour? Explain.
b) Name one thing the contract does not say that Cedar Line should ask about before signing.
