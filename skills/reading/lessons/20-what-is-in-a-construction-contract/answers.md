# What is in a construction contract

## Task 1 (level 1)

a) **Kestrel Point Builders Ltd.** (the contractor) and **Cedar Line Framing Ltd.** (the subcontractor). Section 1.
b) **$18,000**, a fixed price. Section 3.
c) Any two of: **roofing, siding, installing windows and doors, electrical.** Section 2.

## Task 2 (level 1)

Cedar Line invoices on the **last working day of each month** for that month's work. Kestrel Point pays **within 30 days** of receiving the invoice, less the 10% holdback. Section 5.

## Task 3 (level 2)

A **written change order signed by both parties**, stating the price of the extra work, must be in place before the crew frames it. Section 6. The owner asking the foreman out loud is not enough. The foreman should send the request to Cedar Line's office, which deals with Kestrel Point, the company it has the subcontract with.

## Task 4 (level 2)

a) Month 1:
- Holdback: 10% × $10,000 = **$1,000**
- Payment: $10,000 − $1,000 = **$9,000**

b) Month 2:
- Invoice total: $8,000 + $1,500 = **$9,500**
- Holdback: 10% × $9,500 = **$950**
- Payment: $9,500 − $950 = **$8,550**

c) Total holdback: $1,000 + $950 = **$1,950**

Check: the full value with the change order is $18,000 + $1,500 = $19,500, and 10% of $19,500 = $1,950. The two payments add to $9,000 + $8,550 = $17,550, and $17,550 + $1,950 = $19,500.

## Task 5 (level 2)

Count from the day after June 10. June has 30 days, so June 30 is 20 days after June 10.

a) Holdback period, 55 days: 20 days to June 30, then 31 days of July (51 days), then 4 more days. **August 4.**
b) Lien deadline, 45 days: 20 days to June 30, then 25 more days. **July 25.**

Sections 8 (1) and 20 (1) of the *Builders Lien Act*. In real life, do not wait until the last day.

## Task 6 (level 3)

**No, it does not work.** Section 42 (2) of the *Builders Lien Act* says an agreement that the Act is not to apply, or that its remedies will not be available to a person, is void. A promise never to file a lien tries to take away a remedy the Act gives. Signing it does not make it valid.

Points a good response covers: names the clause, names the rule in the Act, and says that a signed clause can still have no effect.

## Task 7 (level 3)

a) **Yes.** Section 2 gives a lien to "a contractor, subcontractor or worker" who performs work. A worker in the Act is an individual engaged for wages, so the framer has lien rights for his unpaid wages on the garage.
b) **No.** Section 4 (6) says a holdback must not be kept back from a worker.

He should still act quickly. Lien deadlines are short, and he can also contact BC Employment Standards about unpaid wages (see Reading lesson 09). For a real case, talk to a lawyer.
