Title blocks, sheet sets and the drawing index

Read the title block on a real sheet, find any sheet in a full drawing set with the drawing list, and check the issue, the revision and the date before you build.

  • Components: Identify the information, Make connections between different parts of the document(s), Understand and apply the information, Evaluate the document(s)

Every answer below was read from the real sheets shown in the figures.

Task 1 (level 1)

a) A-901 (Door schedule). b) P. There are 9 plumbing sheets, P-1 to P-9. They are listed under the Mechanical Drawings heading. c) ID-1 Room finish schedule. d) E-2.3 Floor plan Level 3 & 4.

Steps: find the discipline heading first, then read down the sheet names.

Task 2 (level 1)

a) A-201, Floor plan Level 1. b) 21740. c) Issue 8, 2022.05.06, Issued for IFC (issued for construction). d) It says "Do no scale drawings." It is misspelled. It should say "Do not scale drawings." The meaning is still clear: do not measure the drawing with a ruler.

Task 3 (level 1)

a) A1-04, Second Floor Plan. b) Revision 1 (Current Rev. No. 1, in a triangle). c) EPP (the drafter's initials). d) A bar (a graphic scale). It is marked 0, 500 and 5000. There is no ratio in the title block.

Task 4 (level 2)

a) 2022-05-06 b) 2021-11-26. The E-000 dates are year, month, day with two digits each: 21 = 2021, 11 = November, 26 = the day. c) 1999-11-08 d) November 8, 1999 (1999-11-08). The same title block says "Nov 8/99" in the revision line and in the Print box, and "08 November 99" at the top. So 08/11/99 is written day/month/year.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) 2 clouds. b) 1 in both deltas. c) In the revision block of the title block (Figure 5): "1 Nov 8/99 Issued for Construction." The "Current Rev. No." box also shows 1. d) One cloud is around three square symbols beside room 206 (JAN.). The other is around the note "2-HOUR RATED FIRE SEPARATION" near room 202.

Task 6 (level 2)

a) 2022-05-06 (A-201 writes it 2022.05.06, E-000 writes it 22 05 06). b) Issue 8 is not listed on E-000. c) No. Issue 7 is "Issued for Tender". The latest issue on the sheet is issue 9, "Issued for IFC" (issued for construction). The electrician needs the issue 9 print.

Task 7 (level 2)

a) A3-02, A3-03 and A3-04 (Wall Sections). b) A5-01 (Door Schedule). c) 13 structural sheets: S-101, S-102, S-201 to S-204, and S-301 to S-307. d) M09, M10 and M11, written by hand under M08.

Task 8 (level 2)

a) Millimetres ("All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise noted"). b) "No dimension shall be taken (scaled) from the drawings." c) To stud face, or to the face of concrete, unless otherwise noted. d) Not in Contract (note 5).

Task 9 (level 3)

a) M06 and M07 have the same name, "Second Floor Plan Plumbing". M05 is "Foundation Plan Plumbing" and M08 is "Third Floor Plan Plumbing". b) The ground floor (main floor) plumbing plan does not appear by name. One of M06 or M07 may really be the ground floor, but the list cannot tell you which. Also, M09 to M11 were added by hand with no names, so one of them could hold plumbing work too. c) Do not guess. Open M05 to M11 and read each title block, because the title block on the sheet is the real record. Then ask the architect or engineer in writing (an RFI) to confirm which sheet is the ground floor plumbing plan and to issue a corrected drawing list. Fire Hall general note 2 says to report any discrepancy to the architect before construction starts.

Points a good answer covers: names the duplicate, says why the list alone is not enough, checks the title blocks, asks in writing.

Task 10 (level 3)

a) Issue 6, 2022.02.02, "Issued for IFT Addendum No. 4" (an addendum during tender). b) Each sheet keeps its own issue history. A-201 was sent out again with Addendum No. 4. A-001 was not part of that addendum, so it has one less line. The issue numbers on two sheets do not have to match. c) A sheet can be changed and reissued on its own. The cover sheet or one other sheet does not prove that the sheet in your hand is current. Check the issue block on every sheet you use.

Task 11 (level 3)

a) Scale: "As indicated". The Graphic Scale box is empty. b) No. There is no view title or scale under the plan on A-201. "As indicated" points to a scale that is not shown. c) Do not measure it with a ruler. The note in the title block says "Do no scale drawings" and that contractors "shall verify and be responsible for all dimensions". A half-size print is not at any printed scale anyway. Use the written dimensions on the plan (for example the gym strip between grids 1 and 4 is dimensioned 19500). If a needed dimension is missing, ask the architect in writing.

Check yourself 1

The discipline, or trade, the sheet belongs to: for example A architectural, S structural, M mechanical, E electrical.

Check yourself 2

Every office numbers its sheets its own way. A-401 is a section in one set and an elevation in another. The drawing list tells you for sure.

Check yourself 3

Any five of: project name, firm, sheet title, sheet number, scale, drawn by, checked by, date, plot date, project number, issue or revision block.

Check yourself 4

The cloud shows the area that changed. The delta shows which revision changed it. The reason and date are in the matching line of the revision (issue) block in the title block.

Task 12 (level 2)

These answers go with the section "Extra: what a CAD drawing is". The CAD facts were checked against Autodesk's AutoCAD help on 2026-11-08: "About Model Space and Paper Space" (https://help.autodesk.com/view/ACD/2027/ENU/?guid=GUID-990538B6-DDA1-4190-BCC0-BB5BA94C9879), "Layers" (https://help.autodesk.com/view/ACD/2027/ENU/?guid=GUID-FA005756-B8F5-4A78-988F-31335A68D77C), "About Layers" (https://help.autodesk.com/view/ACD/2027/ENU/?guid=GUID-6B3E3B5D-3AE2-4162-A5FE-CFE42AB0743B), "About Attaching and Detaching Referenced Drawings (Xrefs)" (https://help.autodesk.com/view/ACD/2027/ENU/?guid=GUID-A987D2FF-45BD-474E-99C1-E6316A42F667) and the AutoCAD ActiveX reference "Block Object" (https://help.autodesk.com/view/OARX/2027/ENU/?guid=GUID-E6F7B03B-F5CC-4A18-9C48-BBF1D32A31FD), which describes a simple block as a collection of objects associated together to form a single object.

a) No. The stair is drawn once, at full size, in model space. The sheet is a layout in paper space with two viewports. One viewport shows the floor at 1:100, and the other shows the stair at 1:20.

b) Layers. The doors may be on a layer that was turned off or frozen when the sheet was printed. The electrician should not assume there are no doors. Check the architectural floor plan for the same floor, and ask the foreman or send an RFI if the sheets do not agree.

c) Blocks. The receptacle symbol is one block, inserted many times.

d) An xref. The architect's floor plan is linked into the electrical drawing, so the moved wall showed up when the electrical file was opened again. Points a good answer covers: - The print is not an issued revision. The issue block did not change, and there may be no revision cloud. - The electrical design (receptacles, lights, circuits) may not have been changed to suit the new wall yet. - Build only from the sheet issued for construction, and ask the foreman which issue is current if prints do not agree.
