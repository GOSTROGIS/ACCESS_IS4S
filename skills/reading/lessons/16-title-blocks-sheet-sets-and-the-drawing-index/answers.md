## Task 9 (level 3)

a) **M06 and M07 have the same name, "Second Floor Plan Plumbing".** M05 is "Foundation Plan Plumbing" and M08 is "Third Floor Plan Plumbing".
b) The **ground floor (main floor)** plumbing plan does not appear by name. One of M06 or M07 may really be the ground floor, but the list cannot tell you which. Also, M09 to M11 were added by hand with no names, so one of them could hold plumbing work too.
c) **Do not guess.** Open M05 to M11 and read each title block, because the title block on the sheet is the real record. Then ask the architect or engineer in writing (an RFI) to confirm which sheet is the ground floor plumbing plan and to issue a corrected drawing list. Fire Hall general note 2 says to report any discrepancy to the architect before construction starts.

Points a good answer covers: names the duplicate, says why the list alone is not enough, checks the title blocks, asks in writing.

## Task 10 (level 3)

a) **Issue 6, 2022.02.02, "Issued for IFT Addendum No. 4"** (an addendum during tender).
b) **Each sheet keeps its own issue history.** A-201 was sent out again with Addendum No. 4. A-001 was not part of that addendum, so it has one less line. The issue numbers on two sheets do not have to match.
c) A sheet can be changed and reissued on its own. The cover sheet or one other sheet does not prove that the sheet in your hand is current. Check the issue block on every sheet you use.

## Task 11 (level 3)

a) **Scale: "As indicated".** The **Graphic Scale box is empty.**
b) **No.** There is no view title or scale under the plan on A-201. "As indicated" points to a scale that is not shown.
c) **Do not measure it with a ruler.** The note in the title block says "Do no scale drawings" and that contractors "shall verify and be responsible for all dimensions". A half-size print is not at any printed scale anyway. Use the written dimensions on the plan (for example the gym strip between grids 1 and 4 is dimensioned 19500). If a needed dimension is missing, ask the architect in writing.
