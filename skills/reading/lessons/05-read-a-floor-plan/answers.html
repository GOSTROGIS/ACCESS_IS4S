Read a floor plan and its notes

Use the title block, legend, schedules, notes and detail references on a construction drawing to find what to build and where.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the information; Make connections between different parts of the document(s); Understand and apply the information; Evaluate the document(s)

Task 1 (level 1)

a) Drawing number A2.1, scale 1:50. b) Revision 2, dated 2026-10-20. c) The width of door D2 changed from 865 to 915 (millimetres).

Task 2 (level 1)

a) Room 104 has 2 WCs. Room 105 has 1 WC (and 1 urinal). b) 3 LAVs in total: 2 in room 104 and 1 in room 105. c) Room 103. d) The top wall of room 105 on the plan. The north arrow points to the top of the page.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) 1800 mm above finished floor (room finish schedule and note 2, "Wall tile to 1800 AFF TYP"). b) 1200 mm above finished floor (note 2: "Behind each LAV, tile to 1200 AFF only"). c) Detail 1 on drawing A5.1, shown on the plan as (1/A5.1) next to each LAV and named in note 2.

Steps: the schedule sends you to note 2, and note 2 sends you to the detail. A learner who stops at the schedule misses the exception behind the sinks.

Task 4 (level 2)

a) 2250 mm. 45 × 50 = 2250. This matches the written dimension on the plan. b) Trust the written dimension, 2250 mm. General note 1 says "Do not scale this drawing. Use the written dimensions." A reduced photocopy is no longer at 1:50, so ruler measurements on it are wrong. Note 3 also says to check dimensions on site before ordering.

Task 5 (level 2)

5.4 m²

Steps: 1. Tile height from the schedule: 1800 mm = 1.8 m. 2. Wall length: 3000 mm = 3.0 m. 3. Area = length × height = 3.0 × 1.8 = 5.4 m².

Note 2's exception only applies behind a LAV. The east wall has no fixtures, so the full 1800 mm height applies.

Task 6 (level 2)

No. Revision 2 in the title block changed D2 from 865 to 915 mm wide, and the door schedule shows D2 at 915 × 2135. The supplier priced the old revision. Send the supplier the current revision.

Task 7 (level 2)

P&MG means patch and make good: repair the damaged finishes and refinish them to match. Note 4 says it applies to all finishes affected by the work, including the corridor walls at the new doors.

Task 8 (level 3)

Yes, there is a conflict.

  • Note 2 says the mirror goes above the tile, and the tile behind each LAV stops at 1200 mm AFF.
  • Detail 1 on A5.1 puts the bottom of the mirror at 1050 mm AFF. That is 150 mm (1200 − 1050) below the top of the tile, so the mirror would sit over the tile, not above it.

What to do: do not guess. Stop before setting the tile and mirror at that wall. Tell your supervisor and ask the consultant in writing which height is correct. On many jobs this written question is called an RFI (request for information). Note 5 is about drawings and specifications not agreeing. Two parts of the drawings not agreeing calls for the same careful step: ask in writing and keep the answer.

Points a good response covers: names both sources, gives both numbers, explains the overlap, and gives a safe action that involves asking in writing.
