# Read on a screen and check the source

Session 10 of 26 in the Reading lessons · 75 minutes

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners to Reading lesson 10. Ask: "When you need to know something on the job, what do you do first?" Many will say "Google it" or "ask my phone". Ask: "How do you know the answer is right?" Tell a short story: a crew finds a post online saying a product is safe to use indoors. The post was about a different product from another country. The SDS for theirs says use with good ventilation. Today is about reading on a screen and checking the source.

This is session 10 of 26 in the Reading lessons. It runs 75 minutes.

Before the session: check the Wi-Fi, and make sure each learner or pair has a phone, tablet or computer. Open the three official pages used in the tasks on your own device to confirm they still load and still say what the answer key says.

## Objective (3 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- find a word or table on a web page using zoom and find on page (Identify the information)
- judge the source, date, evidence and purpose of online information (Evaluate the document(s))
- explain who a page is written for and how it tries to get the reader to act (Reflect on the document(s))

## Pre-assessment (5 min)

1. "Show me how you would find one word on a long web page on your phone."
2. "Name one website you trust for workplace safety rules in BC, and say why."

## Post-assessment (6 min)

1. "Find on page: on the WorkSafeBC WHMIS 2015 page, find the word 'decanted'. Read the sentence it is in."
2. "Give two signs that a web page might not be trustworthy."
3. "Why is an official page from 2010 not always the right source today?"

## Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

- zoom, sideways view and find on page
- checking who published a page, using the web address
- checking the date and version
- checking the evidence and the purpose
- treating summaries and posts as leads, not sources
- never logging in from a link you were sent
- the Reading lessons as a set: purpose, questions, instructions, tables, drawings, codes, labels, manuals, policies and screens

Total time: 5 + 3 + 5 + 52 + 6 + 4 = 75 minutes.
