# Find it in a table, log or form

## Task 1 (level 1)

a) By **bin number**, from B01 to B08.
b) **B03**
c) **25**

Steps: for b), scan the Size column for 1/2" × 3", then read across to the Bin column. For c), find row B02 and read across to Box quantity.

## Task 2 (level 1)

- Bolt: **B02** (3/8" × 2" stainless hex bolt)
- Nut: **B05** (3/8" stainless hex nut)
- Lock washer: **B08** (3/8" stainless lock washer)

Steps: two filters at once, size 3/8" and material stainless steel. The note confirms stainless is for outdoor railings. A learner who picks B01 or B04 has matched the size but not the material.

## Task 3 (level 2)

a) **8 hours.** Row Wed, column "C2 hours run today".
b) **486.** Tuesday's "C1 hours since last oil change" is 479. On Wednesday C1 ran 7 hours. 479 + 7 = 486. Check with Thursday: 486 + 9 = 495, which matches the table.
c) **C2 was not run that day**, so nobody checked the oil. The note under the table says what the dash means. It does not mean the oil was low.

## Task 4 (level 2)

a) **Friday.** The title says change the oil every 500 hours. On Friday C1 reached 503 hours.
b) The **oil change is overdue** (503 is more than 500), and the **oil level is not OK** ("No"). Both need action. C1 should not keep running without the lead hand or maintenance being told.
c) **Tuesday.** The asterisk (*) beside "Yes" on Tuesday, and the note "Oil was topped up that day."
d) **C1, by 13 hours.** C1: 8 + 9 + 7 + 9 + 8 = 41 hours. C2: 6 + 7 + 8 + 0 + 7 = 28 hours. 41 − 28 = 13.

## Task 5 (level 2)

A correct form:

| *Date (YYYY-MM-DD) | *Job number | *Requested by (print name) | Crew (tick one) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | 26-114 | SAM WARD | ☑ Install |

| *Bin | *Item | *Size | *Quantity | *Unit (each or box) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B02 | Hex bolt, stainless | 3/8" × 2" | 2 | box |
| B05 | Hex nut, stainless | 3/8" | 2 | box |

| Needed by (YYYY-MM-DD) | *Approved by (lead hand signature) |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-09 | (left blank for Priya Desai to sign) |

Office use only: left blank.

Points a good response covers: the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format, the job number, a printed name, one crew box ticked, bin numbers that match the items, a unit for each quantity, and the approval box left for the lead hand. Sam does not sign for Priya and does not write in the office-use area.

## Task 6 (level 3)

Any five of these:

1. The **date is in the wrong format**. It should be 2026-11-06, not 11/06/26.
2. The **job number is missing**, and it is a required field.
3. The **name is not printed in full**. "J. Ross" may not be enough to identify the person. (Accept this if the learner explains it. The form asks for a printed name, and a full name is clearer.)
4. **Two crew boxes are ticked.** The form says tick one.
5. **The bin and the item do not match.** B02 is stainless steel, but the item says zinc. The office cannot tell which one the worker wants.
6. **The quantity has no unit.** 50 could mean 50 bolts or 50 boxes. 50 boxes from B02 would be 1,250 bolts.
7. **"ASAP" is not a date.** The field asks for YYYY-MM-DD.
8. **The approval signature is missing.** It is a required field.
9. **The worker wrote in the office-use area.**
