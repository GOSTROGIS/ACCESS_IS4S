Reading · Lesson 04 · Instructor

Find it in a table, log or form

Read lists, tables and logs by their titles, headings and notes, find the cell where a row meets a column, and fill in a work form correctly.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Identify the information; Make connections between different parts of the document(s); Understand and apply the information

Session 4 of 10 in the Reading lessons · 75 minutes

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Hold up any real table you have on hand: a bus timetable, a parts catalogue page or a pay stub. Ask: "Does anyone read this from the top left corner to the bottom right corner?" Then tell a short story: a worker grabs bolts from the bin next to the right one because the label looks the same. The railing they built rusts in a year. The skill today is landing on the right row and the right column every time.

This is session 4 of 10 in the Reading lessons. It runs 75 minutes.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • find a single item in a list or table using its title, headings and notes (Identify the information)
  • find a value where a row meets a column, and fill a blank cell from the cells around it (Make connections between different parts of the document(s))
  • fill in a work form correctly and find errors in a completed form (Understand and apply the information)

Pre-assessment (5 min)

  1. "What do you look at first when you open a table?"
  2. "On a form, what does 'Office use only' tell you?"

Post-assessment (8 min)

  1. "In Document B, find C1's hours since last oil change on Thursday. Which row and which column did you use?"
  2. "Why must the bin number and the item description on a request form match?"
  3. "Look at the bus timetable or parts page from the opening. Find one item in it and tell your partner the title, the heading and the row you used."

Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

  • simple lists and how they are ordered
  • reading a table: title, headings, notes, then the row
  • finding a value where a row meets a column, and filling a blank cell
  • reading and filling in a form: required fields, formats, signatures and office-use areas

Total time: 5 + 2 + 5 + 50 + 8 + 5 = 75 minutes.
