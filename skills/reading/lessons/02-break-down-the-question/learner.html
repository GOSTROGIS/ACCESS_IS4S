Break down the question first

Use the question word, the key words and the limit words in a question to know exactly what answer to look for.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to read; Identify the information; Understand and apply the information

Why this matters on the job

A lead hand asks, "How many of the new hires have been signed off on the lift?" A wrong answer here can put an untrained worker on a machine. Many reading mistakes happen before you even look at the document, because the question was not clear in your head. A few seconds spent breaking down the question saves time and gets the right answer.

Key ideas

1. The question word tells you what kind of answer to look for.

Question word Look for
Who a person, a role or a group
What a thing, an action or an event
Where a place or a location
When a time, a date or a condition ("when the gate does not close")
Why a reason
How a way or a method
How many / How much a number or an amount
How often a frequency, such as daily or every 30 days
Which one choice out of several

2. Key words carry the meaning. Underline them. Cross out the words that only hold the sentence together. If the sentence still makes sense with only the key words, you found them.

~~What should~~ you do ~~if the~~ entry gate ~~on the lift~~ does not close ~~by itself~~?

3. Given and requested. The question gives you some information (the given) and asks for other information (the requested). The given tells you where to look. The requested tells you when to stop.

4. Limit words narrow the answer. Watch for only, except, not, before, after, first, last, at least, no more than, both, either.

5. The document may use a different word. The question says "inspect", but the document says "check". The question says "number", but the document says "quantity". If you cannot find your key word, think of other words that mean the same thing.

Worked example

Question: Where must the lift be charged?

  • Question word: where, so look for a place.
  • Key words: lift, charged.
  • Given: it is about charging the lift.
  • Requested: the place.
  • Scan the document for "charge". Item 4 says: "Charge it in the charging bay only."
  • Limit word: only. So the answer is the charging bay, and nowhere else.

Practice document

Northline Fabrication Ltd. (fictional company) Shop Procedure SP-07: Scissor lift pre-use check · Revision B

Do this check at the start of every shift before you raise the platform. Only workers trained on this model may operate it. The shop supervisor keeps the training records. The lead hand does not.

  1. Guardrails and gate. Check the platform guardrails and the entry gate. The gate must close and latch by itself. If it does not, tag the lift DO NOT USE and tell the lead hand.
  2. Tires and wheels. Look for cuts, bulges and loose wheel nuts.
  3. Hydraulic fluid. Check the level with the platform fully lowered. Top up only with the fluid named on the decal on the machine.
  4. Battery. Look at the charge indicator. Do not start work if fewer than 2 bars show. Charge it in the charging bay only.
  5. Emergency stops. Test the emergency stop button at the platform controls and at the base controls. Both must stop all movement.
  6. Emergency lowering. Test the emergency lowering valve at the base.
  7. Record. Write the check on the daily log card in the holder on the base. Print your full name, sign, and write the date.

If any item fails, do not use the lift. Tag it and tell the lead hand. The lead hand books the repair.

Practice

Task 1 (level 1) For each question, write the question word and the kind of answer you will look for.

Question Question word Kind of answer
a) Who keeps the training records?
b) When should you do the pre-use check?
c) How many bars must show before you start work?
d) Where is the daily log card kept?
e) Why do you think the platform must be fully lowered before you check the fluid?

Task 2 (level 1) Cross out the supporting words and leave only the key words. a) What should you do if a tire on the lift has a deep cut in it? b) Which fluid can you use to top up the hydraulic tank?

Task 3 (level 2) For each question, write the given and the requested. Then answer it from the procedure.

a) If the gate does not latch by itself, what tag goes on the lift? Given: ___ Requested: ___ Answer: _____

b) What three things must you write on the daily log card? Given: ___ Requested: ___ Answer: _____

c) Who books the repair when an item fails? Given: ___ Requested: ___ Answer: _____

Task 4 (level 2) A new worker asks you: "Who do I ask to find out if I've been signed off on this lift?" The words "signed off" are not in the procedure. Which words in the procedure mean the same thing, and what is the answer?

Task 5 (level 2) Use the limit words to answer. a) The charging bay is full. Can you charge the lift at your workstation? b) The emergency stop works at the base controls but not at the platform controls. Can you use the lift? c) The indicator shows exactly 2 bars. Can you start work?

Task 6 (level 1) Here is an old riddle. Read the question at the end very carefully.

As I was going to St Ives, I met a man with seven wives. Each wife had seven sacks, each sack had seven cats, each cat had seven kits. Kits, cats, sacks, wives: how many were going to St Ives?

What exactly does the question ask? What is your answer, and why might someone who starts multiplying get a different one?

Task 7 (level 2) Each answer below does not match its question. Say what is wrong, then write an answer that does match. Use the procedure where you can.

a) Q: When did you last test the emergency stops? A: The red button is on the base. b) Q: Why is the lift tagged? A: The lead hand tagged it. c) Q: How many workers are trained on this model? A: The shop supervisor keeps that.

Check yourself

  1. What kind of answer does a "how often" question ask for?
  2. What is the difference between the given and the requested?
  3. You scan for the word "inspect" and cannot find it. What do you try next?
  4. Name three limit words.
