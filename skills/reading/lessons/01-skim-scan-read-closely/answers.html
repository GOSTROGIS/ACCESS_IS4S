Reading · Lesson 01 · Answers

Skim, scan and read closely

Decide why you are reading, then choose the fastest way to find what you need in a workplace safety document.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to read; Identify the information

Task 1 (level 1)

a) Nine numbered headings (1 to 9). b) Site supervisors on Kestrel Point job sites. The title says "for Site Supervisors" and heading 1 repeats it. c) Version 3, issued March 2026. It replaces Version 2.

Steps: read only the title block and the bold headings. Count the numbers in front of the headings. A learner who read the whole text in one minute has not skimmed. Talk about what they could have skipped.

Task 2 (level 1)

48 hours. It is under heading 4, Preliminary investigation.

Steps: good key words to scan for are "preliminary", "hours" or any number. A learner who scanned for "48" already guessed the answer. Ask what they would scan for if they did not know the number.

Task 3 (level 1)

Any three of these:

  • where people, equipment and materials were
  • anything broken, bent or out of place
  • marks on the ground, such as tire tracks or drag marks
  • spills, wet spots or loose material on walking surfaces
  • lighting, noise and weather at the time
  • guards, rails and other safety devices, and whether they were in place

Steps: scan for "mapping" or "sketch", locate heading 6, read the list.

Task 4 (level 2)

Yes, it counts. Heading 2 says an incident is also "any event that did not hurt anyone … but could have caused a serious injury. We call that a near miss." A trip at the top of a stairwell could have caused a fall down the stairs, which could be a serious injury.

Steps: scan for "incident", locate heading 2, read both sentences closely. The word "also" tells you there is a second kind of incident. A learner who answers "No, she was not hurt" stopped after the first sentence. Send them back to the next one.

Task 5 (level 2)

a) Skim. You want the general idea and where things are. b) Scan. You know you want a form number, so scan for "form" or "KP". The answer is KP-12, under heading 4. c) Scan, then read closely. Scan for "full investigation", then read heading 5 word by word, because both "who" and "what you hand over" matter.

A good reason names the purpose, for example: "I skim first because I only need to know what the guide covers, not the details."

Task 6 (level 2)

  • The preliminary investigation report must be completed within 48 hours, not a week (heading 4).
  • The safety coordinator submits the full investigation report, not the supervisor. The supervisor gives the safety coordinator notes, sketches and photos (heading 5). The full report is due within 30 days.

Steps: break the statement into its two claims, then scan for each one separately.

Check yourself 1

"I am reading this so that I can…"

Check yourself 2

Scan. You know you are looking for a phone number, so scan for digits or the word "phone".

Check yourself 3

Any three of: must, only, not, within, before, unless. Other correct answers include except, after, never and always.

Check yourself 4

Scan, locate, decide. Scan for the key word, locate the sentence that holds it, decide whether it answers the question.
