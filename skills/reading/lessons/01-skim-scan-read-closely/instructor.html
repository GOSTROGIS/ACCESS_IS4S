Reading · Lesson 01 · Instructor

Skim, scan and read closely

Decide why you are reading, then choose the fastest way to find what you need in a workplace safety document.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to read; Identify the information

Session 1 of 26 in the Reading lessons · 60 minutes

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Ask: "When was the last time someone handed you a sheet at work and said, 'Read this'? Did you read every word?" Take two or three answers. Tell a short story: a new hire is given a ten-page site safety package on day one and told to sign it before lunch. Most people flip through it. The skill today is how to flip through on purpose, so that the one rule that matters to you is not missed.

This is session 1 of 26 in the Reading lessons. It runs 60 minutes.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • state the purpose of a reading task in one sentence (Identify the task that requires you to read)
  • choose to skim, scan or read closely, and say why (Identify the information)
  • use scan, locate, decide to pull one correct answer out of a workplace safety document (Identify the information)

Pre-assessment (5 min)

  1. "What is the difference between skimming and scanning?" Many learners use the two words the same way. Note who can tell them apart.
  2. Hold up the practice guide for ten seconds, then turn it over. "What do you think this document is for, and who is it for?"

Learning tasks (38 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Write "I am reading this so that I can…" on the board. Give three job situations (checking a site rule, finding a phone number, reading a new procedure) and have learners finish the sentence for each. Finish the purpose sentence aloud or on paper for each situation. Board 5 min
1.2 Show the table of three ways of reading. Model skimming the practice guide aloud: say each heading as you read it and what it tells you. Follow along and mark each heading they hear. Learner page, Key ideas and Practice document 5 min
1.3 Time one minute for Task 1. Have learners cover the guide before answering. Skim for one minute, cover the guide, answer Task 1. Learner page, Task 1, a timer 4 min
2.1 Model scan, locate, decide with the worked example. Then set Tasks 2 and 3. Ask learners to say the key word or number they held in mind. Do Tasks 2 and 3. Say the key word they scanned for. Learner page, Tasks 2 and 3 8 min
2.2 Set Task 4. Ask learners to underline the exact words that decide the answer. Draw attention to "could have caused a serious injury". Do Task 4. Underline the deciding words and compare with a partner. Learner page, Task 4 6 min
3.1 Set Tasks 5 and 6 in pairs. Do Tasks 5 and 6 in pairs. Learner page, Tasks 5 and 6 6 min
3.2 Go over the answers. For each, ask "Where did you find it?" before "What is it?" Check answers and point to the source line for each. answers.md 4 min

Post-assessment (6 min)

  1. "Your supervisor hands you a three-page notice and asks what time the crane arrives. Which way of reading do you use first, and what key word do you scan for?"
  2. "Name the three steps of scan, locate, decide."
  3. "Give one word that tells you to slow down and read closely, and explain why."

Summary (4 min)

During this session we covered:

  • finishing the sentence "I am reading this so that I can…" before reading
  • the difference between skimming, scanning and reading closely
  • the ACCESS routine: scan, locate, decide
  • small words that change meaning: must, only, not, within, before, unless

Total time: 5 + 2 + 5 + 38 + 6 + 4 = 60 minutes.
