## Task 5 (level 3)

A sample reply:

> Thanks for asking. The ramp handrail is not in our scope. It is listed as an exclusion in section 3 of subcontract SC-14. Section 6 says any extra work needs a written change order signed by Kestrel Point's project manager before we start. If you want it done, please ask your PM to send one, and our office will price it.

Points a good reply covers:
- polite tone, no refusal in anger
- names the section (section 3 exclusion, section 6 changes)
- says what is needed: a written change order
- says who signs: Kestrel Point's project manager
- says it happens **before** the work starts

## Task 6 (level 3)

Any sensible gap with a reason. For example:
- **No dates.** The scope does not say when shop drawings are due, when install day is or how long Kestrel Point has to review the drawings. A late review could delay the job, and nobody agreed who pays.
- **Who supplies the anchors** is not stated in words. Item 2.5 says "install and anchor", which suggests Fraser Bend does, but it is worth confirming.
- **Lifting and access equipment.** It does not say who supplies a lift or scaffold to reach the mezzanine edge.
- **What happens if the superintendent will not sign** the checklist, or how long they have to do the walk-through.
- **Ramp handrails** are excluded, but nobody is named to do them.
