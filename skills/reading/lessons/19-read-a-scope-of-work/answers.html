Read a scope of work

Find what the crew must do, what is left out and when the work counts as done, and spot extra work that was never in the scope.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the information; Make connections between different parts of the document(s); Understand and apply the information; Evaluate the document(s)

Task 1 (level 1)

a) Fraser Bend Metalworks Ltd. does the work. Kestrel Point Builders Ltd., the general contractor, hired them. Both names are at the top of Document A.

b) Add the three lengths in section 2: - handrail 4.2 m (item 2.2) - handrail 3.6 m (item 2.2) - guard 6.0 m (item 2.3)

4.2 + 3.6 + 6.0 = 13.8 m

Task 2 (level 1)

Any two of these, from section 3:

Exclusion Who does it
Finish paint The painting contractor ("by others")
Building permit The owner
Removing the existing wood railing Kestrel Point
Structural design of the stair and mezzanine The owner's engineer
Handrails at the exterior ramp Not in Fraser Bend's work. The scope does not say who does them.

Task 3 (level 2)

Message Group Section that tells you
a) Handrail on the outside ramp Out of scope Section 3 excludes handrails at the exterior ramp. Section 6 says it needs a signed change order first.
b) Primer touch-ups done by Thursday In scope Item 2.6, touch up primer on site.
c) Stair treads not set, cannot anchor until Monday Assumption problem Section 4: "The stair and mezzanine are complete and ready for anchors on install day." Tell your supervisor the same day.
d) Throw the old wood railing in your bin Out of scope Section 3: removing the existing wood railing is "By Kestrel Point". Item 2.7 covers only Fraser Bend's own scrap.
e) Paint it black Out of scope Section 3: finish paint is by others. Item 2.4 says grey primer only.
f) Sweep up your cut-offs tonight In scope Item 2.7, remove our own cut-offs, scrap and packaging at the end of each day.

Task 4 (level 2)

No, not yet. Four of the five acceptance items may be met, but section 5 also says the site superintendent must walk the work and sign the checklist. Until that happens on Friday, the work is installed but not accepted. The crew should also check the first item: installed as shown on the reviewed shop drawings.

Task 5 (level 3)

A sample reply:

Thanks for asking. The ramp handrail is not in our scope. It is listed as an exclusion in section 3 of subcontract SC-14. Section 6 says any extra work needs a written change order signed by Kestrel Point's project manager before we start. If you want it done, please ask your PM to send one, and our office will price it.

Points a good reply covers: - polite tone, no refusal in anger - names the section (section 3 exclusion, section 6 changes) - says what is needed: a written change order - says who signs: Kestrel Point's project manager - says it happens before the work starts

Task 6 (level 3)

Any sensible gap with a reason. For example: - No dates. The scope does not say when shop drawings are due, when install day is or how long Kestrel Point has to review the drawings. A late review could delay the job, and nobody agreed who pays. - Who supplies the anchors is not stated in words. Item 2.5 says "install and anchor", which suggests Fraser Bend does, but it is worth confirming. - Lifting and access equipment. It does not say who supplies a lift or scaffold to reach the mezzanine edge. - What happens if the superintendent will not sign the checklist, or how long they have to do the walk-through. - Ramp handrails are excluded, but nobody is named to do them.

Check yourself 1

Another trade or company does that part of the work. Your crew does not.

Check yourself 2

One item on the "done when" list. All of them must be met before the work is accepted.

Check yourself 3

A written change order signed by the person named in the scope (here, Kestrel Point's project manager), before the work starts.

Check yourself 4

Because the price depends on them. If one is false (the stair is not ready, there is no power), the schedule or the price may change, and your supervisor needs to know.
