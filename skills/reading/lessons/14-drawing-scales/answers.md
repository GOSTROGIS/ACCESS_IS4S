## Task 4 (level 2)

**22'-6"**

Steps: at 1/4" = 1'-0", multiply by 4. 5 5/8 × 4 = 22 1/2 feet. 1/2 foot = 6 inches.

## Task 5 (level 2)

a) **11'-6".** At 1/2" = 1'-0", multiply by 2. 5 3/4 × 2 = 11 1/2 feet.
b) **3'-0".** At 3/4" = 1'-0", multiply by 4/3. 2 1/4 × 4/3 = 3 feet.
c) **2'-6".** At 3" = 1'-0", multiply by 1/3 (divide by 3). 7 1/2 ÷ 3 = 2 1/2 feet.

## Task 6 (level 2)

b) **28 mm.** 140 ÷ 5 = 28.
c) **42 mm.** 4200 ÷ 100 = 42.

## Task 7 (level 2)

## Task 9 (level 3)

c) Check the same sizes on the plan and other details. If they still do not agree, tell the foreman and ask the designer **in writing** (an RFI) before building.
