Write sentences that say who does what

Build clear sentences with a doer, an action and an object, choose active or passive voice on purpose, and keep pronouns and verb tenses from confusing the reader.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task; Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

Task 1 (level 1)

Subject Verb Object
a The millwright aligned the coupling
b Our crew will frame the east wall (tomorrow tells when)
c The supplier delivered forty studs
d Maria is grinding the welds (on the handrail tells where)
e The inspector checked the firestopping (in Room 204 tells where)

Steps: find the action word first (verb). Ask "who did it?" for the subject and "did it to what?" for the object. Words that tell when or where are extra detail, not the object.

Task 2 (level 1)

Any answer that removes the double meaning is correct. Samples:

a) "The pipe rolled into the trench box, and the pipe was dented." (or "...the trench box was dented") b) "Jordan told Lee, 'I left the van keys in the shop.'" (or "...'You left the van keys...'") c) "Put the extension cord away." (or "Put the grinder away", or "Put both away") d) "The client needs an answer about the invoice by Friday." (or name the schedule, or both)

Steps: find the pronoun, list the things it could mean, replace it with the one you mean.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Passive: "The guard was left off the bench grinder (by the apprentice)." b) Active: "The foreman inspected the scaffold this morning." c) Passive: "The barricade was moved." d) Active: "Two labourers entered the trench before it was shored." (A full active version is "...before anyone shored it.") e) Passive: "The saw was tagged out of service (by me)." f) Active: "Priya signed for the delivery."

For an incident report, the active versions are usually better, because they say who did each action. The passive "The barricade was moved" is acceptable only if nobody knows who moved it, and the report should then say "Not known who moved the barricade."

Task 4 (level 2)

Corrected:

At 8:00 am I checked the tank level and it was low. I called the supplier. The trucks were coming at noon. One of the fill caps was cracked, so I tagged it and told the foreman.

The five errors: check to checked, call to called, is to were (plural doer "trucks", past tense), are to was (doer is "one"), tag to tagged.

Task 5 (level 2)

Sample answer:

At 1:15 pm Darren switched off breaker 12. At 1:17 pm he opened panel P2. At 1:18 pm Kim started pulling wire. Darren put his lock on breaker 12 at 1:25 pm, after the work had started.

A good answer: names each doer, keeps past tense, keeps the times in order, and shows that the lock went on after the work started without adding words like "careless" that the facts do not support. "He" is clear here because Darren is the only person in the sentence before it.

Check yourself

  1. Subject: the apprentice. Verb: swept. Object: the shop floor (level 1).
  2. "The carpenters stripped the forms." (level 1)
  3. When the doer is unknown or does not matter, for example "The slab was poured on 2026-10-14." Procedures and descriptions of what happened to a thing are other examples (level 2).
  4. "One of the welds is cracked." The doer is "one", which is singular, so the verb is singular (level 1).
  5. "It" can point to more than one thing in the sentence, so the reader may guess wrong about what was damaged, moved or needs action (level 1).
