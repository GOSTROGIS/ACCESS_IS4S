# Take meeting notes and keep an issue log

## Why this matters on the job

On most jobs the trades meet once a week, or every morning, to sort out who goes where and what is in the way. People leave the meeting sure they agreed. Three days later the framer thinks the electrician was calling the inspector, and the electrician thinks the super was. Good notes fix that. They say what was decided, who will do what by when, and which problems are still open. An issue log keeps those open problems on one page until someone closes them. Taking the notes is often a job for an apprentice or a lead hand, and it is a good way to be noticed.

## Key ideas

### 1. Notes are not a word-for-word record

You do not write down everything people say. You listen for three things:

| Listen for | It sounds like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision**: something agreed | "OK, let's do that." "Drywall starts Thursday." | Drywall starts Thursday, November 19. |
| **Action item**: someone will do something | "I'll call them." "Can you book it?" | Leo Brandt to call the door supplier today. |
| **Issue**: a problem not solved in the meeting | "Nobody knows yet." "That's still a problem." | The back door does not lock. |

Jokes, stories and repeated points do not go in the notes.

### 2. The header

> **Meeting:** Weekly coordination, Unit 104 fit-out
> **Date and time:** 2026-11-16, 7:30 am
> **Place:** Site office
> **Present:** Leo Brandt (GC project manager), Tasha Bell (framing foreman), Mina Cho (electrician), Kofi Mensah (drywall foreman)
> **Notes by:** your name

### 3. Every action item has one owner and a due date

| Weak | Clear |
|---|---|
| Someone will look at the door. | Leo Brandt: call the door supplier about the back door lock, by end of day Monday, November 16. |
| The crew to clean up. | Tasha Bell: clear the framing offcuts from Room 105, by noon Tuesday, November 17. |

"Everyone", "the crew" and "someone" are not owners. Write a person's name. If nobody takes it, write it as an issue.

### 4. Open problems go into an issue log

An issue log is a table that lists every open problem on the job:

| No. | Date raised | Issue | Raised by | Owner | Due | Status | Update |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **No.**: give each issue a number and never reuse it.
- **Owner**: one person who will get it closed.
- **Status**: Open, In progress or Closed.
- **Update**: what changed, and when.

### 5. Keep the log alive

- Update it after every meeting, and when a text or call changes something.
- Never erase an issue. When it is fixed, mark it Closed and write the date.
- Bring the log to the next meeting and go through the open issues first.

### 6. Send the notes the same day

Send or post the notes the day of the meeting. End with: "Please tell me by noon tomorrow if anything here is wrong." Now nobody can say later that they did not agree.

### Worked example

**Weekly coordination meeting, Unit 104 fit-out at Brightwater Plaza** (a fictional job). What was said:

> **Leo (GC project manager):** Morning. Three things. First, drywall delivery is Wednesday at 7. Kofi, can your crew start Thursday?
> **Kofi (drywall foreman):** Yes, if the framing in Room 105 is done.
> **Tasha (framing foreman):** 105 will be done Tuesday by noon.
> **Leo:** Good. Second, electrical rough-in inspection. Mina?
> **Mina (electrician):** Booked for Tuesday at 1 pm. I need the ceiling in 105 left open till then.
> **Kofi:** We won't board 105 before Thursday anyway.
> **Leo:** Third, the back door still won't lock.
> **Tasha:** That's not ours. The door supplier installed it.
> **Leo:** I'll call them today. And coffee's on me Friday.

The notes:

> **Meeting:** Weekly coordination, Unit 104 fit-out **Date and time:** 2026-11-16, 7:30 am **Place:** Site office
> **Present:** Leo Brandt, Tasha Bell, Mina Cho, Kofi Mensah **Notes by:** Jordan Paul
>
> **Decisions**
> 1. Drywall delivery Wednesday, November 18, at 7:00 am. Drywall starts Thursday, November 19.
> 2. The Room 105 ceiling stays open until the electrical inspection on Tuesday, November 17.
>
> **Action items**
>
> | Action | Owner | Due |
> |---|---|---|
> | Finish framing in Room 105 | Tasha Bell | Tue Nov 17, noon |
> | Electrical rough-in inspection | Mina Cho | Tue Nov 17, 1:00 pm |
> | Call the door supplier about the back door lock | Leo Brandt | Mon Nov 16 |
>
> **Open issues:** see issue log, No. 1.
>
> Please tell me by noon tomorrow if anything here is wrong.

The issue log:

| No. | Date raised | Issue | Raised by | Owner | Due | Status | Update |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-11-16 | Back door does not lock | Leo Brandt | Leo Brandt | 2026-11-17 | Open | |

The coffee on Friday did not go in the notes.
