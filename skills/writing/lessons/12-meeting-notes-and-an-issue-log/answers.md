# Take meeting notes and keep an issue log

## Task 1 (level 1)

1 X, 2 D, 3 D, 4 I, 5 A, 6 I, 7 A, 8 X, 9 I, 10 I, 11 A

Steps: a decision is agreed by the group (line 3 confirms what Kofi offered in line 2, so either line can carry the D). An action names a person who will do something. An issue is a problem that is not fixed yet, or news about one (line 9 updates issue 1). Line 1 only asks a question, and line 8 is chatter. Accept 4 as I and A together, because the inspector's request is both an issue and the start of Mina's action.

## Task 2 (level 2)

Sample:

> **Meeting:** Weekly coordination, Unit 104 fit-out **Date and time:** 2026-11-19, 7:30 am **Place:** Site office
> **Present:** Leo Brandt, Tasha Bell, Mina Cho, Kofi Mensah **Notes by:** (learner's name)
>
> **Decisions**
> 1. Drywall goes up in Room 104 on Thursday, November 19.
> 2. Drywall starts in Room 105 on Friday, November 20.
>
> **Action items**
>
> | Action | Owner | Due |
> |---|---|---|
> | Put a cover plate on the junction box above the back door | Mina Cho | Thu Nov 19 |
> | Put up a no-parking sign at the garbage bin | Leo Brandt | Fri Nov 20 |
> | Send an RFI to the architect about the Room 106 header | Leo Brandt | Thu Nov 19 |
>
> **Open issues:** see issue log, Nos. 1 to 4.
>
> Please tell me by noon tomorrow if anything here is wrong.

A good answer has the full header, both decisions, three actions each with one named owner and a date, and nothing about the game.

## Task 3 (level 2)

| No. | Date raised | Issue | Raised by | Owner | Due | Status | Update |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-11-16 | Back door does not lock | Leo Brandt | Leo Brandt | 2026-11-23 | In progress | 2026-11-19: supplier says the new lock comes Monday, November 23. |
| 2 | 2026-11-19 | Inspector wants a cover plate on the junction box above the back door | Mina Cho | Mina Cho | 2026-11-19 | Open | |
| 3 | 2026-11-19 | Vehicles parked in front of the garbage bin. Bin truck could not get in on November 18. | Tasha Bell | Leo Brandt | 2026-11-20 | Open | |
| 4 | 2026-11-19 | Room 106 header is 50 mm lower than the drawing. Framing in 106 stops without an answer. | Tasha Bell | Leo Brandt | 2026-11-20 | In progress | 2026-11-19: RFI to architect. |

A good answer keeps issue 1 on its original row and adds an update, numbers the new issues 2 to 4, gives each one owner and a due date, and writes facts with no blame (not "somebody keeps being careless"). For issue 4 the due date is before Monday, so Friday, November 20, is correct.

## Task 4 (level 2)

| No. | Status | Update added |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Closed 2026-11-23 | 2026-11-23: new lock installed and working (Tasha). |
| 2 | Closed 2026-11-19 | 2026-11-19, 3:10 pm: cover plate installed (Mina). |
| 3 | Closed 2026-11-20 | 2026-11-20: no-parking sign up at the bin (Leo). |
| 4 | Closed 2026-11-20 | 2026-11-20: architect says the header is OK as built (Leo). |

Steps: match each message to its issue number, add the update with its date and who sent it, and change the status to Closed with the date. No row is erased.

## Task 5 (level 1 to 2)

Samples:

a) "Leo Brandt: ask the bin company to move the pickup time, by Friday, November 20." (Added: an owner, what "sort out" means, a date.)
b) "Kofi Mensah: check that all drywall in Room 104 is screwed off before taping, by end of day Thursday, November 19." (Added: what to check, where, a date.)
c) "Tasha Bell: order 4 boxes of 1-5/8 in drywall screws for delivery by Monday, November 23." (Added: an owner, what kind, how many, when.)

Any owner and details are fine if the learner names one person, a clear action and a due date, and says what they added.

## Check yourself

1. Decisions, action items and issues (level 1).
2. A group cannot be followed up. If one person is not named, everyone thinks someone else will do it (level 1).
3. Mark it Closed with the date and a short update. Never erase it (level 1).
4. Ask readers to tell you by a set time if anything is wrong. Then nobody can say later that they did not agree (level 2).
5. Any four of: number, date raised, issue, raised by, owner, due, status, update (level 1).
