# Write steps and conditions a crew can follow

## Task 1 (level 2)

a) The conditions are:

- 18 or older
- lives in Surrey or Langley
- has a valid BC driver's licence **or** her own way to get to the Surrey and Langley shops
- has steel-toed boots

A fall protection course is not a condition to apply. If you have not taken one, the company provides it in the first week.

b) Getting to work. It can be met with a valid BC driver's licence or with your own way to get to both shops.

c) Yes. She is 19 (18 or older), lives in Langley, has her own way to get to both shops (the bus), and owns steel-toed boots. She does not need a licence because "or" means one of the two is enough. She does not need the fall protection course to apply, because the company will provide it.

Steps: underline "must" and "need", circle "and" and "or", then check the applicant against each condition one at a time.

## Task 2 (level 2)

Wording can differ if the meaning is the same.

a) If you do not have a permit, then do not do hot work. (Or: If you are going to do hot work, then you need a permit first.)
b) If the planer is running, then wear ear protection.
c) If you are a visitor, then sign in at the office.
d) If you see this sign, then do not enter, because it is a confined space.

## Task 3 (level 2)

a) False. Counter-example: brass screws, nylon cable ties, aluminum rivets or any fastener not made of steel.
b) True.
c) True. A square is a rectangle with four equal sides.
d) False. Counter-example: a rectangle 2 m by 4 m has sides that are not all equal, so it is not a square.
e) False. Counter-example: one apprentice who is 25 or older is enough to prove it false.

Steps: for "all" statements, look for one case that does not fit. For "some" statements, one case that fits is enough to make it true.

## Task 4 (level 3)

Sample answer:

> **End-of-shift procedure: Fraser Bend Fabrication**
>
> 1. Shut off your welder.
> 2. Close the gas cylinder valve.
> 3. If you used the plasma table and the dross tray is more than half full, empty the dross tray.
> 4. Put aluminum offcuts in the blue bin.
> 5. Put steel offcuts in the steel scrap bin.
> 6. Sweep your bay.
> 7. Coil the extension cords and hang them up.
> 8. If anything is broken, write it in the shop log.
> 9. If you are the last person out, turn off the compressor and lock the side door.

A good answer:

- has a title
- uses one action per step, each starting with a verb
- puts every condition (plasma table, aluminum, broken items, last person out) before its action
- keeps aluminum and steel separate
- puts the last-person steps at the end, where they happen in real life

Order of steps 1 to 7 can vary, but shutting off equipment before cleaning, and the last-person steps at the end, show good planning.

## Task 5 (level 3)

No single answer. A good revision removes every place where the partner had to stop and ask. Common fixes: saying which bin is for which metal, saying "the gas cylinder valve" instead of "the gas", and saying where the extension cords hang.

## Check yourself

1. "If the pressure drops below the red line, shut down the pump." (level 2)
2. Both. "And" means every item is required (level 1).
3. One case that does not fit an "all" statement, which proves the statement false (level 2).
4. The reader knows straight away what to do, and each step holds one action, so none get skipped (level 2).
5. When the order of the steps matters (level 1).
