Writing · Lesson 08 · Instructor

Write a trades résumé and cover letter

Build a one-page résumé that shows your skills to a trades employer, choose a format that fits your experience, and write a short cover letter tailored to one job posting.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 120 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to write; Plan the writing task; Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task; Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task; Choose the appropriate format for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

Session length: 120 min. All times below are part of the 120 minutes and match the steps in the learning tasks table.

Before class

Print or project the two versions of Rowan Pierre's résumé from the worked example, side by side. Make one card for each character in Task 4. Have flipchart paper and markers ready for the group résumés. Open Job Bank's "How to write a good resume" page on the projector.

Learners may choose to write about themselves or about a fictional character. Do not require anyone to share personal history, work gaps or identity with the class.

Opening (10 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Show the two versions of Rowan Pierre's résumé side by side. Ask: "Same person, same jobs. Which one would a shop owner call? Why?" List the differences on the board: contact details, a summary, skills that match the trade, numbers, achievement lines, dates, no "I".

Then say, adapted from the ACCESS résumé lesson: "Sometimes we struggle to find work not because we lack skills, experience or knowledge, but because our résumé doesn't show them clearly, or doesn't show them at all."

Objective (2 min, step 1.2)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • read a job posting and name what the employer needs (Identify the task that requires you to write)
  • gather their skills, including hidden skills from life outside work, and match them to the posting (Plan the writing task)
  • write achievement lines with action verbs and numbers, spelled correctly (Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task)
  • use a confident, plain, professional tone without "I", slang or empty claims (Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task)
  • choose a chronological, functional or combined format (Choose the appropriate format for the writing task)
  • proofread and revise a résumé and cover letter (Review and revise your writing)

Pre-assessment (5 min, step 1.3)

  1. "Do you have a résumé now? Do you know if it is chronological, functional or combined?"
  2. "Have you ever handed out résumés and heard nothing back? What do you think the employer saw?"

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Compare Rowan Pierre's two résumés and ask the opening question. Name the differences. Projector, board 10 min
1.2 State the objectives. Listen, ask questions. Board 2 min
1.3 Ask the pre-assessment questions. Answer aloud or on paper. none 5 min
2.1 Teach the parts of a résumé and the three formats. Make the point about leaving out the SIN, with Service Canada as the source. Call out résumé headings, follow the table. learner.md, board 12 min
2.2 Assign Task 1. Sort the lines under headings. learner.md 8 min
2.3 Teach hidden skills. In a circle, each learner names one thing they enjoy doing. The group brainstorms the skills it uses. Assign Task 2. Share an activity, help name skills, complete Task 2. learner.md 15 min
2.4 Show Job Bank's do's and don'ts on screen. Teach the duty versus achievement table. Assign Task 3. Rewrite the three duty lines. Projector, learner.md 12 min
3.1 Read the Fraser Bend posting aloud. Hand out character cards. Assign Task 4 in groups of two or three. Choose a format, write the résumé on flipchart paper. Character cards, flipchart, markers 20 min
3.2 Each group presents. The class gives one strength and one fix. Check that each résumé matches the posting's requirements. Present, give and take feedback. Flipcharts 10 min
3.3 Teach the cover letter plan. Assign Task 5. Write the cover letter. learner.md 15 min
3.4 Assign Task 6. Swap and check letters. learner.md 5 min
4.1 Ask the post-assessment questions. Answer in writing. none 4 min
4.2 Give the summary. Listen, ask final questions. none 2 min

Total: 10 + 2 + 5 + 12 + 8 + 15 + 12 + 20 + 10 + 15 + 5 + 4 + 2 = 120 min.

Post-assessment (4 min, step 4.1)

  1. Name the three common résumé formats and say when one of them is useful.
  2. Rewrite as an achievement line: "I did inventory."
  3. What did you discover today about your own hidden skills?

Summary (2 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • the parts of a résumé, and leaving out your SIN
  • chronological, functional and combined formats
  • Job Bank's do's and don'ts: short, numbers, action verbs, no "I", achievements not duties
  • finding hidden skills and proving them with facts
  • a three-part cover letter tailored to one posting
  • proofreading before you send
