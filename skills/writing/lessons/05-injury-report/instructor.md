# Report a workplace injury in BC

Session length: 90 min. All times below are part of the 90 minutes and match the steps in the learning tasks table.

## Before class

Open these WorkSafeBC pages on the projector and check them again on the day, because forms and phone hours change:

- Workers: How to report an injury and what to expect: https://www.worksafebc.com/en/claims/report-workplace-injury-illness/how-workers-report-workplace-injury-illness
- Worker's Notice to Employer of Injury or Occupational Disease (Form 6A): https://www.worksafebc.com/en/resources/claims/forms/workers-report-of-injury-or-occupational-disease-to-employer-form-6a?lang=en
- Record first aid incidents: https://www.worksafebc.com/en/health-safety/first-aid/record-first-aid-incidents

Remind learners that all practice uses the fictional case. Nobody writes their own SIN or Personal Health Number on practice sheets.

Some learners may have been hurt at work before, or may have had a claim go badly. Invite them to share only if they want to. Do not ask for details of anyone's injury.

## Opening (5 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Read out the weak description from the worked example: "Was carrying stuff and fell on the ramp. Hurt my leg. Ramp is bad." Ask: "If you were the claims officer, or the foreman trying to stop it happening again, what would you still need to know?" List the questions on the board.

## Objective (2 min, step 1.2)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- identify which BC injury report the worker, first aid attendant and employer each complete (Identify the task that requires you to write)
- put the facts of an incident in time order before writing (Plan the writing task)
- write a description in past tense and active voice, naming body parts, sides, conditions and witnesses (Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task)
- use the boxes of an injury report and first aid record for their intended content (Choose the appropriate format for the writing task)
- remove blame and guesses from a description (Review and revise your writing)

## Pre-assessment (5 min, step 1.3)

1. "If you get hurt at work in BC, who do you tell first?"
2. "Name any form or report you know of that gets filled in after a workplace injury."

## Learning tasks

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Read the weak description and ask the opening question. | List what is missing. | Board | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the objectives. | Listen, ask questions. | Board | 2 min |
| 1.3 | Ask the pre-assessment questions. | Answer on paper. | none | 5 min |
| 2.1 | Teach "who writes what" using the table. Show the WorkSafeBC pages on screen. Point out that the paper Form 6 is gone and Form 6A goes to the employer. Assign Task 1. | Follow the table, complete Task 1. | learner.md, projector | 15 min |
| 2.2 | Teach that investigations look for causes, not blame. Teach fact versus opinion and blame with the table. Assign Task 2. | Sort the statements, suggest rewrites. | learner.md | 10 min |
| 2.3 | Read the Dakota case aloud. On the board, build a timeline with learners: 7:00, 10:40, 10:50, 11:30, 3:00, after work. Mark body parts and sides. | Help build the timeline. | Board | 8 min |
| 2.4 | Assign Task 3. Circulate. Check for past tense, "I" as the doer, left and right, and the witness. | Write Dakota's description. | learner.md | 15 min |
| 2.5 | Pairs swap descriptions and check them against the timeline and the "good description" list. | Check a partner's work, give one fix. | learner.md | 7 min |
| 3.1 | Assign Task 4. Remind learners the first aid record describes what the attendant saw and did. | Write the three first aid entries. | learner.md | 8 min |
| 3.2 | Assign Task 5. Read section 3.10 aloud from the learner page. | Write Chris's hazard report. | learner.md | 7 min |
| 4.1 | Ask the post-assessment questions. | Answer in writing. | none | 5 min |
| 4.2 | Give the summary. | Listen, ask final questions. | none | 3 min |

Total: 5 + 2 + 5 + 15 + 10 + 8 + 15 + 7 + 8 + 7 + 5 + 3 = 90 min.

## Post-assessment (5 min, step 4.1)

1. Name the form an employer uses to report an injury to WorkSafeBC.
2. Rewrite as a fact: "The new guy was rushing and cut himself."
3. Within how many days must an employer complete a full investigation report?

## Summary (3 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- who writes what after a BC workplace injury: worker report to WorkSafeBC, Form 6A if asked, first aid record, Form 7, employer investigation
- reporting hazards under section 3.10
- why investigations look for causes, not blame
- writing a description in order, in past tense and active voice, with body parts, sides, conditions and witnesses
- protecting your SIN and Personal Health Number
