Writing · Lesson 06 · Instructor

Text and email a foreman or client

Choose text or email, lead with the point, write a subject line that says what the message is about, match your tone to the reader, and confirm spoken agreements in writing.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to write; Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task; Choose the appropriate format for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

Session length: 90 min. All times below are part of the 90 minutes and match the steps in the learning tasks table.

Opening (5 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Put this text on the board: "hey no pipe". Ask: "You're the foreman. You get this at 7:05 am. What do you do next?" Take answers. Most will be "call and ask what they mean". Point out that one clear text would have saved the call, and the foreman's time.

Objective (2 min, step 1.2)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • decide whether a message needs a text, an email or a call followed by a written note (Identify the task that requires you to write)
  • adjust tone and wording for a foreman, a crew member or a client (Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task)
  • use a subject line, greeting, short paragraphs and a signature in a client email (Choose the appropriate format for the writing task)
  • reread and fix a message before sending, including one written in anger (Review and revise your writing)

Pre-assessment (5 min, step 1.3)

  1. "What is the last work text you sent? Would a stranger understand it?"
  2. "When would you email instead of text?"

Post-assessment (6 min, step 4.1)

  1. Write a subject line for an email telling a client their bathroom fan install is finished.
  2. Rewrite "u there? need help" as a clear text to a co-worker asking for help lifting a water heater in Unit 3 at 10:00 am.
  3. Name one rule from the BC Motor Vehicle Act about phones and driving.

Summary (4 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • choosing text, email, or a call followed by a written note
  • leading with the point, and giving who, what, where, when and how many
  • clear subject lines
  • tone for crew and for clients
  • captions for photos
  • confirming spoken changes in writing
  • never using a phone while driving in BC
