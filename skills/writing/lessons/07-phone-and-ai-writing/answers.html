Check writing made with a phone or AI tool

Use voice typing and AI writing tools without losing control of your message. Check every word against the facts, keep private information out, and write so any reader or tool keeps the key facts.

Task 1 (level 2)

Hey Kai, can you grab two 1/2" ball valves for Unit 6, north wall, before lunch? Thanks.

Fixes: "kye" to "Kai", "to" to "two", "half inch" to 1/2", "bowl" to "ball", "sex" to "6" (with "Unit" capitalized), commas and a question mark added.

Task 2 (level 2)

Errors and added promises:

  1. Name is wrong: "Thompson" should be "Thomas".
  2. Date is wrong: November 18 should be Tuesday, November 17.
  3. "As required by WorkSafeBC" is not in the source facts. Remove it.
  4. Arrival time is wrong: 7:30 am should be 8:00 to 8:30 am.
  5. Finish time is wrong: noon should be about 4:00 pm.
  6. The 10% discount is a promise nobody agreed to. Remove it.
  7. "Licensed crew" is not supported by the source facts. Remove it unless verified.
  8. Missing: the reason (furnace arrives November 16), that the heat will be off, the request to reply by Friday, and a signature.

Corrected email:

Dear Ms. Thomas,

Your furnace installation at 2214 Birch Lane has moved from Thursday, November 12, to Tuesday, November 17, because the new furnace will not arrive until November 16.

Our crew will arrive between 8:00 and 8:30 am and expects to finish by about 4:00 pm. The heat will be off during that time.

Could you please reply by Friday to confirm that someone will be home to let us in?

Kind regards, Sam Ortiz Cedar Ridge Mechanical

Task 3 (level 2)

a) OK to paste. It describes what happened and has no names or private details. b) Do not paste. A Personal Health Number and a full name together are personal information about someone else. c) Remove or replace first. Replace it with [client address]. d) OK to paste. It is an instruction with no private information. e) Do not paste, unless your employer has approved the tool for that use. Prices are the employer's confidential information.

Task 4 (level 3)

Sample:

Subject: Request to move Willow Creek Unit 6 drywall start to Monday, November 16

Hi Marie,

The drywall start at the Willow Creek duplex, Unit 6, set for Thursday, November 12, needs to move to Monday, November 16. The framing inspection has not passed yet.

Could your crew start Monday, November 16, provided the framing inspection has passed by then? Please confirm availability; we will confirm clearance before work starts.

Thanks, Sam Ortiz

A good answer: puts the job, unit and proposed date in the subject line, names the drywall start instead of "it", gives both dates in full, gives the reason in plain words, and asks a clear question.

Check yourself

  1. You are, as the person who sends it (level 1).
  2. Any two of: sound-alike words (tee, tea, two, to), numbers and sizes, names of people or streets, trade terms (level 1).
  3. Look up the law or form yourself on the official site and check that it says what the tool claims. If it does not, remove the claim (level 2).
  4. Any two of: Social Insurance Numbers, Personal Health Numbers, addresses or phone numbers, someone's injury or health details, your employer's prices, bids, drawings or client lists (level 1).
  5. "Thursday" could be any Thursday, and the reader, a search or a summary may not know which one. A full date has only one meaning (level 2).
