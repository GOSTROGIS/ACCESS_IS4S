Fill in a workplace form correctly

Read a form before you write, put each fact in the right box, calculate hours with BEDMAS, report facts without accusing anyone, and check before you sign.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to write; Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task; Choose the appropriate format for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

Task 1 (level 1)

Employee name: Alex Paul. Employee #: 4471. Week ending: 2026-11-06.

Day Date Job # Start Finish Unpaid break Hours
Mon 2026-11-02 26-118 7:00 am 3:30 pm 0.5 h 8.0
Tue 2026-11-03 26-118 7:00 am 3:30 pm 0.5 h 8.0
Wed 2026-11-04 26-118 7:00 am 5:00 pm 0.5 h 9.5
Thu 2026-11-05 26-118 7:30 am 3:30 pm 0.5 h 7.5
Fri 2026-11-06 26-120 7:00 am 1:00 pm 0.5 h 5.5
Total 38.5

Employee signature: Alex Paul. Supervisor approval: left blank, because it is for office use only.

Steps: the week ends on Friday, November 6, so Monday is November 2. Write each date as yyyy-mm-dd. Use job 26-118 Monday to Thursday and 26-120 on Friday.

Task 2 (level 1)

  • Wednesday: 5:00 pm = 17:00 = 17.0. (17.0 − 7.0) − 0.5 = 10.0 − 0.5 = 9.5 h
  • Thursday: 3:30 pm = 15:30 = 15.5. 7:30 am = 7.5. (15.5 − 7.5) − 0.5 = 8.0 − 0.5 = 7.5 h
  • Friday, for the total: 1:00 pm = 13.0. (13.0 − 7.0) − 0.5 = 6.0 − 0.5 = 5.5 h
  • Week: 8.0 + 8.0 + 9.5 + 7.5 + 5.5 = 38.5 h

Task 3 (level 2)

  • Type: Missing
  • Date reported: 2026-11-04
  • Date and time noticed: 2026-11-04, 7:10 am
  • Job # and location: 26-118, Langley townhouse site, locked material cage
  • Reported by: Jesse Lim, Apprentice, #4502, extension 214
  • Material: 3 coils, 3/4" PEX pipe
  • What you saw: "On 2026-11-03 at 3:30 pm I counted 8 coils of 3/4" PEX in the cage. On 2026-11-04 at 7:10 am the cage lock was hanging open and 5 coils were left."
  • Other information: "When I arrived at 6:50 am a white pickup truck was parked beside the cage. It left at 7:00 am. I did not see the licence plate or the driver. I told Carla Ng at 7:20 am."
  • Signatures: Jesse Lim (employee reporting), Carla Ng (supervisor receiving)

A good answer works out the quantity (8 − 5 = 3 coils), keeps the times in order, and mentions the truck only as something seen. It does not say the truck driver took the pipe, because Jesse did not see that.

Task 4 (level 2)

  1. Tuesday's date "11/03/26" could mean November 3 or March 11. Write 2026-11-03.
  2. Tuesday's hours are wrong. (15.5 − 7.0) − 0.5 = 8.0, not 8.5.
  3. Wednesday's job number is blank. Write 26-120.
  4. The total is wrong. The hours as written add to 40.5, and the correct hours add to 8.0 × 5 = 40.0. Write 40.0.
  5. Sam signed in the supervisor box and left the employee signature blank. Sam should sign as employee and leave the supervisor box for the supervisor.

Check yourself

  1. N/A, meaning not applicable (level 1).
  2. 3:00 pm = 15.0, 6:30 am = 6.5. (15.0 − 6.5) − 0.5 = 8.5 − 0.5 = 8.0 h (level 1).
  3. You may be wrong, and the form becomes an accusation without proof. The supervisor investigates, and needs facts to do it (level 2).
  4. That you have read the form and the information is true and complete (level 1).
  5. Any part marked "Supervisor", "Office use only" or for another person, such as the supervisor approval box (level 1).
