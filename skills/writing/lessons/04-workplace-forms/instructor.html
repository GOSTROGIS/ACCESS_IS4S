Writing · Lesson 04 · Instructor

Fill in a workplace form correctly

Read a form before you write, put each fact in the right box, calculate hours with BEDMAS, report facts without accusing anyone, and check before you sign.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Identify the task that requires you to write; Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task; Choose the appropriate format for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

Session length: 75 min. All times below are part of the 75 minutes and match the steps in the learning tasks table.

Opening (5 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners. Ask: "Has a form ever cost you money or time? A timesheet that came back, a cheque that was short, an application that was returned?" Take two or three stories. Then say: "Payroll can't pay hours it can't read. Today we fill in forms so nobody has to come and find you."

Objective (2 min, step 1.2)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • read a whole form and identify which parts they fill in (Identify the task that requires you to write)
  • enter dates, times, numbers and units in the format the form asks for (Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task)
  • use a pre-formatted workplace form to report facts (Choose the appropriate format for the writing task)
  • check a completed form for errors before signing (Review and revise your writing)

Pre-assessment (3 min, step 1.3)

  1. "What does 'N/A' mean on a form?"
  2. "A shift runs 7:00 am to 3:30 pm with a 30 min unpaid lunch. How many hours is that?"

Post-assessment (4 min, step 4.1)

  1. Write March 9, 2027 in the format "(yyyy-mm-dd)".
  2. Calculate the hours, showing the bracket: 6:00 am to 2:30 pm, 30 min unpaid lunch.
  3. Rewrite this report line as a fact: "Kevin stole the drill again."

Summary (3 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • reading the whole form first and finding your parts
  • N/A for boxes that do not apply
  • dates in the format the form shows, numbers with units
  • hours worked = (finish − start) − unpaid break, brackets first by BEDMAS
  • facts, not accusations, on report forms
  • checking before you sign
