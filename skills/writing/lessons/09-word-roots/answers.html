Use word roots to spell and explain trade words

Break long trade words into Latin and Greek roots to remember their meaning and spelling, use Canadian spelling, and explain technical words in plain language when the reader needs it.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Use written words and phrases so you can achieve the purpose of the writing task; Choose the appropriate language and style for the writing task; Review and revise your writing

  1. d (water)
  2. f (hot)

  4. a (around)

Task 2 (level 1 to 2)

Meanings below are from the Online Etymology Dictionary. A learner's dictionary may word them differently.

a) perimeter = peri (around) + metron (measure). Guess: the measure around something. Dictionary: the outer boundary or border of a figure or surface.

b) pneumatic = pneuma (wind, breath) + -ic. Guess: to do with air. Dictionary: moved by means of air, or having to do with air or gases.

c) non-ferrous = non (not) + ferr (iron) + -ous. Guess: not containing iron. Dictionary: "ferrous" means pertaining to or containing iron, so non-ferrous metals contain no iron.

d) manual = manu (hand) + -al. Guess: by hand. Dictionary: of or belonging to the hand. A manual valve is one you work by hand.

e) circumference = circum (around) + ferre (to carry). Guess: the line that goes around. Dictionary: the line that bounds a circle.

Steps: find a root you know from the table, write the guess, then check. A good answer notes any place where the guess was off.

Task 3 (level 1)

a) metres (unit of length) b) meter (measuring device) c) litres d) centre e) labour f) colour

Task 4 (level 2)

Sample:

We'll run the line perpendicular to the joists (at a right angle to them), put a pneumatic test on it (a pressure test using air), and set the torque on the flange bolts (the turning force used to tighten them) to spec (the value set out in the job's specifications).

A good answer keeps the technical words, explains each one in plain words the first time, and does not change the meaning of the work.

Task 5 (level 2)

Answers depend on the root chosen. Example for metron (measure): thermometer, diameter, perimeter, isometric. A good answer checks every word in a dictionary and crosses out any where the letters look the same but the root is different.

Check yourself

  1. Measure. Any two of thermometer, diameter, perimeter, isometric (level 1).
  2. Metre. "Meter" is a measuring device (level 1).
  3. A worker in lead, from Latin plumbum, lead (level 1).
  4. A root gives a good guess, but the word may have changed meaning over time, or the letters may only look like the root (level 2).
  5. When the reader may not know the term, such as a homeowner or client, and the first time the word appears (level 2).
