Giving and taking feedback and criticism

Give thanks and corrections that are specific and fair, take criticism without getting defensive, and know when criticism crosses into bullying.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers
  • Level: 2
  • Time: 75 min
  • Components: Listen to understand; Speak with purpose; Adapt to your audience and contexts

Opening (5 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners to session 5 of the Communication series. The session runs 75 minutes. Ask who said no assertively since last session.

Hook: "Write down, just for yourself, one piece of feedback that helped you get better at something. Now one that just made you angry." After a minute, ask: "What was the difference?" Take a few answers without asking for the stories. Usually the helpful one was specific and fair.

Objective (shared during the opening)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • give specific, sincere thanks and fair, specific corrections (Speak with purpose)
  • listen to criticism, ask for details and respond with the A B C D techniques (Listen to understand)
  • choose time, place and tone for feedback, and tell fair feedback from bullying and harassment (Adapt to your audience and contexts)

Pre-assessment (5 min, step 1.2)

  1. "What do you usually do when someone points out a mistake you made?"
  2. "When was the last time you thanked someone at work or in class for something specific?"

Learning tasks

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 Opening, action plan check, hook, objective. Report back, write and discuss. Paper 5 min
1.2 Pre-assessment questions. Answer out loud. None 5 min
2.1 Teach giving and receiving compliments. Run Task 1. Rewrite three vague compliments. Learner page 7 min
2.2 Task 2, the wrapped package. Halfway through, stop and ask: "How does it feel to receive a compliment? Does the giver know how you feel?" Then ask receivers to acknowledge each compliment out loud or with a nod before unwrapping. Give and receive compliments, answer the check-in questions. A small item wrapped in one layer per learner. If the item is food, check for allergies first. 10 min
2.3 Teach the three parts of a correction. Run Task 5 and hear three examples. Write and share a correction. Learner page 8 min
2.4 Warm-up for criticism. Ask each learner to volunteer a short, critical statement that would be hard for them to answer, such as "You're lazy." Flipchart them and set them aside. Suggest statements. Flipchart, markers 4 min
2.5 Teach the A B C D techniques with the table. Act out the worked example with a learner as Maria. Read the replies, act out the example. Learner page 8 min
3.1 Task 3. Check answers together. Label four replies. Learner page 5 min
3.2 Task 4 in groups of four to six. Give each group the Task 4 statements plus two or three from the flipchart. Debrief: "Could you use the techniques? What more did you need? How did you handle the hardest statements?" Practise replies out loud, then write the Task 4 answers. Learner page, flipchart list 10 min
3.3 Teach "Criticism or bullying?" Show worksafebc.com/bullying on screen if available. Run Task 6. Classify six situations, discuss. Learner page, screen 6 min
4.1 Post-assessment questions. Answer. None 4 min
4.2 Summary and action plan. Commit to one specific compliment and one acknowledge-first reply before next session. None 3 min
Total 75 min

Notes for the instructor:

  • In step 2.4, learners choose their own statements. Do not ask anyone to repeat criticism they have actually received if they do not want to.
  • If a learner describes something that sounds like bullying or harassment at their own workplace, talk with them privately after class and point them to their employer's reporting procedure and worksafebc.com/bullying.

Post-assessment (4 min, step 4.1)

  1. "Someone says, 'You were late again.' It is true. Give an Acknowledge reply."
  2. "Give a compliment to someone in this room that names exactly what they did."
  3. "Name one behaviour that WorkSafeBC says is bullying and harassment, and one that is not."

Summary (3 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

  • specific, sincere compliments and how to accept one
  • a fair correction: what I saw, why it matters, what to do
  • the A B C D techniques: Acknowledge, Boundaries, Clarify, Disagree
  • acknowledging before explaining, and skipping excuses
  • the difference between fair feedback and bullying and harassment, and where to find WorkSafeBC information

Action plan: give one specific compliment and use one acknowledge-first reply before next session.
