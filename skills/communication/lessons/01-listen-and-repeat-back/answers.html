Listening and repeating back instructions

Catch every detail of a spoken job-site instruction and say it back so you both know you have it right.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 70 min
  • Components: Listen with intention; Listen to understand

Task 1 (level 1) Draw what you hear

The intended drawing is a simple house: a box 4 inches wide and 3 inches tall with a pointed roof made of two 3-inch lines meeting above the middle of the box.

Steps that lead to it: the bottom line and two side lines make three sides of the box, step 4 closes the top, and steps 5 and 6 add the two roof lines from the top-left corner to the top-right corner.

A good response to the questions:

  • Round 1 was hard because it was fast, there was no chance to ask, and step 5 did not say which left corner.
  • In round 2 the learner asked questions (for example "top-left or bottom-left corner?"), said steps back and checked measurements before drawing.

Task 2 (level 1) Sort the instruction

Card A

Part Answer
WHAT Red extension cord and work light, set up and plugged in
WHERE Parkade level P2, by the elevator, panel TEMP 3
HOW MUCH One cord, one light
WHEN Before the concrete crew arrives at ten
WHO The concrete crew is coming. No one named to report to
SAFETY Use TEMP 3, not TEMP 1

Card B

Part Answer
WHAT Move scaffold planks
WHERE From bay 4 to bay 6
HOW MUCH Twelve planks
WHEN After lunch
WHO Help Priya. Tell the lead hand when done
SAFETY Wear gloves because of splinters

Accept the learner's own words if the facts match. Treat a wrong number, panel or bay as a miss even when the other parts are right.

Task 3 (level 2) Say it back and catch the gap

Card C. Repeat-back: "A box of three-inch screws to the stairs." Missing: WHERE the box is, which stairs (if there is more than one set), and possibly what kind of screws. A good question: "Where do I find the box, and which stairs, the north or the south?"

Card D. Repeat-back: "Forty short brackets for the second floor by end of day." Missing: WHAT to do with them (get them, install them, order them) and WHERE on the second floor. A good question: "Do you want me to bring forty to the second floor, or install them? Where should they go?"

Any question that targets a real gap is correct. A question about something the card already said is not.

Task 4 (level 2) Fact or assumption

  1. F. Card A said level P2.
  2. A. Card A said the crew arrives at ten. It did not say they will be late.
  3. A. Card A said not to use TEMP 1. It did not say why.
  4. F. "Before the concrete crew gets here at ten."
  5. F. "Plug it into the panel marked TEMP 3."

Task 5 (level 2) Make it hard to mishear

Sample answers. Accept any version that removes the confusion.

  1. "Cut it at fifteen inches, one-five inches." The unit and the digits remove the fifteen or fifty problem.
  2. "Bring thirteen, one-three, of the ..." and name the item. Or ask what "the thirteen ones" means: thirteen of something, or parts marked 13?
  3. "Move it 2 inches toward the window." Give a distance and a direction instead of "a bit."

Task 6 (level 2) Find the six parts

Part What the instruction says
WHAT Pave, starting the north lane
WHERE North lane, from the first manhole
HOW MUCH A 50 mm lift
WHEN Once the tack coat is dry. Done by noon.
WHO Ken is flagging at the east end
SAFETY Roller stays 10 metres back. Ken is flagging.

Some learners put "tack coat dry" under SAFETY. Accept it if they explain why.

Task 7 (level 3) Catch the errors

a) "South lane" should be north lane. "Fifteen-millimetre lift" should be fifty. The operator also left out the condition: wait until the tack coat is dry.

b) Sample: "North lane, not south. Fifty millimetres, five-zero, not fifteen. Start only once the tack coat is dry."

c) Ask for the whole instruction again before starting: "Once the tack coat is dry, start the north lane at the first manhole. Fifty-millimetre lift. Roller stays ten metres back. Ken is flagging at the east end. Done by noon. Is that right?" Confirm or correct the read-back.

Check yourself

  1. What, where, how much, when, who and safety.
  2. So the other person can confirm it or correct it. Without the question, they may not notice a mistake.
  3. Ask: "Did you say the north side or the south side?" Do not act on the guess.
  4. Say the number and then the digits, for example "fifty, five-zero," and say the unit.
  5. Yes. The accurate repeat-back shows they listened. Where someone looks is not proof either way.
