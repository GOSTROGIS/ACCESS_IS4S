# Phone calls, voicemail and video calls at work

## Opening (5 min, step 1.1)

Welcome learners to session 7 of the Communication series. The session runs 75 minutes. Ask who checked a cue out loud since last session.

Hook: play, or read aloud, two voicemails you write yourself in advance: one rambling with the phone number said once, fast, in the middle, and one clear. Ask: "Which one would you call back? Can you write down the number from the first one?" Most cannot.

## Objective (shared during the opening)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- open and close a work call, take an accurate message and leave a clear voicemail (Speak with clarity, Speak with purpose)
- run a video call and use texts and crew chats appropriately (Adapt to other people's different communication modes and tools)
- choose the right mode for a message, including a working alone check-in (Adapt to other people's different communication modes and tools)

## Pre-assessment (4 min, step 1.2)

1. "What do you say first when you call a company you have never called before?"
2. "What should a voicemail always include?"

### Caller script for Task 2

"Hi, this is Sunita Grewal from Peak Glass Supply. I'm calling for Rae about the window order for the Maple Street job. Two of the units are back-ordered until the 18th. Can she call me back today before 4? My number is 604-555-0148."

The names, companies and phone numbers in this script are fictional. Do not call them.

## Post-assessment (5 min, step 4.1)

Scenario for the spoken voicemail: "You need to tell your supervisor, Pat, that the concrete truck is coming at 7:30 instead of 9 tomorrow."

1. "What did you say first in your voicemail?"
2. "When would you call instead of texting the crew chat?"
3. "Who sets the time between check-ins for someone working alone?"

## Summary (3 min, step 4.2)

During this session we covered:

- getting ready to call and the five steps of a work call
- answering a work phone and taking a message that has everything in it
- calling in late or sick
- voicemail: name, number, why, what you need, by when, number again
- video calls, group chats and choosing the right mode
- working alone check-ins under section 4.21 of the BC OHS Regulation

Action plan: before next session, record a clear voicemail greeting on your own phone, or make one work-style call using the five steps.
