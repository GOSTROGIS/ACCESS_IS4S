Problem solving · Lesson 14 · Instructor

Plan a site layout and log your assumptions

Place the gate, crane, trailers, storage, bins and safety points on a building lot, check the crane reach with the Pythagorean theorem, and write an assumption log that says why each choice was made.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 120 min
  • Components: Identify the issue to address; Gather information to help you address the issue; Analyze the issue; Develop multiple routes of action; Address the issue

Opening (5 min)

Session 14 of 16 in Problem Solving. Length: 120 min.

Welcome learners. Ask: "Think of a busy site you have walked past or worked on. Where was the gate? Where were the trailers? Did anything look like it was in the wrong place?" Take two or three answers. Then ask: "Who decided where all that went, and how would you know why?" Tell the group that today they plan a whole site and write down the reason for every choice, so someone else can check it.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • sort job information into fixed things and choices (Identify the issue to address: identify the nature of the issue, identify the goals and objectives)
  • read a job sheet and a site plan for the facts that limit a layout (Gather information to help you address the issue: conduct research and collect relevant information)
  • check crane reach with the Pythagorean theorem and trace truck and walking routes (Analyze the issue: break down the issue into smaller parts, identify possible cause-and-effect linkages)
  • compare possible spots for the gate and crane and choose one (Develop multiple routes of action: create multiple options for action)
  • make a full layout with an assumption log, check a partner's layout and adjust a plan when a fact changes (Address the issue: apply the best course of action, monitor and adjust)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. "What is hoarding on a building site?"
  2. "In a right triangle with sides 6 m and 8 m, how long is the longest side?" (10 m)

Learning tasks (98 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 1.1 Read the job sheet aloud with the plan on screen. Teach fixed things and choices. Assign Task 1. 1.1 Complete Task 1 and share one question for the superintendent. Learner page, projector 12 min
1.2 1.2 Teach the three routes (truck, worker, load). Trace a truck from Cedar Street and from Mill Lane on the screen. Assign Task 2. 1.2 Complete Task 2 and write log line 1. Learner page, projector 10 min
2.1 2.1 Teach the distance formula on the grid. Work the check-yourself example (10, 20) to (34, 2) on the board with BEDMAS: 24² + 18² = 576 + 324 = 900, √900 = 30 m. 2.1 Follow along with a calculator. Board, calculator 8 min
2.2 2.2 Assign Task 3 in pairs. Ask each pair to report one distance and their chosen spot. 2.2 Complete Task 3. Learner page, calculator 15 min
3.1 3.1 Teach the assumption log with the three example lines. Stress the difference between a decision and an assumption. 3.1 Read the example lines and add one of their own about the hoarding. Learner page 5 min
3.2 3.2 Hand out grid paper or printed plans and coloured pencils. Assign Task 4. Circulate and ask "why" about each item. 3.2 Complete Task 4: full layout and at least 8 log lines. Grid paper or printed plan, coloured pencils, ruler 30 min
3.3 3.3 Assign Task 5. Pairs swap layouts and check them. 3.3 Check a partner's layout and give feedback. Partner's layout 10 min
3.4 3.4 Read the supplier's phone call aloud. Assign Task 6. 3.4 Complete Task 6 and update the log. Learner page 8 min

Learning tasks: 12 + 10 + 8 + 15 + 5 + 30 + 10 + 8 = 98 min. Whole session: 5 + 2 + 3 + 98 + 7 + 5 = 120 min.

Facilitation notes:

  • There is no single right layout. Grade the reasons in the log, not the drawing.
  • For Task 3c, learners only need to see that P3 is beside the overhead power line. Do not give a clearance distance. The job sheet says the crane supplier and the power utility decide it, and that is the answer to teach: ask the people responsible.
  • Learners who need more practice with right triangles can go back to Numeracy lesson 15. The Numeracy Hub Construction Operations tab has more construction-site number practice.

Post-assessment (7 min)

  1. "Name three things on a job sheet that you cannot change."
  2. "A crane at (0, 0) must reach (21, 20). Its planning limit is 28 m. Can it?" (√(441 + 400) = √841 = 29 m. No.)
  3. "Write one assumption log line for where the lunch trailer goes."

Summary (5 min)

During this session we covered:

  • sorting job information into fixed things and choices
  • following truck, worker and load routes to find where they cross
  • checking crane reach with the Pythagorean theorem and keeping a margin
  • writing an assumption log: decision, why, how to check, who confirms
  • checking a layout with a partner and changing the plan when a fact changes

Ask learners how they would explain their gate choice to a city inspector in one sentence.
