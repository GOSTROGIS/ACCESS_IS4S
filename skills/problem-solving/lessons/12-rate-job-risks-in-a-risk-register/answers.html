Rate job risks in a risk register

List what could go wrong on a job, score how likely and how bad each risk is, rank them, and give each one an owner, a response and a warning sign to watch for.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the issue to address; Analyze the issue; Develop multiple routes of action; Address the issue

Task 1 (level 1)

  • a. R. It might happen.
  • b. I. It already happened.
  • c. R.
  • d. I. The sheets are already cracked.
  • e. R. It might not be sorted by Monday.
  • f. I. The change already happened. It may create new risks (for example the new paint could arrive late).

Task 2 (level 2)

Any sensible cause is accepted if all three parts are there. Examples:

  • a. Because the supplier is short of beams, the steel may arrive late, which stops the framing crew.
  • b. Because the inspector is booked solid this week, the framing inspection may be a day late, which holds up the insulation and drywall.
  • c. Because there is no parking near the site, workers may arrive late or get tickets, which loses crew time at the start of the day.

Task 3 (level 2)

Score = likelihood × impact.

Rank No. Score Band
1 H1 3 × 3 = 9 High
2 H2 2 × 3 = 6 High
3 H3 2 × 2 = 4 Medium
4 H4 1 × 2 = 2 Low
5 H5 1 × 1 = 1 Low

On the grid, H1 sits in the top right corner (likely and serious) and H5 in the bottom left.

Task 4 (level 3)

Examples. Other well-reasoned answers are accepted.

H1 (fit on site):

  • a. Avoid: measure the real stair on site before any steel is cut, and build to the site numbers. Have the general contractor sign the site measurements.
  • b. Owner: shop foreman or the person who does the site measure.
  • c. Trigger: site measurements differ from the drawing by more than the shop allows for (on this job, any difference at all is reported).
  • d. If the stair is not ready to measure by the date fabrication must start, then the shop builds the parts that do not depend on site numbers first and tells the general contractor the install date will move.

H2 (late steel):

  • a. Reduce: order the pipe as soon as the shop drawings are approved, or share it by getting the supplier's delivery date in writing. A backup supplier can also be lined up.
  • b. Owner: purchaser or project coordinator.
  • c. Trigger: no confirmed ship date from the supplier two weeks before fabrication starts.
  • d. If the supplier cannot confirm a ship date by the trigger date, then order from the backup supplier.

Task 5 (level 2)

Ratings may differ if the reason is sound. A strong set:

  • a. Concrete slab pour: high. Once it is poured and hard, mistakes are buried in the slab and can only be fixed by cutting or breaking concrete.
  • b. Roof membrane: high. A leak shows up later and can damage everything below it.
  • c. Sprinkler pressure test: high. A failed test or a leak means water damage and the building cannot be occupied until it passes.
  • d. Painting the office walls: low. Mistakes are easy to see and cheap to repaint.
  • e. Planting the front garden: low. Plants can be moved or replaced at small cost.

Task 6 (level 2)

  • a. It is now an issue, because the rain happened and the pour moved.
  • b. Example: "R1: Rain Tuesday. Pour moved to Thursday. Job 2 days late. Status: issue, recovery plan with Marcus."
  • c. Yes. The lumber (R2) now has 2 more days before framing needs it, so R2 is less urgent, but the crew must tell the yard so the lumber is not left out in the rain. Any later booked dates, such as the inspection, must also move. Accept any sound link to another risk or booking.

Check yourself

  1. A risk might happen. An issue has already happened. (Level 1)
  2. 2 × 3 = 6, high. (Level 2)
  3. Avoid, reduce, share, accept. (Level 1)
  4. So one person is clearly responsible for watching it and starting the response. If everyone owns it, nobody acts. (Level 2)
  5. Report it to a supervisor or the employer as soon as possible (WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation section 3.10). Do not wait for the next planning meeting. (Level 2)
