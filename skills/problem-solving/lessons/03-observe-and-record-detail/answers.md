# Observe and record what you see

## Task 1 (level 1)

- a. 5 boxes
- b. 2:15 (quarter past two)
- c. JOB 4471, 12 BRACKETS, DUE THU 3 PM
- d. Orange
- e. The bench grinder. The tag says OUT OF SERVICE, DO NOT USE.
- f. 3 sawhorses
- g. EXIT

Count how many you got right out of 7. Most people score 4 or 5 on the first try.

## Task 2 (level 1)

- **Zone 1:** door, green EXIT sign, dark puddle on the floor in front of the door.
- **Zone 2:** red FIRE EXTINGUISHER sign, fire extinguisher, five stacked boxes in front of it.
- **Zone 3:** wall clock at 2:15, work light on a stand, workbench, bench grinder with a yellow OUT OF SERVICE tag, blue vise, grey flat bar sticking out past the end of the bench, orange cord on the floor.
- **Zone 4:** whiteboard (JOB 4471, 12 BRACKETS, DUE THU 3 PM), three sawhorses, wall outlet, orange cord running from the outlet across the floor.

Steps: one zone at a time, top to bottom, then the floor of that zone.

## Task 3 (level 2)

| What | Where | Why it could hurt someone |
|---|---|---|
| Dark puddle about a metre wide | Floor in front of the exit door, zone 1 | Slips. It is on the way out in an emergency. |
| Boxes stacked in front of the fire extinguisher | Zone 2 | Nobody can reach the extinguisher quickly in a fire. |
| Orange extension cord lying across the floor | From the outlet in zone 4 to the work light in zone 3 | Trips, and the cord can be damaged by carts or feet. |
| Flat bar sticking out past the end of the bench | Zones 3 to 4, into the walkway | People can walk into it or catch clothing on it. |

A strong note also gives the time seen and who was told, for example: "Told the lead hand at 2:20 p.m."

The tagged grinder is **not** one of the four. It has been taken out of service and tagged so nobody uses it by mistake, which is what WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation section 12.10 requires for unsafe equipment. It becomes a hazard only if someone ignores the tag.

## Task 4 (level 2)

Any rewrite that states only what can be seen is correct. Examples:

- a. "Dark liquid on the floor in front of the bay 2 exit door, about one metre across. Source unknown."
- b. "Bench grinder in bay 2 has a yellow OUT OF SERVICE, DO NOT USE tag." (Whether it is broken is not shown.)
- c. "Five boxes are stacked in front of the fire extinguisher in bay 2. A flat bar sticks out past the end of the bench." My opinion: storage needs sorting out.
- d. "Whiteboard says job 4471, 12 brackets, due Thursday 3 p.m." My opinion: it may be late. (Add what you base that on, such as how many are done.)

## Task 5 (level 2)

| Item | Purchase order | Packing slip | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| PO number | 30871 | 30817 | Digits swapped. The slip may be for another order, or it is a typo. |
| Flat bar | 50 mm × 6 mm | 50 mm × 8 mm | Wrong thickness. |
| Drill bit | qty 2 | qty 1 | One bit short. |

The plate and the paint match.

Steps: read across one line at a time, and compare every number, including the header numbers. What each one could cost: brackets made from the wrong thickness, a second trip or delay for the missing bit, and billing or receiving errors from the wrong PO number.
