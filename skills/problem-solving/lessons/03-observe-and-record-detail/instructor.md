# Observe and record what you see

## Opening (5 min)

**Session 3 of 10 in Problem Solving. Length: 60 min.**

Welcome learners. Before they arrive, set out ten everyday shop items on a table under a cloth (for example a tape measure, a pencil, a utility knife, safety glasses, a 3/8-inch socket, a zip tie, a roll of tape, a marker, a pair of gloves and a level). Lift the cloth for 30 seconds, cover it again, and ask learners to write down as many items as they can. Reveal and count.

Ask: "Which items did you miss, and why?" Most people remember the bright or unusual ones. Tell the group that on a job site the item you miss may be the one that hurts someone, so today we practise looking in order.

## Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- scan a work area zone by zone and recall details accurately (**Gather information to help you address the issue**)
- write report notes that separate observation from interpretation (**Gather information to help you address the issue**: differentiate fact from opinion)
- find unsafe conditions and differences between two documents, and explain why each matters (**Analyze the issue**)

## Pre-assessment (3 min)

1. "Have you ever spotted something at work or at home that nobody else noticed? What helped you see it?"
2. "What would you write down if you found a spill near a door?"

## Learning tasks (43 min)

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | 1.1 Show the shop picture for exactly 60 seconds, then ask learners to cover it. Assign Task 1. | 1.1 Study the picture, cover it, answer Task 1 from memory. | Learner page, timer | 5 min |
| 1.2 | 1.2 Teach the fixed-order scan from the key ideas. Assign Task 2. | 1.2 Scan zone by zone, list everything, check Task 1. | Learner page | 7 min |
| 2.1 | 2.1 Explain observation versus interpretation and the five questions. Read the hose worked example. Assign Task 4. | 2.1 Complete Task 4 and read one rewrite aloud. | Learner page | 7 min |
| 2.2 | 2.2 Explain the duty to report what appears unsafe (section 3.10). Assign Task 3. Discuss the tagged grinder. | 2.2 Complete Task 3 in pairs and share one report note. | Learner page | 10 min |
| 3.1 | 3.1 Explain that detail matters in paperwork too. Assign Task 5. | 3.1 Compare the purchase order and packing slip and list differences. | Learner page | 8 min |
| 3.2 | 3.2 Check Task 5 aloud. Ask what each difference would cost the shop if nobody caught it. | 3.2 Mark answers and give one cost for each difference. | Learner page | 6 min |

Learning tasks: 5 + 7 + 7 + 10 + 8 + 6 = 43 min. Whole session: 5 + 2 + 3 + 43 + 5 + 2 = 60 min.

**For learners who use a screen reader or cannot see the picture**, read this description aloud for Task 1 and give it in writing for Tasks 2 and 3: "Zone 1: a brown door with a green EXIT sign above it. A dark puddle about a metre wide lies on the floor in front of the door. Zone 2: a red sign reading FIRE EXTINGUISHER. Under it, five cardboard boxes are stacked in front of a red fire extinguisher, so only its top shows. Zone 3: a round wall clock showing 2:15. A yellow work light on a stand. A wooden workbench holding a bench grinder with a yellow tag reading OUT OF SERVICE, DO NOT USE, and a blue vise. A grey flat bar lies on the bench and sticks out past its right end into the walkway. Zone 4: a whiteboard with blue writing: JOB 4471, 12 BRACKETS, DUE THU 3 PM. Three wooden sawhorses. A wall outlet on the far right, with an orange extension cord running from it across the floor to the work light in zone 3."

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. "Rewrite this note so it states only what can be seen: 'The welding area is a mess.'"
2. "Name the five questions a good report note answers."
3. "You see a ladder with a cracked rail leaning against a wall. What do you write, and who do you tell?"

## Summary (2 min)

During this session we covered:

- scanning in a fixed order and checking the floor
- observation versus interpretation
- the five questions of a useful note
- reporting what appears to be unsafe
- checking documents line by line
