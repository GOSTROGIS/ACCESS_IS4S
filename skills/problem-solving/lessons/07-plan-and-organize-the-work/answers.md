# Plan and organize the work

## Task 1 (level 1)

1. Check the cut list against the drawing
2. Get the plywood and screws from the rack
3. Set up and check the saw
4. Cut the parts
5. Sand the edges
6. Assemble the units with glue and screws
7. Sweep up and put tools away
8. Send the supervisor a photo of the finished units

Tasks that could swap: getting the materials and setting up the saw (2 and 3), and cleaning up and sending the photo (7 and 8). Some learners may sand after assembly. Accept it if they explain why.

## Task 2 (level 1)

Checklist:

- 19 mm plywood (enough for 4 sides and 8 shelves, see Task 3)
- 45 mm screws
- wood glue
- 120-grit sandpaper
- table saw, tape measure, pencil, square, drill and driver bits, clamps if used
- safety glasses and hearing protection

Screws: 8 shelves × 6 screws = **48 screws**. One box of 100 is enough: **1 box**.

## Task 3 (level 2)

- Sides: 4 sides ÷ 2 sides per strip = **2 strips**
- Shelves: 8 shelves ÷ 3 shelves per strip = 2.67, so round up to **3 strips** (one shelf left over)
- Total: 2 + 3 = **5 strips**
- Sheets: each sheet gives 4 strips. 5 ÷ 4 = 1.25, so round up to **2 sheets** (3 strips left over)

Steps: always round up when you are counting whole strips or sheets. You cannot buy part of a sheet.

## Task 4 (level 2)

Action planner, for example:

- **What:** first day at Cedar Flats Builders, sign-in and orientation
- **When:** Monday, sign in by 6:45 a.m., start 7:00 a.m.
- **Where:** 2250 Mountview Road, street parking
- **How:** drive, 45 minutes
- **Resources:** hard hat, safety boots, safety glasses, high-visibility vest, work gloves, lunch, water, ID and phone
- **Anything else:** traffic, finding parking, weather, packing the night before

Leaving time, working back from the start: 7:00 − 15 min (arrive early) = 6:45. 6:45 − 15 min (traffic) = 6:30. 6:30 − 45 min (drive) = **5:45 a.m.**

## Task 5 (level 2)

Points a good response covers:

- entries such as: alarm Monday 4:45 a.m., reminder Sunday 8 p.m. "pack gear and lunch," checklist of the six items to bring, the site address saved in maps, Lena's email saved so you can reach the site office
- three checks, such as: the alarm is set for a.m. and for the right day, the phone is charged and a charger is in the truck, the sound is on, the address is saved offline or written down, anyone driving with you has the plan too

## Task 6 (level 2)

Before 10:00 you can: check the cut list, set up and check the saw, get the screws, glue and sandpaper, and set up the assembly area.

After the plywood arrives at 10:00, the remaining work is cutting, sanding, assembly and cleanup:

2 × 25 + 15 + 2 × 20 + 15 = 50 + 15 + 40 + 15 = 120 min = 2 hours.

New finish time: 10:00 + 2 hours = **12:00 noon**. Tell the supervisor the new finish time as soon as you know the delivery is late.

## Check yourself

1. "What has to be done before this can start?" (Level 1)
2. Multiplication first: 3 × 10 = 30, then 5 + 30 = **35**. (Level 1)
3. Any two of: setup, cleanup, breaks, travel, waiting for material. (Level 1)
4. That it is set for a.m. or p.m. correctly and on the right day, that the sound is on, and that the phone is charged. (Level 2)
