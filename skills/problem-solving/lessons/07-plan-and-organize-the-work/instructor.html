Plan and organize the work

Break a job into tasks, put them in order, list what you need, estimate the time, and adjust the plan when something changes.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-2
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Analyze the issue; Develop multiple routes of action; Address the issue

Opening (5 min)

Session 7 of 16 in Problem Solving. Length: 60 min.

Welcome learners. Adapted from the ACCESS warm-up "This represents me": ask each learner to find one thing in a pocket, bag or phone that shows how they keep themselves organized, such as a calendar, a list, a key ring or a bus pass. Go around the room and have each person say in one sentence how it helps them.

Then ask: "Who has ever shown up to a job or an appointment without something they needed? What would have prevented it?" Today we plan a small job and a first day on site.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • break a job into tasks and put them in a workable order (Analyze the issue: break down the issue into smaller parts)
  • make a materials and tools checklist and estimate the time using BEDMAS (Address the issue)
  • complete an action planner, including a digital reminder, and adjust a plan when a fact changes (Develop multiple routes of action, Address the issue)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. "How do you plan your day now?"
  2. "When you estimate how long a job takes, what do you usually forget?"

Learning tasks (44 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 1.1 Teach breaking a job into tasks and ordering by "what must be done first?" Assign Task 1. 1.1 Complete Task 1 and name two tasks that could swap. Learner page 6 min
1.2 1.2 Teach the checklist idea. Assign Task 2. 1.2 Complete Task 2. Learner page 6 min
1.3 1.3 Draw a 1220 mm × 2440 mm sheet on the board and show the four strips. Assign Task 3 in pairs. 1.3 Complete Task 3, sketching the strips. Learner page, board 7 min
2.1 2.1 Work the time estimate in the worked example on the board, step by step with BEDMAS. 2.1 Follow along and check each step with a calculator. Learner page, calculator 5 min
2.2 2.2 Teach the action planner. Assign Task 4. 2.2 Complete Task 4 and work out the leaving time. Learner page 8 min
2.3 2.3 Assign Task 5. Ask two learners to share what they would check. 2.3 Complete Task 5. Learner page, own phone if they choose 5 min
3.1 3.1 Assign Task 6. Discuss what to tell the supervisor about the new finish time. 3.1 Complete Task 6 and say what they would tell the supervisor. Learner page 7 min

Learning tasks: 6 + 6 + 7 + 5 + 8 + 5 + 7 = 44 min. Whole session: 5 + 2 + 3 + 44 + 4 + 2 = 60 min.

Post-assessment (4 min)

  1. "A job has setup 10 min, 3 parts at 12 min each, and cleanup 10 min. Write the total using BEDMAS and solve it."
  2. "Name the six parts of the action planner."
  3. "Your ride to work cancels the night before. What is your if-then plan?"

Summary (2 min)

During this session we covered:

  • breaking a job into tasks and ordering them
  • materials, tools and safety gear checklists
  • estimating time with BEDMAS, including setup, cleanup and breaks
  • the action planner
  • using digital reminders and checking them
  • changing the plan when a fact changes
