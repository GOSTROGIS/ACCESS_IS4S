# Spot patterns and possible causes

## Why this matters on the job

A breaker that trips every morning, welds that fail only on one shift, a crew that always finishes late on Fridays: problems that repeat leave a trail. A tradesperson who can read that trail finds the cause faster and does not waste parts swapping things at random. Patterns also help with everyday work, such as laying out studs or bolt holes.

## Key ideas

### Look for what repeats

When the same problem shows up more than once, sort the cases and ask what they share:

- **Time:** time of day, day of the week, season, right after a delivery
- **Place:** one machine, one booth, one floor, one side of the building
- **People and tools:** one crew, one shift, one batch of material, one tool
- **Conditions:** cold, wet, busy, after a change

### A pattern is a clue, not proof

Two things happening together does not prove one causes the other. If welds fail on the night shift and the night shift always uses booth 3, the cause could be the shift **or** the booth. To find out, change one thing and keep the rest the same.

### Break the problem into parts

A big problem like "the crew keeps finishing late" is easier to analyze in parts. Common headings are **people, materials, equipment, method** and **environment**. Put each known fact under a heading. The headings organize your questions. They do not prove a cause.

### Possible cause and effect

Write a possible link as: **"If ___ is causing ___, then we should see ___."** Then go and look for it. If you do not see it, drop that idea or change it.

### Worked example

Stud marks on a wall plate at Riverbend Framing (fictional) are laid out every 400 mm, starting at 0: 0, 400, 800, 1200 and so on. Where is the 9th mark?

The 1st mark is at 0, so the 9th mark is 8 spaces along. Using BEDMAS, brackets first, then multiply:

(9 − 1) × 400 = 8 × 400 = 3200 mm

Where the pattern breaks tells you something too. If a plate reads 0, 400, 800, 1220, 1600, the 1220 mark breaks the pattern. Either it is an error, or something is there on purpose, such as an opening on the plan. Check before you cut.

## Practice

**Task 1** (level 1) Find the next two numbers and say what the pattern is.

- a. 2, 4, 6, 8, \_\_, \_\_
- b. 0, 400, 800, 1200, \_\_, \_\_
- c. 25, 50, 75, 100, \_\_, \_\_
- d. 2, 4, 6, 2, 4, 6, \_\_, \_\_

For a., give a second way the pattern could carry on that still fits the first numbers.

**Task 2** (level 2) A wall plate is laid out every 400 mm starting at 0. How far from the start is the 14th mark? Show the steps with BEDMAS. Then find the mark in this list that breaks the pattern, and say what you would check: 0, 400, 800, 1200, 1600, 2000, 2440, 2800.

**Task 3** (level 2) Northshore Pipe and Steel (fictional) bend-tests weld samples. Here is the record for the last two weeks.

| Shift | Booth | Welder | Samples tested | Samples failed |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Day | 1 | Mara | 12 | 1 |
| Day | 2 | Tyson | 12 | 0 |
| Night | 3 | Wes | 12 | 4 |
| Night | 3 | Kiri | 12 | 5 |

Extra facts: Night shift only uses booth 3. Wes and Kiri both passed all their test samples in booth 1 last month. Booth 3 is next to the big overhead door.

- a. Add up the samples tested and failed for each shift. What fraction failed on each shift? Give each as a percentage.
- b. What pattern do you see?
- c. Does the record show that the welders are the cause? Explain.
- d. Name two possible causes the record cannot tell apart.
- e. Describe one test that would tell them apart. Change one thing only.

**Task 4** (level 2) The site trailer at a fictional job site has a breaker that trips. Here is the log.

| Date | Day | Time | Who was in the trailer | What was plugged in and on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 2 | Monday | 7:05 a.m. | 6 people | heater, kettle, microwave, phone chargers |
| Nov 3 | Tuesday | 7:10 a.m. | 7 people | heater, kettle, microwave, phone chargers |
| Nov 4 | Wednesday | none | 6 people | heater, phone chargers (kettle broken) |
| Nov 5 | Thursday | 7:00 a.m. | 6 people | heater, new kettle, microwave, phone chargers |
| Nov 7 | Saturday | none | 2 people | heater, phone chargers |

- a. Write the pattern in one sentence.
- b. Write one "If ___ is causing ___, then we should see ___" statement that fits the log.
- c. Who should you give this log to, and why should you not try to fix the electrical system yourself?

**Task 5** (level 3) The concrete crew at Lakeview Concrete Forming (fictional) has finished late four Fridays in a row. Sort these facts under the headings **people**, **materials**, **equipment**, **method** and **environment**. Then pick the heading you would look into first and explain why.

- Concrete is delivered at 10 a.m. on Fridays and 7 a.m. on other days.
- Two of the five crew members leave at 2 p.m. on Fridays for a course.
- The vibrator broke on two of the four Fridays.
- It rained on one of the four Fridays.
- On Fridays the crew also strips last week's forms before the pour.
- Monday to Thursday the crew finishes on time.

## Check yourself

1. Name four kinds of things to compare when a problem repeats.
2. Why is a pattern not proof of a cause?
3. Using BEDMAS, how far along is the 6th mark if marks are 400 mm apart starting at 0?
4. What does "change one thing at a time" help you find out?
