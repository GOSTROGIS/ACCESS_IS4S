Break a job into a work breakdown structure

Split a whole job into the pieces you will hand over, number them, check that nothing is missing or extra, and write a work package card for each piece.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Identify the issue to address; Analyze the issue

Opening (5 min)

Session 11 of 16 in Problem Solving. Length: 60 min.

Welcome learners. Ask: "Has anyone ever finished a job and then found a piece nobody planned for? The cleanup, the last trim, the trip to the dump?" Take two or three stories. Write on the board: What exactly are we building? Tell the group that today they break a job into pieces before they plan the days, which is the step before the network diagrams in Numeracy lesson 19.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • name the goal of a job and the pieces that make it up (Identify the issue to address: identify the goals and objectives you need to achieve)
  • break a job into numbered pieces down to work packages (Analyze the issue: break down the issue into smaller parts)
  • check a WBS for missing, extra and doubled pieces (Analyze the issue: think critically about the issue using the information gathered)
  • write a work package card and turn work packages into activities (Analyze the issue)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. "When you get a job, how do you decide what needs doing first?"
  2. "What parts of a job do people forget to price or plan?"

Learning tasks (44 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 1.1 Teach the family tree idea and pieces versus activities. Assign Task 1. 1.1 Complete Task 1 and read their sort aloud. Learner page 6 min
1.2 1.2 Teach the numbering. Draw the deck WBS on the board as a tree. Assign Task 2 in pairs. 1.2 Complete Task 2 on paper as a tree or an indented list. Learner page, board 8 min
1.3 1.3 Teach the 100 percent check. Assign Task 3. Ask three learners to name one problem each. 1.3 Complete Task 3. Learner page 7 min
2.1 2.1 Teach work packages. Walk through the pier footings card and show the BEDMAS in the cost line on the board. 2.1 Follow along and check the cost with a calculator. Learner page, calculator 5 min
2.2 2.2 Assign Task 4 in pairs. Circulate and check the hours and cost lines. 2.2 Complete Task 4. Learner page, calculator 12 min
2.3 2.3 Show how work packages become activities. Assign Task 5 and open the deck table in Numeracy lesson 19 on screen. 2.3 Complete Task 5 and compare with the Numeracy table. Learner page, projector 6 min

Learning tasks: 6 + 8 + 7 + 5 + 12 + 6 = 44 min. Whole session: 5 + 2 + 3 + 44 + 4 + 2 = 60 min.

Link for step 2.3: Numeracy lesson 19. Learners who want more job-planning number practice can use the Numeracy Hub Construction Operations tab.

Post-assessment (4 min)

  1. "Write a WBS with three big pieces for painting one room."
  2. "A WBS has 'Install doors' under 1.3. What is wrong with that line, and how would you fix it?"
  3. "Write a done-when line for a work package called 'Kitchen backsplash tile'."

Summary (2 min)

During this session we covered:

  • a work breakdown structure as a family tree for the job
  • pieces (things) before activities (actions)
  • numbering pieces 1, 1.1, 1.1.1
  • the 100 percent check: nothing missing, nothing extra, nothing in two places
  • work packages and the work package card
  • turning work packages into the activity list for the schedule
