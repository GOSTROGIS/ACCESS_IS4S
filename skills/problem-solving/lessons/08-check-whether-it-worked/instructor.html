Problem solving · Lesson 08 · Instructor

Check whether the fix worked

Decide what success looks like, test the fix under real conditions, follow up, ask for feedback and record what you learned.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 60 min
  • Components: Address the issue; Evaluate the effectiveness of the solution or decision

Opening (5 min)

Session 8 of 10 in Problem Solving. Length: 60 min.

Welcome learners. Ask: "Have you ever fixed something that broke again a week later? A leaky tap, a car, a phone, a door?" Take two or three stories. For each one ask: "When did you find out the fix had not lasted? What would have told you sooner?"

Write the last three of the ACCESS decision steps on the board: implement the decision, follow up on the decision, evaluate and revise if necessary. Tell the group this lesson is about those three.

Objective (2 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • write observable success checks and test a fix under real conditions (Evaluate the effectiveness of the solution or decision)
  • read a follow-up log, spot a returning problem and plan short-term and long-term actions (Address the issue: monitor and adjust)
  • compare before and after rates and state only what the record supports (Evaluate the effectiveness of the solution or decision)
  • ask for feedback and write a lesson learned (Evaluate the effectiveness of the solution or decision: provide opportunities for feedback, identify best practices and lessons learned)

Pre-assessment (3 min)

  1. "How do you know a repair is really finished?"
  2. "Who would you ask to find out whether a fix is working?"

Learning tasks (44 min)

Step Instructor activities Learner activities Resources Time
1.1 1.1 Teach success checks, real conditions and side effects with the shop vac worked example. Assign Task 1. 1.1 Complete Task 1 and read one check aloud. Learner page 7 min
1.2 1.2 Teach follow-up. Assign Task 2 in pairs. 1.2 Complete Task 2. Learner page 9 min
2.1 2.1 Teach rates versus counts and why two changes at once hide the cause. Work a quick example on the board: 2 of 24 compared with 2 of 200. 2.1 Follow along and work the example. Board, calculator 5 min
2.2 2.2 Assign Task 4 in pairs. Check the percentages aloud and discuss each statement in part c. 2.2 Complete Task 4. Learner page, calculator 10 min
3.1 3.1 Assign Task 3. Ask learners to share who else they would ask. 3.1 Complete Task 3. Learner page 5 min
3.2 3.2 Assign Task 5. Ask two learners to read their lesson learned as if giving a toolbox talk. 3.2 Write and present the lesson learned. Learner page 8 min

Learning tasks: 7 + 9 + 5 + 10 + 5 + 8 = 44 min. Whole session: 5 + 2 + 3 + 44 + 4 + 2 = 60 min.

Post-assessment (4 min)

  1. "You re-hung a door that stuck. Write two success checks."
  2. "Before a change, 6 of 30 parts failed inspection. After, 2 of 30 failed. What were the two rates?"
  3. "What goes into a lesson learned?"

Summary (2 min)

During this session we covered:

  • writing success checks before testing
  • testing under real conditions and watching for side effects
  • following up and spotting a returning problem
  • comparing rates, and why two changes at once hide the cause
  • asking for feedback and recording lessons learned
