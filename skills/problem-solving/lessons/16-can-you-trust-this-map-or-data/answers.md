# Decide whether a map or data is good enough to act on

## Task 1 (level 1)

- a. **Precise, not accurate.** Same reading every time, but 6 mm off.
- b. **Both.** The readings agree with each other and with the true length.
- c. **Neither.** A rough guess, and 6 feet off.
- d. **Accurate on average, not precise.** The readings jump around 25 kg. If a learner says "neither" because no single reading is exactly right, accept it with that reason.

## Task 4 (level 2)

- a. 2.8 − 1.5 = **1.3 m** closer than the drawing showed.
- b. 234 × 1000 = **234,000 litres**.
- c. 9.8 − 4 = **5.8 m**.
- d. **When:** the drawings were based on a 1957 record, so the information was old. **Can I check it:** the line could have been located on site along the whole street before digging. Either answer, with the reason, is accepted. Both is best.

## Task 5 (level 3)

- a. Up to **12 m** in any direction, across a total width of 12 + 12 = **24 m**.
- b. **No.** The possible error (24 m across) is 8 times the width of the driveway: 24 ÷ 3 = 8.
- c. Many decimal places make a number look precise, but the stated accuracy is what tells you how close it is to the truth. Extra digits do not make it more accurate.

## Task 6 (level 2)

Steps in a sensible order:

1. Mark the planned post line and mark where each of the 12 holes will go.
2. Request a locate from BC 1 Call online at bc1c.ca or by phone at 1-800-474-6886. Have ready: contact information, the exact location, the depth of the holes, the start date, whether it is public or private property, and where on the property you will dig.
3. Wait for the responses. For a regular request, BC 1 Call members have 3 business days to respond. Do not dig until every owner has responded.
4. Ask the property owner about private lines they installed themselves, such as power to a shed or an irrigation line.
5. On site, check the paint marks or plans from each owner against your post layout. Follow each owner's requirements for working near their line (section 20.79(2)). Do not use pointed tools to probe for gas or electrical lines (section 20.79(3)).
6. Move any post that falls too close to a marked line, as the owner's instructions say, then auger the first hole.

A strong answer includes the locate request, the wait for responses, the on-site check of marks, and following the owners' requirements before any powered digging.

4. The location of all underground utility services in the area must be accurately determined, and any danger to workers from them must be controlled. (Level 2)
