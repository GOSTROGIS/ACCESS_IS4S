# Pitching an improvement to a supervisor

Session 7 of 12 in the Creativity and Innovation lessons. Total length 75 minutes.

## Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Act out two short pitches yourself, playing a worker talking to a foreman. First: "Hey, this way of doing it is dumb, we should change it." Second: a 30-second version of Jordan's pitch from the learner page. Ask: "Which one would you say yes to as a foreman? Why?"

## Objective (3 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

- plan and deliver a two-minute pitch with a problem, idea, test, cost and time, safety and a clear ask (Apply your ideas)
- answer a supervisor's questions and take on a supervisor's or peer's idea without judging it first (Use your imagination and curiosity)
- coach a partner's pitch by asking questions and offering an idea (Facilitate a creative and innovative environment for yourself and others)
- state the saving in numbers using BEDMAS

## Pre-assessment (4 min)

1. Think of a time you suggested a change at work, school or home. What happened, and why?
2. What do you think a supervisor needs to know before saying yes to a new way of working?

## Learning tasks

The table covers the whole session, including the opening, objective, pre-assessment, post-assessment and summary blocks.

| Step | Instructor activities | Learner activities | Resources | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Act out the two opening pitches and ask the question. | Compare the pitches. | None | 5 min |
| 1.2 | State the objectives. | Ask questions. | Learner page | 3 min |
| 1.3 | Pose the pre-assessment questions. | Answer on paper. | Paper | 4 min |
| 2.1 | Teach the key ideas: timing, the six parts, listening, being open, "not yet", credit, the safety committee path. Read Jordan's pitch and mark each of the six parts. | Name each part as it comes up in the example. | Learner page | 10 min |
| 2.2 | Set Task 1. Help learners choose a real or earlier-lesson idea. | Fill in the pitch planner. | Learner page, earlier lesson work | 10 min |

| 2.4 | Pair learners. Run Tasks 3 and 4: each learner pitches once and plays a supervisor once, using a different card each time. Time each pitch at 2 minutes. | 2.5 | Set Task 5. | Write a response to "not yet". | Learner page | 6 min |
| 2.6 | Set Task 6. Ask two learners to share an idea they took from their partner. | Write one idea to add. | Learner page | 5 min |

**Coaching tip.** Learners playing card C often enjoy the role a bit too much. Remind them the goal is realistic questions, not winning. After each pair, ask the presenter which question helped them most.

## Post-assessment (5 min)

1. Which part of your pitch did your supervisor ask the most about? How would you change that part?
2. Give one example of a clear, small ask for an idea from today.
3. A supervisor says your idea will not work because "we've always done it this way." What do you say next?

## Summary (2 min)

During this session we covered:

- choosing a good moment to pitch
- the six parts: problem, idea, test, cost and time, safety, ask
- using numbers to show the saving
- listening to questions and being open to the supervisor's ideas
- what to do with "no" or "not yet"
- giving credit to others who helped
- the joint health and safety committee or worker representative as a path for safety ideas
