# Picturing yourself in a trade

## Task 1 (level 1)

Answers will vary. A good response:

- names three different settings, not three versions of one job (for example, a framing crew on a townhouse site, a structural steel fab shop, a maintenance crew at a sawmill)
- gives one concrete action for each, with a tool, task or place ("bolting a conveyor guard back on", not "working")

## Task 2 (level 1)

Open task. A good turn in the career chair:

- names two or three areas of interest in plain words
- listens to every comment without interrupting or arguing
- when invited, responds by saying which idea surprised them or which they want to learn more about
- passing on the turn is a valid choice and is not marked down

## Task 3 (level 1)

Open task. A good comment:

- starts with "I can picture you … because …"
- names a setting or task
- gives a reason based on something the speaker has seen the person do in class
- is positive and leaves the idea open

Example: "I can picture you as a sheet metal worker, because your layouts in numeracy class are always neat and exact."

## Task 4 (level 1)

Open task. Good scribe notes:

- record each idea in the speaker's own words
- leave out the scribe's own opinion
- are handed to the person in the chair at the end

## Task 5 (level 2)

Answers will vary. A good response:

- picks an idea from the scribe notes or their Task 1 list; a familiar idea needing further investigation is also suitable
- asks three questions that would really help decide fit, such as what a normal day looks like, whether the work is shift work or travel, how long the apprenticeship takes, what the physical demands are
- names a real place to look: a WorkBC career profile, a SkilledTradesBC trade profile, an instructor, or a person working in the trade

## Check yourself

**1.** Imagination is picturing a possibility ("what if I worked at a mill?"). Curiosity is asking questions about it ("what would I do there all day?"). Level 1.

**2.** Any positive, specific comment with a reason. Example: "I can picture you on a framing crew, because you like moving and working with a team." Level 1.

**3.** So they hear every idea before reacting. If they answer each comment, they may argue with ideas and others may stop offering them. Level 1.

**4.** Any one of: read a WorkBC career profile, read a SkilledTradesBC trade profile, talk to someone working in the trade, visit a shop or site with the class, ask the instructor. Level 1.
