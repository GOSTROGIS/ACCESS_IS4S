Sketch your idea so others can build it

Sketch an idea in isometric and in three views with the right lines and sizes, so someone else can build it without you there.

  • Learner
  • Instructor🔒 (instructor code)
  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 1-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Develop your ideas; Apply your ideas; Use your imagination and curiosity

Task 1 (level 1)

The top view goes directly above the front view. The right side view goes directly to the right of the front view.

Task 2 (level 1)

a) Centre line.

b) Visible line (also called an object line).

c) Hidden line.

Task 3 (level 2)

a) Length 100 mm (front view), depth 60 mm (top view), height 50 mm (front view).

b) 20 mm (right side view).

c) 50 mm from the left end and 20 mm from the front edge (top view).

d) From the front you cannot see the hole, because it is inside the base. Hidden edges are drawn as dashed lines. From the top you look straight into the hole, so its edge is visible and drawn as a solid circle.

Task 4 (level 2)

Open answer. Check that:

  • vertical edges are drawn vertical, and the other edges run along the two 30° axes
  • the box is 6, 4 and 3 squares along the right axes
  • the notch is 2 squares wide, 1 square deep, in the middle of the top, and runs all the way from front to back
  • only visible edges are drawn

Task 5 (level 2)

The views depend on which face the learner calls the front. If the front is the 6-square face where the notch shows:

  • Front view: a rectangle 6 wide and 3 high, with the notch shown as a 2 by 1 step down in the middle of the top edge. All lines visible.
  • Top view (above the front): a rectangle 6 by 4, with two lines running front to back 2 squares apart in the middle. These are the edges of the notch. All lines visible.
  • Right side view (to the right of the front): a rectangle 4 wide and 3 high, with one hidden line across the whole width, 1 square below the top. That is the bottom of the notch, which you cannot see from the side.

Task 6 (level 2)

  1. The right side view is on the wrong side. It is drawn to the left of the front view. In third-angle layout it goes to the right of the front view.
  2. Hidden lines for the hole are missing. The hole goes through the base, so it should show as two dashed lines with a centre line in the front view and in the right side view.

Also accept: no sizes, and no centre lines on the hole in the top view.

Task 7 (level 2)

40 mm. In the top view, the line where the base meets the upright is drawn 40 mm from the front edge. Or from the sizes: the block is 60 mm deep (top view) and the upright is 20 mm thick (right side view). 60 − 20 = 40 mm.

Task 8 (level 3)

Open answer. A good drawing has:

  • Top view: a rectangle 300 by 99 (the 80 mm strip plus the 19 mm fence), a visible line where the strip meets the fence, and the holes drawn as circles in a row 37 mm from the fence face, with centre lines. A note "9 holes, Ø5 thru, 32 mm centres".
  • Front view (looking at the fence face): a rectangle 300 wide and 31 high. The holes are behind the fence, so any hole shown is drawn with dashed lines.
  • Right side view: an L shape. The strip is 80 wide and 12 thick. The fence is 19 wide and 31 tall, hanging 19 below the strip.
  • Views in third-angle layout, with the main sizes (300, 80, 12, 19, 31, 37, 32).

Task 9 (level 3)

Open answer. A good response names specific missing details, for example a missing hole location, no thickness, or a view that did not line up. The best learning is in the list of what was missing.

Task 10 (level 3)

a) Top view: a square 200 by 200 with a circle 60 across in the centre. All lines are visible. You can see the top of the bar and the plate around it.

b) Front view: a wide, thin rectangle 200 wide and 10 high for the plate, with a tall rectangle 60 wide and 400 high standing on it in the centre. All lines are visible.

c) In the top view, two centre lines cross at the centre of the circle. In the front view, one vertical centre line runs up the middle of the bar.

Check yourself

1. 30° above the horizontal, one to the left and one to the right. Level 1.

2. The front view, the top view directly above it, and the right side view directly to the right of it. Level 1.

3. A hidden edge, one you cannot see from that side. Level 1.

4. It is the real test of whether the drawing has everything a builder needs. Level 2.
