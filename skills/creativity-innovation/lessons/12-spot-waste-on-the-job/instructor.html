Spot waste on the job

Walk a work area, spot waiting, extra trips, rework and extra stock, measure one waste, and pick the one most worth fixing.

  • Learner

  • Answers🔒 (instructor code)
  • Level: 2-3
  • Time: 90 min
  • Components: Identify opportunities for you to innovate; Use your imagination and curiosity; Facilitate a creative and innovative environment for yourself and others

Session 12 of 12 in the Creativity and Innovation lessons. Total length 90 minutes.

Opening (5 min)

Welcome learners. Ask: "Think of your last day at work or in the shop. Where did time go that did not build anything?" Write answers on the board without judging. Say: "Today you go looking for that lost time on purpose, measure it, and pick one thing to fix. This is the last Creativity and Innovation session, and it uses the opportunity statement from lesson 2, the scoring from lesson 3 and the measuring from lesson 11."

Objective (3 min)

By the end of the session, learners will be able to:

  • name and spot four common wastes: waiting, extra trips, rework and extra stock (Identify opportunities for you to innovate)
  • plan and run a short, safe waste walk with a tally sheet (Use your imagination and curiosity)
  • measure the time lost to one waste and score wastes to choose one to fix (Identify opportunities for you to innovate)
  • talk about waste in terms of the process, not the person, so others feel safe to share ideas (Facilitate a creative and innovative environment for yourself and others)

Pre-assessment (4 min)

  1. Name one thing on a job site that wastes time.
  2. A trip takes 3 minutes and happens 10 times a day. How many minutes a day is that?

Learning tasks

The table covers the whole session, including the opening, objective, pre-assessment, post-assessment and summary blocks.

3.1 Run the post-assessment. Answer the three questions. Paper 5 min

Safety. During the waste walk, learners stay in areas they are allowed in, wear the PPE the area needs and do not stand near moving machines or vehicles to watch. Remind them that they are watching the work, not judging the people doing it.

Post-assessment (5 min)

  1. A crew of 2 waits 20 minutes each morning for a key. How many crew-minutes is that in a 5-day week? (200)
  2. Name the four wastes from this lesson.
  3. Rewrite "Joe is lazy about cleaning up" so it is about the process.

Summary (3 min)

During this session we covered:

  • four wastes: waiting, extra trips, rework and extra stock
  • running a safe waste walk with a tally sheet
  • measuring the time lost to a waste
  • scoring wastes and choosing one to fix
  • talking about the process, not the person
