Skill 9 of 9

Creativity and innovation

This small fictional example shows the skill in use. NEED: Find the current card quickly. IDEAS: Divider tabs or a labelled tray.

Lessons

8 ACCESS lessons for creativity and innovation, each with a learner page, an instructor lesson plan and answers.

1Picturing yourself in a trade

Use your imagination and the views of others to try on work you may not have pictured before you choose a trade. 60 min · Level 1.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

2Spotting a better way on the job site

Look hard at a routine job-site task, question the habits behind it and find where a change could help. 60 min · Level 1-2.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

3Many ideas before you choose

Come up with a wide range of ideas for a repeated shop task, without judging them, then pick one for a reason. 75 min · Level 2.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

4Combining two ideas into something new

Plan a week of work camp meals that blends two food traditions and still follows Canada's food guide. 90 min · Level 2.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

5Building a quick model and explaining it

Turn an idea into a quick model with building bricks, then give spoken directions clear enough for someone else to build it. 60 min · Level 1-2.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

6Testing an idea and learning from what fails

Build a cardboard bracket, load-test it, and use each failure to make the next version better. 90 min · Level 2-3.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

7Pitching an improvement to a supervisor

Present a tested idea to a supervisor in two minutes, answer their questions, and take their ideas on board. 75 min · Level 2-3.

  • Learner
  • Instructor
  • Answers

8Designing your own personal mark

Design an original symbol for your toolbox, business card or portfolio, research what it stands for, and present it. 90 min · Level 1-2.

  • Instructor
