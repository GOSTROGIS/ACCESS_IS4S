Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Hidden lineShows edges and surfaces you cannot see.
  • Cutting plane lineShows the path of an imaginary cut through the part.
  • Break lineShows the part was shortened on the drawing.
  • Phantom lineRemoves repeated detail.
  • IsometricThe axes are drawn at 30 degrees. No view faces you directly.
  • Metric drawingsDimensions are in millimetres. A special note says the drawing is metric.
  • Welding symbolThe lower side of the reference line is the arrow side. A tail is not always needed.
  • Title blockFabricator, customer, job number, date, detailer, checker, revisions, material grade, paint, electrodes.
