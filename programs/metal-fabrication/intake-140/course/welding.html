⚡ Arc welding and welding symbols

Understand SMAW, GMAW, electrodes, positions, faults and the bend test.

  • Read and listen
  • Games
  • Pictures
  • Documents

Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Arc lengthA longer arc raises arc voltage and lowers amperage.
  • Lens shadeOver 300 amps SMAW uses a No. 12 lens. GMAW and FCAW use 10 to 12.
  • Electrode codeA 3 in the next-to-last digit means flat position only.
  • Fillet weld strengthSet by the effective throat.
  • Undersized weldsOften caused by travelling too fast.
  • GMAW currentDCEP is most common. Short-circuit transfer works in all positions.
  • Shielding gasCO2 in the arc can make carbon monoxide. Aluminum wire uses 100% argon.
  • Bend testThe CWB bend test coupon and the worked numbers are in the guided bend test lesson.
