⛑ Safe work practices

Know the PPE, the shop rules, lockout, fire and WHMIS.

  • Read and listen
  • Games
  • Pictures
  • Documents

Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Minimum PPE in the shopFlame resistant pants or coveralls, CSA approved safety boots, CSA approved safety glasses.
  • Welding area addsFull-length flame resistant shirt or leather jacket, hearing protection, welding gloves, welding shield.
  • Grinding and cutting area addsHearing protection, a face shield, fitting gloves for grinding, welding gloves for cutting.
  • No jewelleryNo rings, loose watchbands, bracelets or neck chains. They catch in machinery.
  • Hair and sleevesTie long hair back. Button sleeves at the wrist or roll them to the elbow.
  • NoiseShop noise is usually over 85 dB. Plasma and carbon arc cutting can go over 120 dB. Wear hearing protection.
  • Unsafe conditionReport it to your instructor. You have the right to refuse unsafe work.
  • Serious hazardPut up a barrier or a "danger" tag, then report it.
  • LockoutOnly the person who put the lock on takes it off.
  • Fire alarmGo to the nearest muster point.
  • WHMISWorkplace Hazardous Materials Information System. You learn the hazards from labels, the SDS and training.
  • Toolbox meetingHeld weekly. High risk work may need a Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) first.
