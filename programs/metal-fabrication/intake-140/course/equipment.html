⚙ Shop equipment

Lock out and run the shear, ironworker, rolls, bandsaw and brake press safely.

  • Read and listen
  • Games
  • Pictures
  • Documents

Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Five machinesPlate shear, ironworker, plate rolls, horizontal bandsaw, brake press.
  • Before any machineKnow the controls. Check for slip or trip hazards. Check the guards.
  • Never cut or formUnknown material, stacked material, or material with welds or oxy-fuel cuts on the surface.
  • Stainless steelDecrease shear and ironworker capacity by one third.
  • Plate shearNever cut more than 1/2 inch. Do not cut less than three times the material thickness.
  • Plate shear guardA light curtain. Blocking the beam stops the blade coming down.
  • Plate rollsDo not wear gloves. Roll in the middle of the rolls. Pinch pressure maximum 700 psi.
  • BandsawIf the blade starts to click, lift the head out, stop the blade, tell your instructor.
  • Brake pressWork in the centre. Never leave it running unattended.
  • OperatorThe operator is responsible for the safety of the helper.
