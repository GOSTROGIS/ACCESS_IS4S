Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Study guidesTen step-by-step lessons with audio and TI-30Xa keys are in the Intake 140 study guides.
  • Decimal of a footInches divided by 12 gives the decimal of a foot.
  • Mean diameterMD = ID + T = OD - T. Stretch-out SO = pi x MD.
  • Plate mass7.85 kg per square metre per millimetre. 40.8 lb per square foot per inch.
  • Capacity1 cubic metre = 1000 litres. 1 cubic foot = 6.25 gallons (course value).
  • RoundingFractions to the nearest 1/16 inch. Metric to the nearest millimetre.
