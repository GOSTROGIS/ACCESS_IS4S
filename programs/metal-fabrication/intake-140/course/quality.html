Know the metals, how steel is made and how to tell them apart.

Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • FerrousContains 5% or more iron. Non-ferrous has less than 5%, often none.
  • AluminumNon-ferrous. Told apart from steel by its light weight.
  • CarbonMore carbon raises tensile strength and makes steel harder to form.
  • ChromiumAdded for corrosion and abrasion resistance.
  • SAE 1020A plain carbon steel.
  • RollingStructural shapes and bars are hot rolled. Most sheet is finished by cold rolling.
  • SteelmakingMost steel comes from the basic oxygen furnace.
  • Spark testWear a face shield.
