🔥 Thermal cutting

Set up and use oxy-fuel, plasma and carbon arc gouging safely.

  • Read and listen
  • Games
  • Pictures
  • Documents

Read and listen

The voice reads the key points slowly. Pause any time.

  • Oxy-fuel cuttingRapid oxidation. Used mainly on carbon steels.
  • Kerf and slagThe cut opening is the kerf. The molten oxide is slag.
  • AcetyleneNever use copper fittings. Never open the acetylene valve fully, so you can close it fast.
  • HosesOxygen hoses are usually green.
  • CylindersStore oxygen and fuel gas in separate rooms. Keep liquid fuel cylinders upright.
  • FlamesToo much acetylene gives a carburizing flame.
  • Plasma (PAC)DCEN polarity. Little heat in the base metal, so little distortion.
  • Carbon arc (CAC-A)Never use on lead, cadmium or beryllium. Toxic fumes.
