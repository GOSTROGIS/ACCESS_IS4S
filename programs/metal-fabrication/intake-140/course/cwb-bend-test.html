CWB all-position bend test

Know the CWB all-position bend test coupon: the plates, the stops, the straps and the numbers to check.

Listen to the coupon facts.

The CWB all-position bend test coupon is two 10 millimetre plates, 75 by 150, on a 50 by 6 by 180 millimetre back-up bar. Plate 1 is bevelled 30 degrees. Plate 2 is square. The first pass stops at about two thirds to three quarters of the joint. The second pass stops at about one quarter to one third. Tell the inspector once those two stops are made. Three bend straps, 38 millimetres each, are cut below a 19 millimetre cut-off: root bend, face bend, root bend.

The coupon, to scale

Every stop range lands inside a root bend strap. The 2nd pass stop is in the root bend nearest the start. The 1st pass stop is in the root bend at the far end.

The handout numbers, worked

TI-30Xa: TAN goes after the angle, in DEG. For inches, take off the whole inches, × 16, round to the nearest sixteenth.

Checks on the handout

  • Plate width. 75 mm is 2 15/16". The handout prints 3 1/2" (88.9 mm). Ask which width the coupons are cut to.
  • 1st pass stop. 2/3 to 3/4 of the joint is 100 to 112.5 mm (4" to 4 1/2"). The bracket, 112 to 125 mm (4 1/2" to 5"), is 3/4 to 5/6. They overlap only at about 112 mm (4 1/2"). Ask the inspector which one they hold you to.
  • Bar and plate length. 180 mm = 7 1/16" and 150 mm = 5 7/8", printed 7" and 6". Nominal sizes. Nothing to change.
  • Not on the handout page. Root opening for each position, electrode diameters and root face. The handout says these are given. Ask for them.
