Listen: worked examples

Two more examples, a mistake to watch for, and practice questions, read aloud.

Worked example 1. Check a frame is square

  1. Square both sides. Add.

    48² + 36² = 2,304 + 1,296 = 3,600

  2. Square root.

    √3,600 = 60"

    On the TI-30Xa

    48x²+36x²=√x

    Screen 60.

  3. Measure the diagonal. 60" means the frame is square.

    Both diagonals equal = square

Answer: 60"
