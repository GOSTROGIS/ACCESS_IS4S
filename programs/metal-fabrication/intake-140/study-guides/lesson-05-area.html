Two more examples, a mistake to watch for, and practice questions, read aloud.

Worked example 1. A plate with a round hole

  1. Area of the plate.

    36 × 24 = 864 sq in

  2. Area of the hole: π × r × r.

    π × 4 × 4 = 50.27 sq in

    On the TI-30Xa

    4x²×π=

    Screen 50.26548246

  3. Take the hole away.

    864 − 50.27 = 813.73 sq in

    On the TI-30Xa

    36×24−4x²×π=

    Screen 813.7345175

Answer: 813.73 sq in

Worked example 2. A round plate in square metres

  1. Radius = 600 ÷ 2 = 300 mm.

    300 mm = 0.3 m

  2. A = π × r × r.

    π × 0.3 × 0.3 = 0.2827 sq m

    On the TI-30Xa

    .3x²×π=

    Screen 0.282743339

Answer: 0.2827 sq m

Watch out: Forgetting to divide by 144.

A square foot is 12 × 12 = 144 square inches.
