Lesson 7. Formed plate and radiused sections

Find the flat length of a plate before it is bent.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. Bend line: the middle of the plate.

    MR = IR + T ÷ 2 = 1 + 0.25 = 1.25"

  2. Length of a 90° bend.

    B = π × 1.25 × 90 ÷ 180 = 1.9635"

  3. Straight legs. Take off IR + T.

    A = 6 − 1.5 = 4.5". C = 6 − 1.5 = 4.5"

  4. Flat length = A + B + C.

    4.5 + 1.9635 + 4.5 = 10.9635 → 10 15/16"

  5. Quick rule for 90° parts.

    Up to 3/8" thick: add the inside legs. 7/16" or more: add the mean legs.
