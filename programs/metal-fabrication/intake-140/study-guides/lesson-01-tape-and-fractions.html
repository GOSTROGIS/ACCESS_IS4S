Lesson 1. Tape and fractions

Read the tape to the nearest 1/16". Add and subtract lengths.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. One inch has 16 small marks.

    1" = 16/16"

  2. Count the marks. Then make the fraction smaller.

    14/16 = 7/8. 8/16 = 1/2. 4/16 = 1/4.

  3. Add two lengths on the calculator. Use the ab/c key.

    5 7/8" + 2 3/4" = 8 5/8"

    On the TI-30Xa

    5a b/c7a b/c8+2a b/c3a b/c4=

    Screen 8⌟5⌟8

  4. Change a fraction to a decimal. Top ÷ bottom.

    7/8 = 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875

    On the TI-30Xa

    7÷8=

    Screen 0.875

Listen: worked examples

Two more examples, a mistake to watch for, and practice questions, read aloud.

Worked example 1. Cut a piece off a bar

  1. Write both lengths in sixteenths.

    3/8 = 6/16. So 12 6/16" − 4 15/16".

  2. 6/16 is less than 15/16. Borrow one inch.

    12 6/16 = 11 22/16

  3. Subtract.

    11 22/16 − 4 15/16 = 7 7/16"

    On the TI-30Xa

    12a b/c3a b/c8−4a b/c15a b/c16=

    Screen 7⌟7⌟16

Answer: 7 7/16"

Worked example 2. Find the centre of a bar

  1. Centre = length ÷ 2.

    37 5/8 ÷ 2

  2. Use ab/c for the fraction.

    37 5/8 ÷ 2 = 18 13/16"

    On the TI-30Xa

    37a b/c5a b/c8÷2=

    Screen 18⌟13⌟16

  3. Mark 18 13/16" from one end. Check from the other end.

    18 13/16 + 18 13/16 = 37 5/8"

Answer: 18 13/16"

Watch out: Counting eighth marks as sixteenths.

Check which marks you count. The smallest marks are sixteenths.
