Lesson 1. Tape and fractions

Read the tape to the nearest 1/16". Add and subtract lengths.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. One inch has 16 small marks.

    1" = 16/16"

  2. Count the marks. Then make the fraction smaller.

    14/16 = 7/8. 8/16 = 1/2. 4/16 = 1/4.

  3. Add two lengths on the calculator. Use the ab/c key.

    5 7/8" + 2 3/4" = 8 5/8"

    On the TI-30Xa

    5a b/c7a b/c8+2a b/c3a b/c4=

    Screen 8⌟5⌟8

  4. Change a fraction to a decimal. Top ÷ bottom.

    7/8 = 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875

    On the TI-30Xa

    7÷8=
