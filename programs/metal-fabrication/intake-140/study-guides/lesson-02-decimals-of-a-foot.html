Lesson 2. Decimals of a foot

Change feet and inches to decimal feet, and back again.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. Start with the fraction. Top ÷ bottom.

    7 ÷ 8 = 0.875

  2. Add the inches.

    0.875 + 4 = 4.875"

  3. Divide by 12. Now it is feet.

    4.875 ÷ 12 = 0.40625

  4. Add the whole feet.

    0.40625 + 6 = 6.40625'

    On the TI-30Xa

    7÷8+4=÷12+6=

    Screen 6.40625

  5. Going back: decimal part × 12 = inches.

    0.40625 × 12 = 4.875 → 4"

    On the TI-30Xa

    .40625×12=

    Screen 4.875

  6. Decimal part of the inches × 16 = sixteenths.

    0.875 × 16 = 14 → 14/16 = 7/8"

    On the TI-30Xa

    .875×16=

    Screen 14.

Listen: worked examples

Two more examples, a mistake to watch for, and practice questions, read aloud.

Worked example 1. A long length to decimal feet

  1. Fraction first.

    3 ÷ 16 = 0.1875

  2. Add the inches.

    0.1875 + 9 = 9.1875"

  3. Inches to feet: ÷ 12.

    9.1875 ÷ 12 = 0.765625

  4. Add the feet.

    0.765625 + 14 = 14.765625'

    On the TI-30Xa

    3÷16+9=÷12+14=

    Screen 14.765625

Answer: 14.7656'

Worked example 2. A calculator answer back to the tape

  3. Make the fraction smaller.

    4/16 = 1/4

Answer: 8'-4 1/4"

Watch out: Treating inches like a decimal.

There are 12 inches in a foot, not 10. Always divide the inches by 12.
