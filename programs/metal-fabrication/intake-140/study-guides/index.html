Study guides

Ten lessons. Each one has two recordings to listen to, pictures, three worked examples, a mistake to watch for and practice, all with the calculator keys.

  1. 1Tape and fractionsRead the tape to the nearest 1/16". Add and subtract lengths.
  2. 2Decimals of a footChange feet and inches to decimal feet, and back again.
  3. 3CirclesFind the distance around a circle: the circumference.
  4. 4Mean diameter and stretch-outCut plate the right length before you roll it.
  5. 5AreaFind the area of a plate: rectangles and circles.
  6. 6Plate mass by the shop ruleFind how much a steel plate weighs before you lift it.
  7. 7Formed plate and radiused sectionsFind the flat length of a plate before it is bent.
  8. 8Pythagoras and trussesFind the long side of a right triangle.
  9. 9Tank capacity and fit-upsFind how much a tank holds.
  10. 10Parallel line developmentLay out the pattern for a pipe cut at an angle.

How to use them: listen first. Then read the steps. Then do the Try it with your calculator.
