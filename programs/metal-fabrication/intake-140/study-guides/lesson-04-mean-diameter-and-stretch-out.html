Lesson 4. Mean diameter and stretch-out

Cut plate the right length before you roll it.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. Rolling stretches the outside and squeezes the inside.

    The middle of the plate keeps its length.

  2. That middle circle is the mean diameter, MD.

    From the inside: MD = ID + T. From the outside: MD = OD − T.

  3. Add one plate thickness to the inside.

    MD = 245 + 10 = 255 mm

  4. Stretch-out = π × MD.

    SO = π × 255 = 801.1 → 801 mm

    On the TI-30Xa

Listen: worked examples

Two more examples, a mistake to watch for, and practice questions, read aloud.

Worked example 1. From the outside, in millimetres

  1. From the outside: take off one thickness.

    MD = OD − T = 610 − 12 = 598 mm

  2. SO = π × MD.

    π × 598 = 1878.67 mm

    On the TI-30Xa

    610−12=×π=

    Screen 1878.672407

  3. Round to the nearest mm.

    1,879 mm

Answer: 1,879 mm

Worked example 2. From the inside, in inches

  1. From the inside: add one thickness.

    MD = 48 + 1/2 = 48 1/2"

  2. SO = π × MD.

    π × 48.5 = 152.3672"

    On the TI-30Xa

    48.5×π=

    Screen 152.3672437

  3. Make it a tape length.

    0.3672 × 16 = 5.88 → 6/16 = 3/8. 152 3/8" = 12'-8 3/8"

    On the TI-30Xa

Answer: 12'-8 3/8"

Watch out: Cutting the plate to π × OD.

Too long by about π × T. Always find MD first.
