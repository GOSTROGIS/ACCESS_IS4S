Lesson 9. Tank capacity and fit-ups

Find how much a tank holds.

Listen

The voice reads this lesson slowly. Pause any time. Play it again as often as you like.

  1. Box tank: L × W × H, inside sizes.

    8 × 4 × 3.5 = 112 cu ft

    On the TI-30Xa

    8×4×3.5=

    Screen 112.

  2. Course factor: 1 cu ft holds 6.25 gallons.

    112 × 6.25 = 700 gallons

    On the TI-30Xa

    112×6.25=

    Screen 700.

  3. Round tank: π × r × r × L. Inches ÷ 1,728 = cu ft.

    π × 18² × 72 ÷ 1,728 = 42.41 cu ft

    On the TI-30Xa

    18x²×π×72÷1728=

    Screen 42.41150082

  4. Then × 6.25.

    42.41 × 6.25 = 265.07 gallons

  5. Metric: 1 cubic metre holds 1,000 litres.

    1.2 × 0.8 × 0.5 = 0.48 cu m = 480 L

  6. Fit-up changes the plate sizes.

    Ask which: corner to corner, outside flush, or inside flush.

Worked example 1. A round tank in litres

  1. Radius in metres.

    1,200 ÷ 2 = 600 mm = 0.6 m

  2. V = π × r × r × L.

    π × 0.6 × 0.6 × 2 = 2.2619 cu m

    On the TI-30Xa

    .6x²×π×2=

    Screen 2.261946711

  3. × 1,000 for litres.

    2.2619 × 1,000 = 2,262 L

Answer: 2,262 L

Worked example 2. A box tank in inches

  1. Volume in cubic inches.

    60 × 30 × 24 = 43,200 cu in

  2. ÷ 1,728 for cubic feet.

    43,200 ÷ 1,728 = 25 cu ft

    On the TI-30Xa

    60×30×24÷1728=

    Screen 25.

  3. × 6.25 for gallons.

    25 × 6.25 = 156.25 gal

    On the TI-30Xa

    Screen 156.25

Answer: 156.25 gal

Watch out: Using outside sizes.

Capacity is the space inside. Take off the plate thickness.
