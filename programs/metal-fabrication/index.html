Metal Fabrication

The metal fabricator (fitter) trade in BC, the math tools every metal fabrication intake shares, and each intake’s own materials.

About the trade

Metal fabricators lay out, cut, form and fit steel and other metals into parts for buildings, tanks, machines and ships. The work runs on shop math: feet and inches to the nearest sixteenth, circles and stretch-outs for rolled plate, plate weights for safe lifts, tank capacities and pattern development.

What metal fabricators do
Fabricate, assemble, fit and install steel or other metal components for buildings, bridges, tanks, towers, boilers, pressure vessels and similar structures and products.[3]
Certification in BC
Red Seal trade.[1]
Work-based training
4,800 hours of training on the job.[1]
Technical training
20 weeks of classroom training over 3 years: Level 1 is 210 hours, Level 2 is 210 hours and Level 3 is 180 hours.[1][2]
Metal Fabricator Foundation
A 690-hour program for people new to the trade. Finishing it gives credit for Level 1 technical training and 450 hours of work-based training.[2]
Marine Fitter endorsement
An optional 240-hour endorsement.[2]
NOC 2021 code
72104 Structural metal and platework fabricators and fitters, TEER 2. Apprentices are included in this code.[3] Look up any job’s NOC code.
